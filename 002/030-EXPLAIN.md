# Line 030 · あんな日々は もう嫌だよ

## Context

- Line 029 (previous): 震え 竦み 目を逸らし 立ち上がれなくて
- **Line 030 (explained here): あんな日々は もう嫌だよ**
- Line 031 (next): 積み重ねてきた事だけが 前に

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — complete sentence: topic (あんな日々は) + na-adjective predicate 嫌だ + sentence-final よ.

The line is a grammatical, complete sentence, and it could be said in conversation exactly as written. あんな "that kind of, like those" is an adnominal (連体詞) on 日々 "days", and は makes あんな日々 the topic. もう "(not) anymore, enough" and the na-adjective 嫌だ "unpleasant, unwanted" form the set phrase もう嫌だ "I've had enough, I don't want that ever again". The particle よ asserts it, to someone or to oneself. The one who doesn't want those days is the speaker (the わたし of line 020), unstated as usual. あんな gathers up line 029: those days of trembling, freezing up, looking away, being unable to stand. The space falls between the topic and the comment, where prose would have nothing. A stanza break follows, and line 031 opens the final stanza with a new sentence. Restored prose, with line 029: （わたしは）震えて、竦んで、目を逸らして、立ち上がれなくて——あんな日々はもう嫌だよ。

## Reading

- Kana: あんな ひび は / もう いや だ よ
- Romaji: anna hibi wa / mō iya da yo

## How it fits together

`(line 029: 震え 竦み 目を逸らし 立ち上がれなくて →) [[あんな 日々] は] もう [嫌 だ] よ。`

あんな日々は is the topic, and もう嫌だ the comment, with よ on top. Line 029's open te-form does not attach to any word here. It sets out the picture, and あんな points back to it as a whole. The non-past 嫌だ is a present feeling about past days, and so a decision about the future: never again.

## Translation

- Literal: "days like those, (I) find unwanted already"
- Natural: "I never want days like those again."

## Notes

- **あんな: looking back from a distance.** The こ-, そ- and あ- demonstratives divide space and memory. あんな refers to something remote from both speaker and listener, or to something they both remember: あの頃 "those days, back then". あんな日々 places the days of line 029 in the past, at a distance. With こんな日々 "days like these", the speaker would still be inside them. The speaker has already stepped outside the days they reject.
- **もう嫌だ.** もう with a negative or negative-feeling predicate means "no more": もう迷わない "I won't hesitate anymore", もう嫌だ "I can't stand it anymore". On its own, もう嫌だ is a cry of exhaustion ("I've had it!"). With あんな日々は as its topic, it becomes a decision: not "I can't take this" but "I won't go back to that". The final stanza that follows (lines 031–038) turns to what carried the speaker forward.
- **嫌: いや or きらい?** The kanji 嫌 has two everyday readings. 嫌だ (いやだ) is an immediate rejection, "I don't want it, no". 嫌い (きらい) is a lasting dislike, "I dislike it" (ピーマンが嫌い "I don't like green peppers"). Here, with だ and no い, it is いや. いや is also the interjection "no!" (いや、違う "no, that's wrong").
- **日々 and 毎日.** The song opens on 毎日 (line 002, すれ違ってばかりの 毎日 "day after day of never quite connecting") and rejects あんな日々 here. 毎日 is "every day", each day as it comes; 日々 is "days, day-to-day life", slightly more literary. The two words frame the song's account of hard days. It begins inside them (毎日) and ends here, looking back on them from outside (あんな日々).
- **よ.** This is the only sentence-final よ in the song. It asserts the statement, to a listener (perhaps the person of lines 027–028) or to the speaker themselves. The other sentences end on のかな, だろう, んだ, の or a bare 大丈夫; only here does the speaker raise their voice a little.
- **Link to line 031.** After the stanza break, line 031 (積み重ねてきた事だけが 前に) begins the final stanza with a new sentence. The song turns from rejecting the past to finding what carried the speaker through it: only what they had built up, bit by bit.
