# Line 037 · 信じる素直な 気持ちさえあれば

## Context

- Line 036 (previous): 受け止めて 言葉にしてきたんだ
- **Line 037 (explained here): 信じる素直な 気持ちさえあれば**
- Line 038 (next): これからも きっと大丈夫

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: conditional clause (〜さえ〜ば) whose main clause is line 038.

The line is grammatical, but it is not a sentence on its own. It is a conditional clause closed by ば, and its main clause is the next line, これからも きっと大丈夫 (line 038). Line 036 ends its sentence with んだ, so this line starts a new one. The noun 気持ち has two modifiers: the verb 信じる in its attributive form (a one-word relative clause, "the feeling of believing") and the na-adjective 素直な ("honest"). 信じる normally names what is believed with を (or a と-clause). Here nothing is named, which is common with this verb (信じる心 "a believing heart"). さえ stands where the subject particle が would be (気持ちがあれば → 気持ちさえあれば), and the owner of the feeling (わたしに, "in me") is left unstated. The space after 素直な falls between the adjective and its noun, so it is a pause in the phrasing, not a grammatical break. Nothing needs correcting. Restored prose: 信じる素直な気持ちさえあれば、これからもきっと大丈夫（だ）。

## Reading

- Kana: しんじる すなおな / きもち さえ あれば
- Romaji: shinjiru sunao na / kimochi sae areba

## How it fits together

`[信じる [素直な 気持ち]] さえ あれ ば → これからも きっと 大丈夫（だ） (line 038)`

信じる and 素直な are stacked modifiers on the head noun 気持ち. 信じる is a relative clause of the content type: 気持ち is not the one who believes; the clause says what the feeling is. It modifies the noun phrase 素直な気持ち, not 素直な alone, because a verb in 連体形 modifies nouns, not adjectives. Putting the clause before the short adjective is the usual order (昨日買った赤い本 "the red book I bought yesterday"). The adverb 素直に would have attached 素直 to the believing instead (素直に信じる気持ち "the feeling of believing without reservation"); the lyric's 素直な describes the feeling itself, though the two come out close. The noun phrase is the subject of ある, with さえ in place of が, and あれ + ば turns it into the condition that line 038 completes.

## Translation

- Literal: "if even (just) a believing, honest feeling is there"
- Natural: "As long as I have an honest heart that believes, (I'm sure I'll be all right from here on, too)"

## Notes

- **Link to line 036.** Line 036 (受け止めて 言葉にしてきたんだ) closes its sentence with んだ, so this line opens the last sentence of the final stanza (031–038), a stanza built from four two-line sentences. Line 038 completes it: 信じる素直な気持ちさえあれば、これからもきっと大丈夫（だ）. The tie to 035–036 is one of meaning, not grammar: having cried in secret over their own feebleness, then faced it and put it into words, the speaker can be heard naming the one thing needed from now on.
- **What is believed?** 信じる usually names what is trusted with を (自分を信じる "believe in oneself", 未来を信じる "believe in the future") or what is held true with と (夢は叶うと信じる "believe that dreams come true"). Neither appears here. Without an object, 信じる means "to have faith", as in 信じる心 / 信じる気持ち "a believing heart". The song offers candidates, such as the speaker (わたし, line 020), the future (未来, line 034) or what has been built up (積み重ねてきた事, line 031), but the lyric leaves it open.
- **Other parses.** (a) A relative clause can also leave a gap for its object, so 信じる気持ち could in principle mean "the feelings (I) trust", as in 自分の気持ちを信じる "trust one's own feelings". With 気持ち, 心 or 力, though, a bare verb normally states the content (諦めない気持ち "the will not to give up", 頑張る気持ち "the will to keep trying"), so "the feeling of believing" is the main reading; the meaning shifts only slightly either way. (b) 信じる has the same terminal and attributive form, so in theory the line could be split 信じる。素直な気持ちさえあれば… "(I) believe. As long as I have honest feelings…". The lyric writes 信じる素直な with no space, though (compare line 011, where 揺れる 焦る stand apart), so the attributive reading is the natural one.
- **さえ〜ば.** On its own, さえ means "even" (名前さえ知らない "don't even know the name"). Followed by a ば-conditional, it marks the minimum that is enough: これさえあれば "as long as I have this", 君さえいれば "as long as you're here" (いる for people). With a regretful main clause, the same pattern means "if only": あの時勇気さえあれば… "if only I'd had the courage then…". Here the main clause is 大丈夫 (line 038), so the sense is "as long as". This is the song's only ば-conditional. Compare the concessive ても…ても of line 033 ("even if…, even if…"): both of the stanza's sentences about the future start from a condition, but one reassurance holds whatever happens (033–034), while the other rests on a single thing (037–038).
- **素直 and 気持ち.** 素直 (素 "plain, bare" + 直 "straight") describes a child as obedient, and words or feelings as frank and unaffected: 素直な感想 "honest impressions", 素直になる "be honest with oneself, stop being stubborn". 素直な気持ち "how one really feels" is a stock phrase, often with 伝える (素直な気持ちを伝える "tell someone how you really feel"), which may be heard against the song's theme of putting feelings into words (lines 004, 036). 気持ち occurs only twice in the song: on line 003, near the start, as 分かって貰えない気持ち "feelings nobody understands", and here, near the end, as a feeling that is honest and believing.
- **信じる.** Today an ichidan verb (信じない, 信じて, 信じれば), like 感じる and 通じる; the older サ変 form 信ずる survives in formal writing. The kanji 信 combines 亻 "person" and 言 "word".
