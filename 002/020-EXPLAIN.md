# Line 020 · それが今の わたしなんだ

## Context

- Line 019 (previous): 取るに足りない 小さな一歩だけど
- **Line 020 (explained here): それが今の わたしなんだ**
- Line 021 (next): 小さな夢積み上げて

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — completes the sentence begun on line 019: identification sentence (Xが Yなんだ); the sentence ends here.

The line is grammatical and complete, and it is the main clause that line 019's けど was waiting for. それ "that" points back to the 小さな一歩 "small step" of line 019. が marks それ as the subject, with focus: "that, precisely". The predicate is the noun phrase 今のわたし "the me of now, who I am now" plus なんだ: な is the form the copula だ takes before ん, ん is the explanatory の, and the final だ asserts it. The whole sentence equates the small step with the speaker's present self. わたし is the song's only first-person pronoun, and the other explanations refer to the speaker by it. The space after 今の falls between a modifier and its noun, so it is a pause in the phrasing, not a grammatical break. A stanza break follows, and line 021 begins the song's second half. Restored prose, with line 019: 取るに足りない小さな一歩だけど、それが今のわたしなんだ。

## Reading

- Kana: それ が いま の / わたし なん だ
- Romaji: sore ga ima no / watashi nan da

## How it fits together

`(line 019: 取るに足りない小さな一歩だけど、) [それ が] [[今 の] わたし] な ん だ。`

The sentence is an equation, X が Y だ: X is それ, the small step, and Y is 今のわたし. Line 019's concession (small, trifling) and this line's assertion (that is me) are joined by けど. The meaning is not "despite being small, the step is me" in any strict logic; it is acceptance: small as it is, this is where I stand now.

## Translation

- Literal: "that is the me of now"
- Natural: "(It's only a tiny step, nothing to speak of, but) that's who I am right now."

## Notes

- **が, not は.** それは今のわたしなんだ would be a comment about それ: "as for that, it's the present me". それが puts the focus on それ, as if answering the question "what is the present me?": "that is". Compare これが私の答えだ "this is my answer", a sentence that presents something as the answer. The same focusing が appears on line 031, 積み重ねてきた事だけが "only what I had built up", where だけ makes the focus explicit.
- **今のわたし.** Japanese can modify a pronoun with の the way it modifies any noun: 昔のわたし "the me of the past", 本当のわたし "the real me". English needs a paraphrase, "who I am now". The phrase implies that the speaker changes over time. The song looks at the self at other times too: the days rejected on line 030 (あんな日々は もう嫌だよ) and the future on line 038 (これからも きっと大丈夫).
- **なんだ after なんだろう.** Line 018 ends on 物なんだろう, a guess ("I suppose it was something that was there all along"). This line ends on わたしなんだ, the same explanatory の without the guess: a conclusion stated outright. The ん-ending returns in the final stanza, on lines 032 (進む力をくれたんだ) and 036 (言葉にしてきたんだ), each time for a conclusion the speaker draws from what they have lived.
- **わたし in kana.** The pronoun can also be written 私. Kana わたし looks softer and is common in lyrics. It is the polite or neutral "I", and on its own it does not identify the speaker. This is the song's only first-person pronoun, and the other explanations of the song refer to the speaker as "the わたし of line 020".
- **それ.** The mid-distance demonstrative それ refers to something just said (文脈指示), here the step of line 019. It can also be heard as summing up everything since line 013, the trying, falling and getting up: all of that, small as it is, is the present self.
- **Link to line 021.** なんだ ends the sentence, the stanza of lines 013–020 and the first half of the song. After the break, line 021 (小さな夢積み上げて "piling up small dreams") repeats 小さな from line 019, and the second half opens with a new chain of te-forms.
