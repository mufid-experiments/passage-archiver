# Line 035 · 不甲斐なさに 隠れて泣いて だけど

## Context

- Line 034 (previous): 未来は閉ざされたりしない
- **Line 035 (explained here): 不甲斐なさに 隠れて泣いて だけど**
- Line 036 (next): 受け止めて 言葉にしてきたんだ

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: te-form clause (隠れて泣いて) plus the conjunction だけど, which opens the clause on line 036.

The line is grammatical but has no main predicate. 不甲斐なさ "feebleness, one's own disappointing weakness" is a noun made from the adjective 不甲斐ない, and に marks it as the cause of what follows: 〜に泣く "cry over ~". 隠れて "hiding, in secret" is a te-form of manner that modifies 泣いて "cried", and 泣いて, the te-form of 泣く, leaves the clause open. The line then ends on だけど "but", a conjunction (接続詞) that belongs to the next line: だけど受け止めて 言葉にしてきたんだ "but (I) faced it and have put it into words". The subject is the speaker (the わたし of line 020), unstated as usual, and the te-forms take their past from きた on line 036. In prose the clause would usually end before だけど (…泣いた。だけど…), but speech and lyrics often keep the te-form running into the conjunction. The spaces separate cause, action and conjunction. Line 034 ends its sentence with ない, so this line starts a new one. Restored prose, with line 036: （わたしは）不甲斐なさに隠れて泣いて、だけど（それを）受け止めて、言葉にしてきたんだ。

## Reading

- Kana: ふがいなさ に / かくれて ないて / だけど
- Romaji: fugainasa ni / kakurete naite / dakedo

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 不甲斐なさ | ふがいなさ (fugainasa) | noun, from i-adjective 不甲斐ない + nominal suffix さ | feebleness, spinelessness; one's own disappointing weakness |
| 2 | に | に (ni) | case particle (格助詞), cause | "at, over": 〜に泣く "cry over ~" (悔しさに泣く "cry with frustration") |
| 3 | 隠れ | かくれ (kakure) | ichidan verb 隠れる (下一段), continuative form (連用形) before て | to hide (oneself); 隠れて "in hiding, secretly" |
| 4 | て | て (te) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞), te-form | manner: "in secret"; joins 隠れ to 泣いて |
| 5 | 泣い | ない (nai) | godan verb 泣く, continuative form with euphonic change (イ音便: 泣き → 泣い) before て | to cry, to weep |
| 6 | て | て (te) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞), te-form | "…and"; left open, followed by だけど |
| 7 | だけど | だけど (dakedo) | conjunction (接続詞), from だ + けど | "but, however"; opens the clause on line 036 |

## How it fits together

`(わたしは) [不甲斐なさ に] [[隠れ て] 泣い て] ／ だけど → line 036 [(それを) 受け止め て] [言葉 に し て き た] ん だ`

不甲斐なさに gives the cause of the crying, and 隠れて the manner. Both depend on 泣いて, and the space after 不甲斐なさに shows that 隠れて泣いて is a unit. だけど sets the two halves of the sentence against each other: crying in secret on one side, facing it and putting it into words on the other. The tense of the whole is set on line 036 by きた: all of this happened in the time leading up to now.

## Translation

- Literal: "(I) hid and cried at (my) feebleness, but…"
- Natural: "I cried in secret over how weak I was, but…" (with line 036: "I cried in secret over how weak I was, but I faced it, and I've been putting it into words.")

## Notes

- **Two readings of 不甲斐なさに.** The main reading, above, takes に as the cause of the crying: 悔しさに泣く "cry with frustration", 寂しさに泣く "cry from loneliness". に also marks where one hides (木の陰に隠れる "hide behind a tree"), so the phrase could in principle go with 隠れて: "hiding in (behind) my weakness and crying", as if the weakness were a shelter or an excuse. The spacing groups 隠れて泣いて together and leaves 不甲斐なさに apart, which favours the first reading.
- **だけど at the end of a line.** Here だけど is a conjunction, "but", that begins the next clause. It is the last of three line-final pointers in the stanza, after 前に (line 031) and 決して (line 033). Line 019 has the same letters in a different role: in 小さな一歩だけど, けど is a conjunctive particle attached to the copula after a noun, inside its own clause. Starting a clause with だけど is casual; written equivalents are しかし and けれども, and でも is also casual.
- **不甲斐ない.** ふがいない is "pathetic, spineless, disappointing", said of oneself or of people one is tied to: 不甲斐ない自分 "my pathetic self", 不甲斐ない試合 "a dismal performance (by one's own team)". The 甲斐 in it means "worth, effect", as in 生き甲斐 "something to live for" and やり甲斐 "worth doing". The suffix さ makes a noun of degree, like もどかしさ on line 015. Both name the speaker's feelings about their own failure.
- **隠れて泣く.** "Cry where no one can see": a te-form of manner, like 急いで行く "go in a hurry". It recalls the hidden feelings of the opening (分かって貰えない気持ち, line 003) and the burden of lines 027–028: now the speaker cries alone instead of leaning on someone.
- **The turn in the middle.** This line and the next are the stanza's one two-line sentence with a "but" at its centre. The crying in secret belongs to the low point the song has already shown (lines 029–030). だけど turns from it to what the speaker did next: 受け止めて "faced it" (line 036). That reverses 目を逸らし "looked away" on line 029.
- **Link to line 034.** Line 034 (未来は閉ざされたりしない) ends a sentence that looks ahead. This line goes back to the past, and line 036 returns with the stanza's second んだ, looking back on what the speaker has done.
