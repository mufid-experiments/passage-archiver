# Line 005 · 春風は青く香り やがて緑は芽吹いて

## Context

- Line 004 (previous): 言葉にして 伝えられるのかな
- **Line 005 (explained here): 春風は青く香り やがて緑は芽吹いて**
- Line 006 (next): 祝福の光の中 また桜は散ってゆく

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — poetic: two linked clauses (continuative 香り, te-form 芽吹いて) with a synaesthetic 青く香る; the sentence continues to line 006.

The line is grammatical, but it does not end a sentence. It holds two clauses, each with its own topic. In the first, 春風は "the spring breeze" is the topic, and 香り, the continuative form (連用形) of the verb 香る "be fragrant", ends the clause and links it to the next (連用中止法, a written, slightly literary way of saying "…and"). In the second, やがて "before long" introduces 緑は "the green", and 芽吹いて, the te-form of 芽吹く "bud, sprout", leaves it open. The sentence continues to line 006, where a third clause with a third topic (桜は) ends it on the terminal 散ってゆく. The unusual part is 青く香る: a colour word describing a scent. That is a poetic choice rather than an error (see Notes). The space marks the break between the two clauses, where prose would put a comma. Line 004 closes its own sentence with のかな, so this line, after the stanza break, starts the refrain with a new sentence. Restored prose: 春風は青く香り、やがて緑は芽吹いて、祝福の光の中、また桜は散ってゆく。

## Reading

- Kana: はるかぜ は あおく かおり / やがて みどり は めぶいて
- Romaji: harukaze wa aoku kaori / yagate midori wa mebuite

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 春風 | はるかぜ (harukaze) | noun, compound of 春 "spring" + 風 "wind", with rendaku か → が | spring breeze. The Sino-Japanese reading しゅんぷう also exists, but in a song with 緑, 芽吹く and 桜, はるかぜ is the likely one |
| 2 | は | は (wa) | binding particle (係助詞), topic | marks 春風 as the topic of 香り; the first of three parallel topics (春風は, 緑は, 桜は on line 006) |
| 3 | 青く | あおく (aoku) | i-adjective (形容詞) 青い, continuative form (連用形) used adverbially | "blue / green; fresh, young": modifies 香り, "smell fresh and green" |
| 4 | 香り | かおり (kaori) | godan verb 香る, continuative form (連用形) used to link clauses (連用中止法) | to be fragrant, to smell sweet; "…is fragrant, and…". Same shape as the noun 香り "scent", but here a verb, with 春風は as its subject and 青く as its adverb |
| 5 | やがて | やがて (yagate) | adverb (副詞) | "before long, in time": marks the next stage of spring; modifies 芽吹いて. Again on line 016 |
| 6 | 緑 | みどり (midori) | noun | green; here the new green of leaves and grass (新緑) |
| 7 | は | は (wa) | binding particle (係助詞), topic | the second parallel topic |
| 8 | 芽吹い | めぶい (mebui) | godan verb 芽吹く, continuative form with euphonic change (イ音便: 芽吹き → 芽吹い) before て | to bud, to sprout: 芽 "bud" + 吹く, here "put forth" |
| 9 | て | て (te) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞), te-form | "…and": links on to line 006; the sentence stays open |

## How it fits together

`[春風 は [青く 香り]] ／ やがて [緑 は 芽吹い て] → line 006 [[祝福 の 光] の 中] また [桜 は 散っ て ゆく]`

The sentence of lines 005–006 has three clauses, each with its own は-topic, in the order of a passing spring: breeze, buds, falling blossoms. 香り (連用中止) and 芽吹いて (te-form) both mean "…and"; neither has a tense of its own, and both take the non-past of ゆく on line 006. やがて belongs to the second clause and marks time moving on from the first.

## Translation

- Literal: "the spring breeze is fragrant greenly, and before long the green buds, and…"
- Natural: "The spring breeze carries a fresh green scent, and soon new green buds appear…"

## Notes

- **青く香る.** Japanese 青い covers much of what English splits into blue and green (青信号 "green light", 青葉 "green leaves", 青りんご "green apple"). It also means "fresh, young, unripe" (青春 "youth", 青二才 "greenhorn"). So 青く香る suggests a scent of new greenery and of youth at once. The pattern is ordinary, an adverbial 〜く before a verb of smelling (甘く香る "smell sweet"), but a colour word in that slot is a poet's choice: a scent described as if it could be seen. Haiku has the related season word 風薫る (かぜかおる), the fresh breeze of early summer, written with 薫, another kanji for かおる.
- **Another parse.** An adjective's continuative form can also end a clause on its own (空は青く、海は広い "the sky is blue and the sea is wide"). Read that way, 春風は青く、香り would mean "the spring breeze is green and fragrant". The lyric writes 青く香り without a space, which favours the first reading, and the meaning hardly changes.
- **香り: verb, not noun.** 香り is the 連用形 of 香る and also the noun "fragrance". The noun would need a different frame (春風は青い香り（だ） "the spring breeze is a green scent", or 春風の香り "the scent of the spring breeze"). Here 香り has a subject (春風は) and an adverb (青く), so it is the verb. The 連用中止法 sounds more written than the te-form 香って, and the line has both kinds of link in a row: 香り, then 芽吹いて.
- **Three topics with は.** 春風は, 緑は and 桜は (line 006) give each element of the scene its own clause and set them side by side, like frames of a picture: the breeze…, the green…, the cherry blossoms…. With が (春風が香り、緑が芽吹いて…) the lines would read more like a report of events. The parallel は also fits a cycle that comes round every year, which line 006's また "again" spells out.
- **やがて.** Modern やがて means "before long, in due course", time passing naturally (classical Japanese also used it for "immediately"). The song uses it once more, on line 016 (やがて景色は流れてゆく), again for things outside the speaker that move on by themselves.
- **Link to line 004.** Line 004 (言葉にして 伝えられるのかな) ends the first stanza on a question the speaker asks themselves. Nothing carries over in grammar. The refrain answers the doubt with a picture of spring instead of an argument. Lines 005–006 return word for word as lines 039–040, after the song has answered the doubt on line 036 (言葉にしてきたんだ).
- **Link to line 006.** 芽吹いて leaves the sentence open, and line 006 closes it: 祝福の光の中 また桜は散ってゆく. In a Japanese spring the new leaves and the falling cherry blossoms overlap; the refrain puts the falling blossoms last, so the scene ends on loss inside celebration.
