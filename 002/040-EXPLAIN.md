## Line 039 · 春風は青く香り やがて緑は芽吹いて

### Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — poetic: two linked clauses (continuative 香り, te-form 芽吹いて) with a synaesthetic 青く香る; the sentence continues to line 040.

The line is grammatical but does not end a sentence. It repeats line 005 word for word. Its first clause has the topic 春風は "the spring breeze" and ends on 香り, the continuative form (連用形) of 香る "be fragrant", used to link clauses (連用中止法, a written, slightly literary "…and"). Its second clause has the topic 緑は "the green", introduced by やがて "before long", and ends on 芽吹いて, the te-form of 芽吹く "bud, sprout". Both links stay open for line 040. 青く香る, a colour word describing a scent, is a poetic choice, not an error. Line 038 (これからも きっと大丈夫) ends the final stanza and the song's last new sentence, so this line, after the stanza break, starts a new sentence with its own topic.

Two clauses with their own topics, in the order of a passing spring. 香り and 芽吹いて are both open links with no tense, and they take the non-past of ゆく on line 040.

### Translation

- Literal: "the spring breeze is fragrant greenly, and before long the green buds, and…"
- Natural: "The spring breeze carries a fresh green scent, and soon new green buds appear…"

## Line 040 · 祝福の光の中 また桜は散ってゆく

### Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — completes the sentence begun on line 039: adverbial 〜の中 (no particle) + main clause ending on the terminal 散ってゆく; the song ends here.

The line is grammatical and ends both the sentence and the song. It repeats line 006 word for word. 祝福の光の中 "amid the light of blessing" is a noun phrase used as an adverbial of setting. Prose could add で, but 〜の中、 with no particle is standard written style for "amid, with ~ all around". The main clause has また "again", the third topic 桜は, and 散ってゆく: the te-form of 散る "scatter, fall (of petals)" plus the subsidiary verb ゆく "go on ~ing, ~ away", the literary form of いく. ゆく is terminal and closes the sentence of lines 039–040. Its non-past covers all three clauses: this is what spring does, and will do again. The space separates the setting from the clause, where prose would put a comma. Nothing follows: the song ends on the refrain, with the cherry blossoms still falling. Restored prose, lines 039–040: 春風は青く香り、やがて緑は芽吹いて、祝福の光の中、また桜は散ってゆく。

### How it fits together

`(line 039: 春風は青く香り やがて緑は芽吹いて →) [[[祝福 の 光] の] 中] ／ また [桜 は [散っ て ゆく]]。`

The 中-phrase sets the scene, most naturally for the last clause (the blossoms falling in the light) but also for the whole picture. また modifies 散ってゆく, and 桜は is the last of the three topics. 散ってゆく is the only verb in the sentence with a finite form, so the sentence, and the song, end on it.

### Translation

- Literal: "amid the light of blessing, again the cherry blossoms go on scattering"
- Natural: "and in the light of blessing, the cherry blossoms fall once again." (with line 039: "The spring breeze carries a fresh green scent, soon new green buds appear, and in the light of blessing, the cherry blossoms fall once again.")

## Notes

- **How the two lines connect.** They are one sentence of three clauses: 春風は青く香り、やがて緑は芽吹いて、祝福の光の中、また桜は散ってゆく. Line 039's links (香り, 芽吹いて) are open, and line 040 closes them. Neither line has anything left out that must be filled in: unlike most of the song, there is no unstated speaker here, because the refrain is about the season, not about わたし.
- **Ending on the refrain.** The song's last new words are on line 038 (これからも きっと大丈夫). The ending repeats lines 005–006 instead of adding a final statement, so the song closes on an image rather than a conclusion. The first time, the refrain followed a doubt (line 004, 伝えられるのかな) and led into failures (line 007). Now it follows reassurance, and nothing comes after it. The words are the same, but they are heard differently.
- **The last word is ゆく.** 〜てゆく is "go on ~ing", a change continuing from now into the future. Ending the song on it leaves the motion running past the last line: the petals are still falling, and time is still moving. The ゆく of the refrain is the literary form the song uses for the world around the speaker (散ってゆく, 流れてゆく on line 016); the speaker's own state got the casual く (増えてく, line 007).
- **また and the cycle.** また "again" says that this spring is one of many. The refrain itself comes round again here, so the word repeats along with the scene. After これからも "from now on, too" on line 038, the cycle can be heard as the future the speaker is sure of: more springs, with new green and falling blossoms.
- **〜の中 without a particle.** For the place of an action, prose has 〜の中で, and for a path 〜の中を (雨の中を歩く "walk in the rain"). But 〜の中、 alone is a standard written pattern for the circumstances around an event (大勢が見守る中、式が始まった "the ceremony began as many looked on"). The lyric's 祝福の光の中 is ordinary written style, not a poetic omission.
- **Blessing and falling blossoms.** In Japan the cherry blossom season is also the season of graduations and entrance ceremonies, of partings and new starts. Falling blossoms are the classic image of beauty that does not last. 祝福の光 "the light of blessing" is not a fixed phrase. It paints the spring sunshine as if it were celebrating someone. The song ends with the two together: something ends, and something is blessed.
