# Line 013 · 思い切って挑んで 壁に当たって

## Context

- Line 012 (previous): いつも 今も 届かなくて
- **Line 013 (explained here): 思い切って挑んで 壁に当たって**
- Line 014 (next): 倒れ起きて 繰り返して

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: chain of te-forms (挑んで, 当たって) that opens the sentence ending on line 015 (募る).

The line is grammatical but has no main predicate. It holds two te-forms with the same unstated subject, the speaker (the わたし of line 020). The first is 挑んで "take on (a challenge)", modified by the adverb 思い切って "boldly, taking the plunge". The second is 当たって "hit", with 壁に "against a wall", the everyday image for running into an obstacle. Neither has tense, and the chain goes on through line 014 (倒れ起きて 繰り返して) to line 015, where the last link, 前に進めずに, is followed by the main predicate もどかしさ募る "frustration builds". What the speaker takes on, which 挑む would mark with に, is unstated. The space separates the two actions, where prose would put a comma. Line 012 ends the previous stanza on an open te-form (届かなくて), and after the stanza break this line opens the long stanza of lines 013–020 with a new chain. Restored prose, lines 013–015: （わたしは）思い切って挑んで、壁に当たって、倒れて起きて、（それを）繰り返して、前に進めずに、もどかしさ（が）募る。

## Reading

- Kana: おもいきって いどんで / かべ に あたって
- Romaji: omoikitte idonde / kabe ni atatte

| 7 | て | て (te) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞), te-form | "…and": the chain continues on line 014 |

## How it fits together

- This line: `(わたしは) [思い切って 挑ん で] ／ [壁 に 当たっ て] → line 014`
- Whole sentence: `(わたしは) [思い切って 挑ん で]、[壁 に 当たっ て]、[倒れ 起き て]、[(それを) 繰り返し て]、[前 に 進め ず に]、[もどかしさ (が) 募る]`

The chain lists events in the order they happen: try, hit a wall, fall, get up, repeat, still not move forward. Each te-form adds one step, and none of them is the main verb. The main verb, 募る on line 015, has a different subject, もどかしさ "frustration": a te-chain about the speaker's actions ends on what those actions leave behind.

## Translation

- Literal: "boldly challenging, hitting a wall, and…"
- Natural: "I take the plunge and try, I run into a wall…" (whole sentence, lines 013–015: "I take the plunge and try, I run into a wall, I fall and get back up, over and over, and still I can't move forward: the frustration keeps building.")

## Notes

- **Link to line 012.** Line 012 (いつも 今も 届かなくて) closes the short stanza of lines 011–012 on an open te-form. This line starts a new stanza and a new chain. Because 届かなくて is left open, a listener can also hear the two chains run together up to 募る on line 015. Either way the story moves on: the smouldering heat of line 011 finally turns into action, 思い切って挑んで.
- **思い切って and 思い切り.** 思い切って is a fixed adverb, "decisively, taking the plunge" (思い切って告白する "pluck up the courage to confess"). It comes from 思い切る, which as a verb mostly means the opposite of trying, "give up on, resign oneself to" (夢を思い切る "abandon a dream"). Both senses go back to cutting off one's thoughts: in the adverb, one's hesitation. Do not confuse it with 思い切り "with all one's might, to the full" (思い切り泣く "cry one's heart out").
- **挑む.** 〜に挑む "take on, challenge": 難関に挑む "take on a tough hurdle", 世界記録に挑む "go for the world record". It is more written and more forceful than 挑戦する. Godan verbs in む, ぶ and ぬ form their te-form with ん + で (読む → 読んで, 飛ぶ → 飛んで, 死ぬ → 死んで). This line therefore has both voicings of the te-form, 挑んで and 当たって.
- **壁に当たる.** The more frequent idioms are 壁にぶつかる "hit a wall" and 壁に突き当たる "run up against a wall". 壁に当たる is the same image with a plainer verb. 当たる covers any contact that hits its mark: a ball (ボールが当たる), a guess (予想が当たる "the prediction comes true"), a prize (くじが当たる "win a lottery"). The wall continues the song's path imagery: unable to move forward (前に進めずに, 015), stumbling (躓いても, 033).
- **The chain of lines 013–015.** Five links come before the main verb 募る: 挑んで, 当たって, 倒れ起きて, 繰り返して and 前に進めずに. The 繰り返して of line 014 turns the first three into a cycle that repeats. The long run of te-forms, each a step that never arrives anywhere, suits the meaning.
