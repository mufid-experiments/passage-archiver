# Line 004 · 言葉にして 伝えられるのかな

## Context

- Line 003 (previous): 分かって貰えない気持ち
- **Line 004 (explained here): 言葉にして 伝えられるのかな**
- Line 005 (next): 春風は青く香り やがて緑は芽吹いて

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — completes the sentence begun on line 003: predicate (potential 伝えられる) with the self-directed question のかな.

The line is grammatical and ends a sentence. Its object is on line 003: 分かって貰えない気持ち, with を dropped, is what the speaker would put into words (言葉にして) and convey (伝えられる). 言葉にして is the te-form of 言葉にする "put into words", and it says how the conveying would be done: "by putting (them) into words". 伝えられる is the potential of 伝える, "can convey". After it, の (the explanatory の of のだ), か (question) and な (musing) together make のかな, "I wonder if…", a question the speaker asks themselves rather than anyone else. The subject (the speaker, わたし of line 020) and the person the feelings should reach (相手に "to them") are unstated, as usual. The space separates the means from the verb, where prose would have nothing or a comma. The register is plain and casual, and the potential keeps its ら (伝えられる, not the colloquial 伝えれる). A stanza break follows, and line 005 starts the refrain with a new sentence. Restored prose: （わたしは）分かって貰えない気持ち（を）、言葉にして（相手に）伝えられるのかな。

## Reading

- Kana: ことば に して / つたえられる の かな
- Romaji: kotoba ni shite / tsutaerareru no ka na

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 言葉 | ことば (kotoba) | noun | words, language |
| 2 | に | に (ni) | case particle (格助詞), result of change | "into": 言葉にする "make (it) into words" |

## How it fits together

`(line 003: 分かって貰えない気持ち (を) →) (わたしは) [言葉 に し て] ／ [(相手に) 伝え られる] の か な`

言葉にして and 伝えられる share their object, 気持ち on line 003. The te-form is not a separate event here: putting the feelings into words is how they would be conveyed. 伝えられる is potential, so the doubt is about ability, "can I?", not about will. の turns the clause into a situation, and かな asks about it without expecting an answer.

## Translation

- Literal: "I wonder if (it is that I) can put (them) into words and convey (them)"
- Natural: "Can I ever put them into words and get them across?" (with line 003: "These feelings no one understands: I wonder if I can put them into words and get them across.")

## Notes

- **The whole sentence, 003–004.** 分かって貰えない気持ち（を）、言葉にして伝えられるのかな。 It closes the first stanza on a question with no answer. The song answers it near the end, on line 036, which takes up the same words: 受け止めて 言葉にしてきたんだ "(but) I faced it and have been putting it into words".
- **かな and のかな.** 伝えられるかな "I wonder if I'll be able to convey them" looks ahead to a single outcome. 伝えられるのかな adds the explanatory の, and with it a doubt about the whole premise: "is it even possible to…?" Both are casual and self-directed; to ask someone else, the speaker would use 伝えられる？ or 伝えられるかな？ with rising intonation.
- **伝えられる: potential, not passive.** られる is also the passive and honorific auxiliary, so 伝えられる could in form mean "be conveyed". Here the agent of 言葉にして is the speaker, and the feelings are its object, so only the potential fits: "can (I) convey". Casual speech often drops the ら (伝えれる, ら抜き言葉), but the lyric keeps the standard form. The song's other potentials belong to godan verbs, where the question of ら does not arise: 貰えない (003), 追い付けない (009), 進めずに (015), 立ち上がれなくて (029).
- **言葉にする.** Xにする means "make into X": に marks the result of a change (形にする "give shape to", 夢を形にする "make a dream real"). 言葉にする "put into words" is a stock phrase for feelings that are hard to express (言葉にできない "beyond words"). 伝える and its intransitive 伝わる form a pair: 気持ちを伝える "convey one's feelings" (the speaker acts) and 気持ちが伝わる "one's feelings get through" (the result). The line asks about the speaker's own ability, not about whether the feelings will arrive.
- **Link to line 003.** Line 003 ends on the bare noun 気持ち; only this line shows that it is an object. In prose the を would normally be said: 分かって貰えない気持ちを言葉にして伝えられるのかな.
- **Link to line 005: the refrain.** のかな is sentence-final, and after the stanza break line 005 (春風は青く香り やがて緑は芽吹いて) starts a new sentence with its own topic, 春風は. Nothing carries over in grammar. The refrain moves from the speaker's doubt to a spring scene of breeze, buds and falling cherry blossoms. Line 038 notes how different the same refrain sounds when it returns after the reassurance of lines 037–038.
