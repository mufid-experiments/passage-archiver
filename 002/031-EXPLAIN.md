# Line 031 · 積み重ねてきた事だけが 前に

## Context

- Line 030 (previous): あんな日々は もう嫌だよ
- **Line 031 (explained here): 積み重ねてきた事だけが 前に**
- Line 032 (next): 進む力をくれたんだ

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: subject (〜事だけが) plus 前に, which goes with 進む on line 032; the predicate (くれたんだ) comes on line 032.

The line is grammatical but has no predicate. 積み重ねてきた "that (I) have built up, layer on layer" is a relative clause on the formal noun 事 "things". The te-form of 積み重ねる "pile up, accumulate" plus the subsidiary verb くる, in the past, means "have kept ~ing up to now". だけ "only" limits the noun phrase, and が marks it as the subject of くれた "gave (me)" on line 032. The line then ends on 前に "forward", which belongs grammatically to the next line: 前に進む力 "the strength to move forward". So the line break falls inside a relative clause, between 前に and its verb 進む. The one who did the building up is the speaker (the わたし of line 020), unstated as usual. Line 030 ends its sentence with よ, so this line, after the stanza break, starts the final stanza with a new sentence. Restored prose: 積み重ねてきた事だけが、前に進む力をくれたんだ。

## Reading

- Kana: つみかさねて きた こと だけ が / まえ に
- Romaji: tsumikasanete kita koto dake ga / mae ni

## How it fits together

`[[(わたしが) 積み重ね て き た] 事] だけ が ／ [[前 に → line 032 進む] 力] を (わたしに) くれ た ん だ`

The subject is a noun phrase: a relative clause on 事, limited by だけ and marked by が. 前に is the first word of a second relative clause, 前に進む, which modifies 力 on line 032. The line therefore ends in the middle of that clause. The main verb くれた, on line 032, takes the subject from this line and the object from the next.

## Translation

- Literal: "only the things (I) have piled up (gave me the strength to move) forward"
- Natural: "It was only what I'd built up, bit by bit, (that gave me the strength to keep moving) forward."

## Notes

- **前に across the line break.** 前に belongs with 進む: 前に進む力 "the strength to move forward". This is the first of three line-final pointers in the stanza, along with 決して (line 033), whose negative comes on line 034, and だけど (line 035), which opens the clause on line 036. 前に is not "before" here. That sense needs a verb or a の-phrase in front of it (出かける前に "before going out", 食事の前に "before the meal"), and here 前 follows the subject particle が.
- **The answer to line 015.** In the first half, the speaker was 前に進めずに "unable to move forward" (line 015), however hard they tried. Here the same phrase returns as something they have been given the strength to do. The song's path imagery, 前に進む, 一歩, 立ち止まる, 躓く, is resolved in this sentence.
- **〜てきた.** 〜てくる looks back along a line from the past to now: 積み重ねてきた "have built up over time", 頑張ってきた "have kept trying". Its mirror image is 〜ていく, from now into the future (増えてく on line 007, 散ってゆく on line 006). The final stanza uses 〜てきた twice, here and on line 036 (言葉にしてきたんだ), both times for effort that went on through the hard days.
- **だけが and ばかりが.** Line 007 (出来ない事ばかりが 増えてく) has the same shape, 事 + "only" + が, with a different "only". ばかり complains that only the unwanted things increase; だけ picks out the one thing that counted. In the first verse, the things that accumulated were failures. Here, what accumulated turns out to be the source of strength.
- **積み重ねる after 積み上げる.** The song uses 積み上げる on lines 008 (努力を積み上げる暇もなく) and 021 (小さな夢積み上げて), and 積み重ねる only here. 積み上げる looks at the height of the pile; 積み重ねる looks at layers added one by one over time (経験を積み重ねる "build up experience", 努力の積み重ね "an accumulation of effort"). What carried the speaker was not a tall pile but many small layers, which fits the 小さな一歩 and 小さな夢 of lines 019 and 021.
- **What was built up?** 事 leaves it unnamed: the small steps, the efforts, perhaps even the failures and the getting up again (lines 013–014). The formal noun 事 is written in kanji, as on lines 007 and 025. Style guides prefer kana こと, so the kanji is a stylistic choice.
