# Line 038 · これからも きっと大丈夫

## Context

- Line 037 (previous): 信じる素直な 気持ちさえあれば
- **Line 038 (explained here): これからも きっと大丈夫**
- Line 039 (next): 春風は青く香り やがて緑は芽吹いて

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — completes the sentence begun on line 037: main clause, na-adjective 大丈夫 with the copula だ omitted.

The line is grammatical. It is the main clause that line 037's conditional (〜さえあれば) was waiting for, so the two lines make one sentence. The predicate is the na-adjective 大丈夫 with the copula dropped, 大丈夫（だ）, which is ordinary in casual speech and in lyrics. The subject is left out too, as usual: most naturally the speaker ("I'll be all right"), or things in general ("it'll be all right"). これからも ("from now on, too") and きっと ("surely") both modify 大丈夫. Nothing is inverted or poetic in the grammar, and the line could be said just like this in conversation. The space marks a breath after the time phrase. A stanza break follows, and line 039 starts a new sentence, so nothing carries over. Restored prose: 信じる素直な気持ちさえあれば、（わたしは）これからもきっと大丈夫（だ）。

## How it fits together

`(line 037: …気持ち さえ あれ ば →) [これから も] きっと 大丈夫（だ）`

Line 037 supplies the condition and this line the consequence: given that one thing, all will be well. これからも (when) and きっと (how sure the speaker is) both modify the predicate 大丈夫, whose だ is omitted. The subject is unstated: the speaker (わたし, line 020) or the situation in general. The sentence, and the stanza, end here.

## Translation

- Literal: "from now on too, surely all right"
- Natural: "(As long as I have an honest heart that believes,) I'm sure I'll be all right from here on, too."

## Notes

- **Completes line 037.** The whole sentence is 信じる素直な気持ちさえあれば、これからもきっと大丈夫（だ）. It has the familiar shape of これさえあれば大丈夫 "as long as I have this, I'll be fine": one condition, then a reassurance. It is also the song's last new sentence. Lines 039–040 only repeat lines 005–006, so 大丈夫 is the last word of new text.
- **The shape of the final stanza.** Its four two-line sentences alternate between what has been and what lies ahead. Lines 032 (進む力をくれたんだ) and 036 (言葉にしてきたんだ) look back, in the past tense with んだ; lines 034 (未来は閉ざされたりしない) and 038 look forward, in the non-past. これから takes up the 未来 of line 034, and the stanza ends on a forecast rather than a report.
- **これからも.** も adds the time ahead to the time already lived: "from now on, too (as up to now)". これからは would instead mark a break ("from now on, unlike before"), which would suit a clean start after the days rejected on line 030 (あんな日々は もう嫌だよ). With も, the reassurance can be heard as resting on continuity: what has carried the speaker so far (031–036) will go on doing so. The same pattern, time word + も, occurs on line 012 (いつも 今も 届かなくて "always, and now too, (it) doesn't reach"): there も stretched a failure across time, and here it stretches 大丈夫 into the future.
- **きっと.** An adverb of the speaker's own conviction: "surely, I'm sure". It often pairs with a guess, as on lines 017–018 (きっとそこに / 最初からあった物なんだろう, a guess about the past). Here it stands with a bare predicate and looks ahead, so it can sound like a promise to oneself. 必ず "without fail" would be more categorical, and たぶん "probably" much weaker.
- **大丈夫 without だ.** 大丈夫だ would be a flat declaration, 大丈夫だよ a reassurance aimed at someone, 大丈夫だろう a guess, and 大丈夫です polite. The bare stem is the everyday casual form and suits talking to oneself. In Chinese, 大丈夫 meant "a fine, stalwart man" (in that sense Japanese reads it だいじょうふ); from "someone solid you can rely on" it came to mean "secure, safe", and then "all right". Its core, 丈夫 (じょうぶ), now means "sturdy, healthy" (丈夫な体 "a strong body"). 大丈夫 appears only here in the song.
- **Link to line 039: the refrain returns.** Nothing carries over in grammar. After the stanza break, line 039 (春風は青く香り やがて緑は芽吹いて) starts a new sentence with its own topic, 春風は, and lines 039–040 repeat lines 005–006 word for word. The first time, the refrain followed line 004's doubt (言葉にして 伝えられるのかな "I wonder if I can put it into words and get it across"). This time it follows the confidence of lines 037–038, after line 036 (言葉にしてきたんだ) has answered that doubt. The spring scene is the same, but what leads into it has changed, so the same breeze, new green and scattering blossoms can be heard first beside a worry and now beside a reassurance. これからも also looks ahead along a line, while the refrain's また ("again", line 040) turns in a cycle; the future the speaker is sure of can be heard as running on into the springs still to come.
