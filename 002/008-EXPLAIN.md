# Line 008 · 努力を積み上げる暇もなく

## Context

- Line 007 (previous): 出来ない事ばかりが 増えてく
- **Line 008 (explained here): 努力を積み上げる暇もなく**
- Line 009 (next): 追い付けない ままでいる

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: adverbial phrase (〜暇もなく "without even time to ~") whose predicate comes on line 009.

The line is grammatical but has no predicate of its own. 努力を積み上げる "build up effort" is a relative clause on 暇 "time (to spare)". The clause tells what the time would be for, so 暇 is not an argument of the verb. も "even" stands where the subject particle が would be (暇がない → 暇もない), and なく is the continuative form (連用形) of the i-adjective ない "there is no". The phrase means "there being no time even to build up effort", and like any 連用形 it needs something after it. Line 007 closes its sentence on the terminal 増えてく, so the natural reading attaches this line forward, to line 009: 努力を積み上げる暇もなく、追い付けないままでいる "with no time even to build up my efforts, I remain unable to catch up". Because the lyric has no punctuation, the line can also be heard as an afterthought to line 007, placed after its verb (see Notes). Its subject, as everywhere in this stanza, is the unstated speaker (the わたし of line 020). The line itself is plain prose; only its link to the lines around it is open. Restored prose, with line 009: 努力を積み上げる暇もなく、（わたしは）追い付けないままでいる。

## Reading

- Kana: どりょく を つみあげる ひま も なく
- Romaji: doryoku o tsumiageru hima mo naku

## How it fits together

`[[[努力 を 積み上げる] 暇] も なく] → line 009 (わたしは) [[追い付け ない] まま] で いる`

努力を積み上げる is a relative clause of the content type: 暇 is not its subject or object; the clause names what the time would be for, as in 休む暇 "time to rest" or 寝る時間 "time to sleep". The noun phrase is the subject of ない, with も in place of が. なく then works adverbially, setting out the circumstances of the main clause on line 009: no time to work at it, and so no catching up.

## Translation

- Literal: "with not even the time to pile up effort"
- Natural: "With no time even to build up my efforts…" (with line 009: "With no time even to build up my efforts, I still can't catch up.")

## Notes

- **Two ways to attach the line.** The main reading, above, takes 暇もなく forward as the circumstances of line 009. The alternative hears it as an afterthought to line 007, placed after the verb it qualifies (倒置法): 出来ない事ばかりが増えてく、努力を積み上げる暇もなく "only the things I can't do keep piling up, with no time even to build up my efforts". In prose order that would be 努力を積み上げる暇もなく、出来ない事ばかりが増えていく, which is also natural. Either way, lines 007–009 describe one situation: more and more the speaker can't do, no time to work at it, and no catching up.
- **〜暇もなく.** A set pattern for "without a moment to ~": 休む暇もなく働く "work without a moment's rest", 息つく暇もなく "without time to catch one's breath". The close 〜間もなく (まもなく) means "with hardly any time to ~" (着替える間もなく "with no time even to change"). Plain 暇がない is the everyday "have no time" (忙しくて暇がない), and 暇だ on its own means "I'm free" or, often, "I'm bored".
- **ない the adjective, not the auxiliary.** In 暇もなく, ない is an i-adjective meaning "there is no" (暇がない "there is no time"). It is not the negative auxiliary that follows a verb, as in 出来ない (line 007) or 追い付けない (line 009). The two conjugate alike (ない, なく, なくて, なかった), but only the adjective can stand after a noun and its particle. The adjective returns on line 026 as 無くて, written in kanji: 思いやる余裕なんて無くて "with no room at all to think of others". That line has the same shape as this one, a relative clause + a noun for "time / room" + a particle + ない. In the first verse there is no time to build up effort; in the second, no room to care for anyone else.
- **積み上げる and 積み重ねる.** The song uses 積み上げる here and on line 021 (小さな夢積み上げて), and 積み重ねる on line 031 (積み重ねてきた事だけが). 積み上げる "pile up" looks at the height the pile reaches; 積み重ねる "stack one on another" looks at the many layers added over time. 努力を積み重ねる is the most common collocation, but 努力を積み上げる is also natural, as in 実績を積み上げる "build up a track record". The verb traces an arc: here there is no time to build anything; on line 031, what has been built up is the one thing that gave the speaker strength.
- **Spelling.** 暇 is the everyday kanji for ひま. In kana, なく is the spelling style guides prefer for ない in both its uses. The song writes it in kana here but 無くて in kanji on line 026, another case where the song's spelling varies without any change in meaning.
