# Line 039 · 春風は青く香り やがて緑は芽吹いて

## Context

- Line 038 (previous): これからも きっと大丈夫
- **Line 039 (explained here): 春風は青く香り やがて緑は芽吹いて**
- Line 040 (next): 祝福の光の中 また桜は散ってゆく

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — poetic: two linked clauses (continuative 香り, te-form 芽吹いて) with a synaesthetic 青く香る; the sentence continues to line 040, the last line of the song.

The line repeats line 005 word for word, and its grammar is the same. It holds two clauses, each with its own topic. In the first, 春風は "the spring breeze" is the topic, and 香り, the continuative form (連用形) of 香る "be fragrant", ends the clause and links it on (連用中止法, a written, slightly literary "…and"). In the second, やがて "before long" introduces 緑は "the green", and 芽吹いて, the te-form of 芽吹く "bud, sprout", leaves it open. Line 040 completes the sentence with a third topic (桜は) and the terminal 散ってゆく. 青く香る, a colour word describing a scent, is a poetic choice, not an error. The space marks the break between the clauses, where prose would put a comma. Line 038 closes the final stanza, and the song's last new sentence, on 大丈夫, so after the stanza break this line starts the returning refrain with a new sentence. Restored prose: 春風は青く香り、やがて緑は芽吹いて、祝福の光の中、また桜は散ってゆく。

## Reading

- Kana: はるかぜ は あおく かおり / やがて みどり は めぶいて
- Romaji: harukaze wa aoku kaori / yagate midori wa mebuite

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 春風 | はるかぜ (harukaze) | noun, compound of 春 "spring" + 風 "wind", with rendaku か → が | spring breeze (the Sino-Japanese reading しゅんぷう also exists; はるかぜ is the likely one in a song) |
| 2 | は | は (wa) | binding particle (係助詞), topic | marks 春風 as the topic of 香り; the first of three parallel topics (春風は, 緑は, 桜は on line 040) |
| 3 | 青く | あおく (aoku) | i-adjective (形容詞) 青い, continuative form (連用形) used adverbially | "blue / green; fresh, young": modifies 香り, "smell fresh and green" |
| 4 | 香り | かおり (kaori) | godan verb 香る, continuative form (連用形) used to link clauses (連用中止法) | to be fragrant; "…is fragrant, and…". Same shape as the noun 香り "scent", but here a verb |
| 5 | やがて | やがて (yagate) | adverb (副詞) | "before long, in time"; modifies 芽吹いて |
| 6 | 緑 | みどり (midori) | noun | green; the new green of leaves and grass (新緑) |
| 7 | は | は (wa) | binding particle (係助詞), topic | the second parallel topic |
| 8 | 芽吹い | めぶい (mebui) | godan verb 芽吹く, continuative form with euphonic change (イ音便: 芽吹き → 芽吹い) before て | to bud, to sprout |
| 9 | て | て (te) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞), te-form | "…and": links on to line 040; the sentence stays open |

## How it fits together

`[春風 は [青く 香り]] ／ やがて [緑 は 芽吹い て] → line 040 [[祝福 の 光] の 中] また [桜 は 散っ て ゆく]`

The refrain's sentence has three clauses, each with a は-topic, in the order of a passing spring: breeze, buds, falling blossoms. 香り and 芽吹いて both mean "…and"; neither has a tense, and both take the non-past of ゆく on line 040, the last verb of the song.

## Translation

- Literal: "the spring breeze is fragrant greenly, and before long the green buds, and…"
- Natural: "The spring breeze carries a fresh green scent, and soon new green buds appear…"

## Notes

- **Same words, new place.** Lines 039–040 repeat lines 005–006 exactly, but what leads into them has changed. The first time, the refrain followed line 004's doubt (言葉にして 伝えられるのかな "I wonder if I can put it into words and get it across"), and the stanza after it opened on failure (出来ない事ばかりが 増えてく, line 007). This time it follows the reassurance of lines 037–038 (信じる素直な 気持ちさえあれば / これからも きっと大丈夫), and line 036 (言葉にしてきたんだ) has already answered the doubt. The breeze, the buds and the blossoms are the same; what has changed is the speaker who hears them.
- **Link to line 038.** Nothing carries over in grammar: 大丈夫 ends its sentence, and this line has its own topic, 春風は. The tie is one of meaning. これからも "from now on, too" looks ahead in a straight line. The refrain, with やがて "before long" here and また "again" on line 040, shows time moving on and coming round. The future the speaker is sure of runs on into the springs to come.
- **青く香る.** Japanese 青い covers much of English blue and green (青葉 "green leaves", 青信号 "green light") and also means "fresh, young, unripe" (青春 "youth"). A scent described with a colour word is synaesthetic, the scent of new greenery and of youth at once. In the first refrain the 青 of youth stood beside the speaker's doubts. Here it can be heard as youth that is still going on, with its difficulties and its fresh start.
- **香り: verb, not noun.** 香り has a subject (春風は) and an adverb (青く), so it is the 連用形 of the verb 香る, not the noun "fragrance". An adjective's continuative form can also end a clause (空は青く、海は広い "the sky is blue and the sea is wide"), so 春風は青く、香り "the breeze is green, and fragrant" is a possible second parse; without a space between 青く and 香り, the adverbial reading is the natural one.
- **Three topics with は.** 春風は, 緑は and 桜は (line 040) set out the scene in parallel frames. With が, the lines would read as a report of events; with は, they read as the familiar features of a season that returns every year.
- **Link to line 040.** 芽吹いて leaves the sentence open, and line 040, the last line of the song, closes it: 祝福の光の中 また桜は散ってゆく "and in the light of blessing, the cherry blossoms fall once again". The song ends on the refrain rather than on the speaker's own words.
