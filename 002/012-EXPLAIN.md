# Line 012 · いつも 今も 届かなくて

## Context

- Line 011 (previous): 揺れる 焦る 燻ぶった熱を 抱えてる
- **Line 012 (explained here): いつも 今も 届かなくて**
- Line 013 (next): 思い切って挑んで 壁に当たって

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: negative te-form (〜なくて) left open at the end of the stanza.

The line is grammatical but has no main predicate. いつも "always" and 今も "now too" are two time adverbials, and 届かなくて is the te-form of 届かない "doesn't reach". The te-form can't end a sentence, and the stanza ends right after it. Neither the subject (what doesn't reach: a hand, a voice, feelings) nor the goal (〜に) is stated. Line 011 ends its sentence on the terminal 抱えてる, so this line is most naturally heard as an afterthought to it: the reason for the smouldering heat, placed after the clause it explains (倒置法). It can also be heard as a sentence left hanging, or as running on across the stanza break into the te-form chain of lines 013–015 (see Notes). The spaces mark short pauses between the time words; prose would write いつも、今も届かなくて. Restored prose, as an afterthought to line 011: （わたしは）くすぶった熱を抱えている。いつも、今も（手が）届かなくて。

## Reading

- Kana: いつも / いま も / とどかなくて
- Romaji: itsumo / ima mo / todokanakute

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | いつも | いつも (itsumo) | adverb (副詞); historically いつ "when" + も | always, all the time |
| 2 | 今 | いま (ima) | noun (time noun, used adverbially) | now |
| 3 | も | も (mo) | binding particle (係助詞) | "too, even": 今も "now too, even now, still"; adds the present to いつも |
| 4 | 届か | とどか (todoka) | godan verb 届く, irrealis form (未然形) before ない | to reach, to arrive: 手が届く "can reach (with one's hand)", 想いが届く "one's feelings reach (someone)". Subject and goal unstated |
| 5 | なく | なく (naku) | auxiliary verb (助動詞) ない, negative, continuative form (連用形) | "not"; ない conjugates like an i-adjective: ない → なく + て |
| 6 | て | て (te) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞), te-form | "…and / …so"; left open at the end of the stanza |

## How it fits together

`[いつも] ／ [今 も] ／ [(〜が) (〜に) 届か なく て] → (reason for line 011 / left hanging / on to line 013)`

いつも and 今も both modify 届かなくて, and together they cover all of time up to the present: "it has never reached, and it doesn't now". The te-form carries no tense and waits for a predicate that the stanza never gives it. Heard as an afterthought, its link goes back to line 011's 抱えてる: because it never reaches, the heat stays smouldering.

## Translation

- Literal: "always, now too, (it) not reaching, and…"
- Natural: "It never reaches, not ever, not even now."

## Notes

- **What doesn't reach?** 届く marks what travels with が (手が届く, 声が届く "a voice carries", 想いが届く "feelings get through") and its goal with に. The lyric names neither, and two earlier lines offer candidates. Line 010's 伸ばした手 suggests the hand: 手が届かない "it's out of reach". Line 004's 言葉にして 伝えられるのかな suggests feelings and words: 気持ちが届かない "my feelings don't get through". The lyric can hold both, the goal the speaker can't reach and the person their feelings don't reach.
- **Three ways to hear the open te-form.** (a) As an afterthought to line 011, giving the reason (倒置法). In prose order: いつも今も届かなくて、燻ぶった熱を抱えてる "never reaching, always and still, I carry this smouldering heat". (b) As a sentence left hanging (言いさし), with the rest, something like つらい "it hurts", unsaid. (c) As the first of a chain that runs across the stanza break into lines 013–015 (思い切って挑んで 壁に当たって… もどかしさ募る), up to the first main verb after it, 募る. The song does the same at line 024, whose open 立ち止まって can be heard to run on into lines 025–028.
- **〜なくて and 〜ないで.** Both are te-forms of a negative, with different uses. 〜なくて gives a cause or a state, "not ~, and so…" (届かなくて悔しい "it hurts that it doesn't reach"). 〜ないで gives the manner, "without ~ing" (手を伸ばさないで待つ "wait without reaching out"), or a request (行かないで "don't go"). Only なくて fits here. The song uses it again on line 029, 立ち上がれなくて "unable to stand up", in the other two-line stanza (029–030). That stanza, unlike this one, gets a closing line.
- **いつも 今も.** いつも is historically いつ + も, "at whatever time". With 今も after it, the line has two も, each stretching the failure further: "always, and even now". Line 038 uses the same shape the other way round, これからも "from now on, too", to stretch a reassurance into the future.
- **届く.** Besides "reach" and "get through", 届く is the everyday verb for deliveries arriving (荷物が届く "a parcel arrives"). Its transitive partner is 届ける "deliver, take (something) to someone". In love songs 想いが届く, "one's feelings reach the other person", is a stock phrase, which colours the line even though the lyric names no feelings.
- **Link to line 013.** After the stanza break, line 013 (思い切って挑んで 壁に当たって) turns the smouldering heat into action: the speaker finally takes the plunge. However the te-form is heard, the story runs on from the failure to reach to the decision to try.
