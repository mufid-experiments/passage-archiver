# Line 024 · 息切らせ立ち止まって

## Context

- Line 023 (previous): 追いかけるのに必死で
- **Line 024 (explained here): 息切らせ立ち止まって**
- Line 025 (next): 自分の事でいっぱいで つらくて

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: continuative 息切らせ (with を omitted) + te-form 立ち止まって; the last link of the chain of lines 021–024, left open at the end of the stanza.

The line is grammatical but has no main predicate. It holds two actions of the speaker (the わたし of line 020). The first is 息（を）切らせ "running out of breath, panting". 息を切らせる is a variant of the idiom 息を切らす, and 切らせ is its continuative form (連用形), used to link clauses (連用中止法). The object particle を is dropped, and the two words are written together. The second action follows with no て and no space: 立ち止まって "coming to a stop", the te-form of the compound 立ち止まる. That te-form is the last link of the stanza's chain (積み上げて, 曖昧で, 必死で, 立ち止まって), and the stanza ends without a main verb. A listener can hear the chain left hanging, or running on into the next stanza up to its first main verb, 掛けてしまったの on line 028 (see Notes). Prose would write 息を切らせて立ち止まって or 息を切らし、立ち止まって. Restored prose: （わたしは）息を切らせ、立ち止まって、…

## Reading

- Kana: いき きらせ たちどまって

## How it fits together

- This line: `(line 023: 追いかけるのに必死で →) (わたしは) [息 (を) 切らせ] ／ [立ち止まっ て] → (open)`
- The stanza: `(わたしは) [小さな 夢 (を) 積み上げ て]、[願い事 は 曖昧 で]、[追いかける の に 必死 で]、[息 (を) 切らせ 立ち止まっ て] → (no main verb)`

The two actions follow each other: breath gives out, and then the legs stop. 切らせ links without て, the written 連用中止法, and 立ち止まって links with it, so the line ends on the more open of the two. The stanza's four links add up to a story (small dreams, vague wishes, a desperate chase, a stop) without a main verb to give it a tense.

## Translation

- Literal: "running out of breath, stopping, and…"
- Natural: "and out of breath, I came to a stop…"

## Notes

- **息を切らす and 息を切らせる.** Both mean "be out of breath, pant": 息を切らして走ってきた "came running, out of breath", 息を切らせて駆けつけた "rushed over, panting". 切らす is "use up, run out of" (在庫を切らす "run out of stock"). 切らせる is in form the causative of 切る "cut", but in this idiom it is used just like 切らす. The related noun 息切れ (いきぎれ) "shortness of breath" also has a figurative sense, "running out of steam" (後半で息切れする "flag in the second half"), and both senses fit a chase that has worn the speaker out.
- **Two verbs pressed together.** 息切らせ runs straight into 立ち止まって with no て, no comma and no space, as 倒れ起きて does on line 014 in the first half of the song. There the compressed pair was falling and getting up again. Here it is losing one's breath and stopping, with no getting up.
- **The stanza never closes.** Lines 021–024 end on four open links and no main verb, so the stanza itself stops mid-sentence, like the speaker. It can be heard as left hanging (言いさし), or as running on across the stanza break into the next chain (lines 025–027, いっぱいで, つらくて, 無くて, なんて) up to the first main verb after it, 掛けてしまったの on line 028. Line 012 (届かなくて) is the same kind of open ending, in the first half of the song.
- **立ち止まる.** 立ち止まる is "stop (walking), stand still", often to look at something or to think (足を止める is close). The same verb returns on line 033, in the conditional 立ち止まっても "even if I stop". Here the speaker actually stops; there, stopping is allowed for in the future, and the future still will not close (未来は閉ざされたりしない, line 034).
- **The end of a run.** The stanza moves from piling up dreams (line 021) through a chase (line 023) to a halt, the same arc as lines 013–015 in the first half (trying, falling, unable to move forward). The difference is what comes next. After line 015 the scenery flows on (line 016). After this line, the next stanza turns inward (line 025, 自分の事でいっぱいで "so caught up in my own problems").
- **Link to line 025.** After the stanza break, line 025 opens a new chain on what the chase left behind: the speaker, stopped and out of breath, with no room in their head for anything but their own troubles. That chain ends on line 028, with a regret (負担ばかり掛けてしまったの "all I did was put a burden on you").
