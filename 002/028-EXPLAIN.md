# Line 028 · 負担ばかり掛けてしまったの

## Context

- Line 027 (previous): 慰めて欲しい、なんて
- **Line 028 (explained here): 負担ばかり掛けてしまったの**
- Line 029 (next): 震え 竦み 目を逸らし 立ち上がれなくて

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — completes the sentence begun on line 025: main predicate (〜てしまった) with sentence-final explanatory の.

The line is grammatical and ends the sentence that began on line 025. Its predicate, 掛けてしまった, is the first main verb since the stanza began: 負担を掛ける "put a burden (on someone)" + しまう "end up, to one's regret", in the past. ばかり "nothing but" stands in place of を (負担を掛ける → 負担ばかり掛ける): all the speaker gave was burden. The sentence ends on the particle の, which explains the situation softly, like a quieter version of んだ. The person burdened, who would be marked with に (相手に, あなたに), is unstated, as is the speaker (the わたし of line 020). With this past tense, the whole chain of lines 025–027 is told as the past. Nothing needs correcting. A stanza break follows, and line 029 opens the two-line stanza of lines 029–030. Restored prose, lines 025–028: （わたしは）自分の事でいっぱいで、つらくて、（人を）思いやる余裕なんて無くて、「慰めて欲しい」なんて（言って）、（相手に）負担ばかり掛けてしまったの。

## Reading

- Kana: ふたん ばかり かけて しまった の
- Romaji: futan bakari kakete shimatta no

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |

| 1 | 負担 | ふたん (futan) | noun (Sino-Japanese, 漢語) | burden, load (負 "bear" + 担 "carry on the shoulder") |
| 2 | ばかり | ばかり (bakari) | adverbial particle (副助詞) | "nothing but": stands in place of を; too much of something unwanted, as on lines 002 and 007 |

## How it fits together

- This line: `(lines 025–027 →) (わたしは) (相手に) [負担 ばかり] [掛け て しまっ た] の。`
- Whole sentence: `(わたしは) [自分 の 事 で いっぱい で]、 [つらく て]、 [[(人を) 思いやる] 余裕 なんて 無く て]、 [「慰め て 欲しい」 なんて (言って)]、 (相手に) [負担 ばかり 掛け て しまっ た] の`

The chain of states and actions (full, hurting, no room, asking for comfort) ends in a single result. The te-forms take their tense from しまった, so the sentence looks back: "I was…, I had no…, and I ended up…". The final の presents the whole sentence as an explanation, as if telling someone what really happened.

## Translation

- Literal: "(it's that I) ended up imposing nothing but burdens"
- Natural: "and all I ever did was weigh you down." (whole sentence, lines 025–028: "I was so caught up in my own problems, and it was all so hard, that I had no room at all to think of anyone else; saying things like 'I want you to comfort me,' I ended up being nothing but a burden.")

## Notes

- **〜てしまった: regret.** しまう makes the action complete and, very often, regrettable: 言ってしまった "I said it (and wish I hadn't)". Casual speech contracts it to 〜ちゃった (掛けちゃった), but the song keeps the full form, as on line 010 (摺り抜けてしまう). There it was non-past, something that keeps happening to the speaker. Here it is past, something the speaker did and now regrets.
- **負担を掛ける and its relatives.** The same かける appears in several fixed phrases about what one causes others: 負担を掛ける "be a burden", 迷惑を掛ける "cause trouble", 心配を掛ける "make someone worry", 手間を掛ける "put someone to trouble". In these senses the verb is often written in kana (負担をかける). 体に負担を掛ける "put strain on the body" shows the literal side of 負担, a load to carry.
- **ばかり, a third time.** すれ違ってばかりの (line 002), 出来ない事ばかりが (line 007), and now 負担ばかり: each time ばかり marks too much of something unwanted. Here it stands in place of を, the way も and なんて stood in place of が on lines 008 and 026. In the first two the trouble happens to the speaker; here the speaker causes it, which sharpens the self-reproach.
- **Sentence-final の.** In a plain statement, a bare の with falling intonation explains or confides: "the thing is, I…". It is softer than のだ / んだ and is common in gentle or intimate speech. Compare the song's んだ endings (line 020 わたしなんだ, line 032 くれたんだ, line 036 言葉にしてきたんだ), which assert a conclusion. This の sounds like a confession told to someone, perhaps the very person who was burdened.
- **Who was burdened?** The lyric never says. The natural candidate is the person asked for comfort on line 027, someone close. The other party in the quarrels of lines 001–002 is never named either, so it may or may not be the same person.
- **Link to line 029.** の ends the sentence and the stanza of lines 025–028. After the break, the two-line stanza of lines 029–030 sums up the low point: 震え 竦み 目を逸らし 立ち上がれなくて "trembling, frozen, looking away, unable to get up", and then the refusal, あんな日々は もう嫌だよ "I never want days like those again".
