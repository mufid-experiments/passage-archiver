# Line 015 · 前に進めずに もどかしさ募る

## Context

- Line 014 (previous): 倒れ起きて 繰り返して
- **Line 015 (explained here): 前に進めずに もどかしさ募る**
- Line 016 (next): やがて景色は流れてゆく

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — completes the sentence begun on line 013: last link (〜ずに) and main predicate 募る, with the subject particle が omitted.

The line is grammatical and ends the sentence that line 013 began. Its first half, 前に進めずに "unable to move forward", is the last link of the chain (挑んで, 当たって, 倒れ起きて, 繰り返して). It is built from the potential 進める "can advance" and ず, the literary negative, followed by に, which together give 〜ずに "without ~ing, not ~ing, and so…". Its subject is still the speaker (the わたし of line 020). The second half is the main clause, with a new subject: もどかしさ "frustration", a noun made from the adjective もどかしい, and 募る "grow stronger, mount", in its terminal form. The subject particle が is dropped (もどかしさが募る), which is common in lyrics. The space falls between the two halves, where prose would put a comma. The register is mixed in an ordinary way: ずに and 募る are slightly written, もどかしさ is everyday. Restored prose, lines 013–015: （わたしは）思い切って挑んで、壁に当たって、倒れては起きて、（それを）繰り返して、前に進めずに、もどかしさ（が）募る。

## Reading

- Kana: まえ に すすめず に / もどかしさ つのる
- Romaji: mae ni susumezu ni / modokashisa tsunoru

## How it fits together

`(lines 013–014: 挑んで、当たって、倒れ起きて、繰り返して →) [前 に [進め ず] に] ／ [もどかしさ (が) 募る]。`

The ずに-phrase is the last link of the speaker's chain of actions, and it shades into a cause: none of the trying gets them forward, and so the frustration builds. The main clause switches subject, from the speaker to their feeling. Japanese often does this at the end of a te-chain (雨が降って、試合が中止になった "it rained, and the match was called off"). 募る is non-past, a present process: the frustration is mounting now.

## Translation

- Literal: "not being able to advance forward, frustration grows"
- Natural: "and still I can't move forward, and the frustration keeps building." (whole sentence, lines 013–015: "I take the plunge and try, I run into a wall, I fall and get back up, over and over, and still I can't move forward: the frustration keeps building.")

## Notes

- **〜ずに and 〜ないで.** Both mean "without ~ing" or "not ~ing, and…": 進めずに = 進めないで. ずに uses ず, the negative of classical Japanese, and sounds more written. This is the only ず in the song; everywhere else the negative is ない.
- **進める: potential or transitive?** Two different verbs share the shape 進める. One is the potential of 進む, "can advance" (前に進める "can move forward"). The other is the transitive 進める, "advance something, push ahead with" (計画を進める "push ahead with a plan"). The lyric has 前に and no object, so it is the potential. The same image returns on lines 031–032, 前に / 進む力をくれたんだ "gave me the strength to move forward": what the speaker cannot do here, they are later given the strength to do.
- **もどかしさ.** もどかしい is the frustration of something that won't go as fast or as well as one wants: 言葉にできないもどかしさ "the frustration of not being able to put it into words", which recalls line 004. It differs from 悔しい "vexed (at losing)" and from いらいら "irritated". The suffix さ turns an adjective into a noun of degree (寂しい → 寂しさ, 優しい → 優しさ); the song has another of these on line 035, 不甲斐なさ "feebleness".
- **募る.** Intransitive, of feelings and of wind, 募る means "grow stronger, intensify": 不安が募る "anxiety mounts", 思いが募る "longing grows". Transitive, it means "recruit, invite, call for" (参加者を募る "invite participants", as in 募集 "recruitment"). Here it is intransitive, and the dropped が would follow もどかしさ.
- **The end of the chain.** Lines 013–015 are one sentence of five te-links and a main clause. Its subjects are the speaker, who acts, and their frustration, which grows. In English the switch reads more smoothly with "I": "I grow more and more frustrated".
- **Link to line 016.** 募る closes the sentence, and line 016 (やがて景色は流れてゆく) starts a new one. The verbs of effort stop. やがて "before long", as in the refrain (line 005), moves the song on, and lines 017–020 turn from struggling to reflecting.
