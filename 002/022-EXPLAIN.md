# Line 022 · 願い事は暖味で

## Context

- Line 021 (previous): 小さな夢積み上げて
- **Line 022 (explained here): 願い事は暖味で**
- Line 023 (next): 追いかけるのに必死で

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Not valid as written: 暖味 is not a word. It is almost certainly a mistranscription of 曖昧 (あいまい, "vague"). Corrected, the line is valid — fragment: topic + na-adjective in its continuative form (曖昧で), the second link of the chain of lines 021–024.

As written, the line has a word that does not exist. 暖味 would be read だんみ, "warm" + "taste", and no dictionary lists it; it makes no sense in the stanza either. The intended word is clear from the shapes. 曖 (日 + 愛) and 暖 (日 + 爰) share their left half and look alike on the right. 昧 (日 + 未) and 味 (口 + 未) share their right half. 曖昧 "vague, unclear, ambiguous" is a common word that fits the line exactly, and it also gives the line the same length as the other three lines of the stanza (see Notes). With 曖昧, the line is grammatical. 願い事は "(my) wishes" is the topic, and 曖昧で is the continuative form (連用形) of the na-adjective 曖昧だ: "are vague, and…". It is the second link of the chain that line 021 began (積み上げて), and the chain goes on through 必死で (line 023) and 立ち止まって (line 024) without reaching a main verb. The subject switches here from the speaker to their wishes, and then switches back on line 023. Restored prose: （わたしの）願い事は曖昧で、…

## Reading

- Kana (with 曖昧): ねがいごと は あいまい で
- Romaji: negaigoto wa aimai de
- As written, 暖味 would be だんみ (danmi), which is not a word.

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 願い事 | ねがいごと (negaigoto) | noun, compound of 願い (連用形 of 願う "wish", used as a noun) + 事 "thing", with rendaku こと → ごと | a wish, something wished for (often a wish made on a star or at a shrine) |
| 2 | は | は (wa) | binding particle (係助詞), topic | marks 願い事 as the topic: "as for (my) wishes" |
| 3 | 暖味 → 曖昧 | あいまい (aimai) | na-adjective (形容動詞) 曖昧だ, stem (語幹) | vague, unclear, ambiguous. As written, 暖味 (だんみ) is not a word |
| 4 | で | で (de) | na-adjective ending: continuative form (連用形) of 曖昧だ (same shape as the copula's で) | "(they) are vague, and…"; links on to line 023 |

## How it fits together

`(line 021: (わたしは) 小さな夢積み上げて →) [願い事 は] [曖昧 で] → line 023 (わたしは) [追いかける の に 必死 で] → line 024`

The topic 願い事は has its own predicate, 曖昧で, a state, not an action. Like the links around it, the continuative form has no tense and no main verb to take one from. Between two lines about the speaker's actions, piling up dreams (line 021) and chasing desperately (line 023), this line says what is wrong with the wishes themselves: they are unclear.

## Translation

- Literal: "(my) wishes are vague, and…"
- Natural: "never quite sure what I was wishing for…"

## Notes

- **Why 曖昧 and not 暖味.** Three things point the same way. (1) Shape: each kanji of 曖昧 has a near twin in 暖味 (曖/暖 share 日 and similar right halves; 昧/味 share 未), a classic slip when lyrics are copied or scanned. Writing 暖昧 for 曖昧 is in fact a well-known misspelling. (2) Meaning: 願い事は曖昧で "my wishes are vague" fits between piling up small dreams (line 021) and a desperate chase (line 023); "warm taste" fits nothing. (3) Rhythm: read as あいまい, the line has eleven morae (ね・が・い・ご・と・は・あ・い・ま・い・で), exactly like the other three lines of the stanza; read as だんみ it would have ten.
- **曖昧.** An everyday word, despite its complicated kanji: 曖昧な返事 "a vague answer", 曖昧な記憶 "a hazy memory", 曖昧にする "leave (something) unclear". Both kanji were added to the 常用漢字 (Jōyō) list in 2010. 曖 is used almost only in this word; 昧 also appears in 三昧 (ざんまい, as in 贅沢三昧 "a life of indulgence"). Here it describes wishes that have no clear shape: the speaker wants something, but cannot say what.
- **The second で.** As on line 025 (いっぱいで), this で is the ending of a na-adjective, not the particle で "at, by". Make the line a finished sentence and it changes: 願い事は曖昧だ / 曖昧だった. The next line ends on another na-adjective in the same form, 必死で "desperate, and…". The stanza thus has two states (曖昧で, 必死で) between two actions (積み上げて, 立ち止まって).
- **願い事.** 願い "a wish" + 事 "thing", voiced to ごと as in 出来事 (できごと) "event". 願い事をする "make a wish" (on a shooting star, at a shrine), 願い事が叶う "a wish comes true". It sounds gentler, even a little childlike, beside 願い or 望み "hope, desire", which suits the 小さな夢 of line 021. In a compound like this, 事 is normally written in kanji even by style guides, unlike the formal noun こと (see line 025).
- **A topic in the middle of a chain.** Lines 021, 023 and 024 have the speaker as subject; this line has its own topic, 願い事は. Japanese te-chains can switch subjects freely (雨が降って、道が混んで、遅刻した "it rained, the roads were jammed, and I was late"), so the chain is not broken. It simply includes a state of the wishes among the speaker's actions.
- **Link to line 023.** Vague wishes and a desperate chase are a telling pair. Line 023 (追いかけるのに必死で "so desperate just to keep up the chase") says the speaker pursues something with all their strength, and this line has just said that they do not clearly know what it is.
