# Line 030 · 踏み出した先に待つのは

## Context

- Line 029 (previous): ここはきっとスタートラインで
- **Line 030 (explained here): 踏み出した先に待つのは**
- Line 031 (next): 新しい世界

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: topic half of a cleft sentence, completed on line 031.

This is (b): grammatical, but a fragment. The line is a nominalized clause marked by は, which is the "What … is" half of the cleft pattern 〜のは X だ (分裂文). X comes on line 031: 踏み出した先に待つのは新しい世界（だ）. The one who steps out and the one being waited for (both the singer, 僕) are left unstated, as is normal in Japanese. Restored prose: （僕が）踏み出した先に待つのは、新しい世界だ。

## Reading

- Kana: ふみだした さき に まつ の は
- Romaji: fumidashita saki ni matsu no wa

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 踏み出し | ふみだし (fumidashi) | godan verb 踏み出す, continuative form (連用形) before た; compound of 踏む "to step" (連用形 踏み) + 出す "to put out" | to step forward, step out; to make a start |
| 2 | た | た (ta) | auxiliary verb (助動詞) た, attributive form (連体形) — completion (完了) | "having (stepped out)"; 踏み出した modifies 先 |
| 3 | 先 | さき (saki) | noun | the point ahead, what lies beyond |
| 4 | に | に (ni) | case particle (格助詞) — location | "at, in"; marks 先 as where the waiting happens |
| 5 | 待つ | まつ (matsu) | godan verb 待つ, attributive form (連体形) | to wait; here "to await, lie in store"; modifies の |
| 6 | の | の (no) | nominalizer (準体助詞) | "the thing that…"; turns 踏み出した先に待つ into a noun phrase |
| 7 | は | は (wa) | binding particle (係助詞) — topic marker | makes "the thing that waits…" the topic; its comment is line 031 |

## How it fits together

`[[[踏み出し た] 先] に 待つ] の は → line 031 (新しい世界)`

踏み出した is a relative clause modifying 先 ("the point beyond the step"). 先に says where the waiting happens, and the whole clause modifies the nominalizer の ("the thing that waits there"), which は makes the topic. The comment (predicate) is 新しい世界（だ） on line 031, so the subject of 待つ is the new world: 新しい世界が（僕を）待っている.

## Translation

- Literal: "The thing that waits at the point beyond having stepped out is…"
- Natural: "…and what awaits me once I take that step is…"

## Notes

- This line opens the second clause of the sentence begun on line 029 (…スタートラインで, "is the starting line, and"). The clause is completed by 新しい世界 on line 031.
- た here marks completion relative to 先, not past time (relative tense, 相対テンス). The singer is at the starting line (line 029), so the step may still lie ahead. 踏み出した先 means "the place you reach once you have stepped out", as in 角を曲がった先に店がある "there's a shop just past the corner" (lit. "at the point after turning the corner").
- 〜の先に待つのは… / 〜た先に待つもの ("what lies in store beyond …") is a common pattern. The thing that waits is the subject, and the person it waits for is usually left unstated.
- The cleft 〜のは X（だ） puts X in focus and holds it back until the end. Here the line break makes the listener wait for the answer.
- 踏み出す often takes an object or a destination (一歩を踏み出す "take a step", 新しい世界へ踏み出す "step out into a new world"). Here it stands alone: "to step out, to make a start".
- 先 is read さき here, not せん (as in 先生).
