# Line 001 · 気温は予報通りに / 肌を刺すような白

This window has only two lines (start of the passage), so both lines are explained.

## Context

- **Line 001 (explained here): 気温は予報通りに**
- **Line 002 (explained here): 肌を刺すような白**

## Line 001 · 気温は予報通りに

### Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: predicate omitted after adverbial 予報通りに.

The line is grammatical but not a complete sentence. 気温は sets up the topic, and 予報通りに is an adverbial ("as forecast") that needs a verb to modify, but the line stops before any verb arrives. The most natural reading is that the obvious predicate is simply left unsaid (trailing off, 言いさし): 気温は予報通りに（下がった） "the temperature (dropped), as forecast". This kind of ellipsis is ordinary in lyrics, and nothing needs correcting. The Notes give a second reading that carries 予報通りに into line 002.

### Reading

- Kana: きおん は よほうどおり に
- Romaji: kion wa yohō-dōri ni

### Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 気温 | きおん (kion) | noun | air temperature |
| 2 | は | は (wa) | topic particle (係助詞) | marks 気温 as the topic, "as for the temperature"; 気温 would also be the subject of the omitted verb |
| 3 | 予報 | よほう (yohō) | noun | forecast (here, the weather forecast) |
| 4 | 通り | どおり (dōri) | suffix (接尾語); とおり voiced to どおり (rendaku, 連濁) | "in accordance with, just as"; 予報通り = "as forecast" |
| 5 | に | に (ni) | case particle (格助詞), adverbial use (manner) | makes 予報通り work as an adverb, "as forecast" (equivalently, the adverbial form of 予報通りだ); the verb it should modify is not stated |

### How it fits together

`[気温 は] [[予報 通り] に] （predicate omitted, e.g. 下がった）`

気温 is the topic. 予報通りに is an adverbial of manner, "in line with the forecast", waiting for a verb that never comes; with the predicate restored, the sentence is 気温は予報通りに下がった.

### Translation

- Literal: "The temperature, in accordance with the forecast, …"
- Natural: "Just as forecast, the temperature (has dropped)…"

## Line 002 · 肌を刺すような白

### Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — poetic: noun-ending fragment (体言止め).

The line is a grammatical noun phrase, not a sentence: there is no predicate, and it ends on the noun 白 (体言止め), presenting an image instead of making a statement. The clause 肌を刺す ("pierce the skin") plus ような ("like, as if") modifies 白, and the implied subject of 刺す is the white itself. As a full sentence it would be something like （辺りは）肌を刺すような白（だった） "(everything around) was a white that stung the skin". Nothing is ungrammatical, only unexpected: the stock phrase is 肌を刺すような寒さ "skin-piercing cold", and here 白 takes the place of 寒さ.

### Reading

- Kana: はだ を さす ような しろ
- Romaji: hada o sasu yō na shiro

### Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 肌 | はだ (hada) | noun | skin |
| 2 | を | を (o) | case particle (格助詞), object marker | marks 肌 as what is pierced |
| 3 | 刺す | さす (sasu) | godan verb 刺す, attributive form (連体形) | to pierce, prick, sting |
| 4 | ような | ような (yō na) | auxiliary verb (助動詞) ようだ, attributive form (連体形) | simile (比況), "like…, as if…"; turns 肌を刺す into a description of 白 |
| 5 | 白 | しろ (shiro) | noun | white, whiteness |

### How it fits together

`[[肌 を 刺す] ような] 白`

肌を is the object of 刺す, and the clause 肌を刺す plus ような ("as if [it] pierces the skin") modifies the head noun 白. The white is the implied subject of 刺す; there is no predicate, so the phrase stands alone as an image.

### Translation

- Literal: "a white as if piercing the skin"
- Natural: "a white so cold it stings the skin"

## Notes

- **How lines 001 and 002 connect.** Line 002 is a noun phrase, so it cannot be the verb that 予報通りに is waiting for. The usual reading is two juxtaposed images, a flat, weather-report-like statement and then a sensory one: 気温は予報通りに（下がって）、（辺りは）肌を刺すような白（だった） "The temperature dropped as forecast, and all around was a white that stung the skin."
- **Alternative parse.** Read straight across the line break as one topic–comment sentence, with 予報通りに modifying 刺す: 気温は ／ 予報通りに肌を刺すような白（だ） "The temperature: a white that stings the skin, just as forecast." No verb has to be supplied, but 気温 is then loosely equated with a colour, with は meaning only "as for the temperature…". The line break after 予報通りに favours the first reading; either way, the forecast cold has arrived, and it is white.
- **Link to line 003.** The 体言止め closes the opening image. Line 003 (凍えた両手をそっと) starts a new clause that is completed by 包み込む on line 004, and its 凍えた "numb with cold" carries on the cold of lines 001–002.
- **Cold made visible.** 肌を刺すような is a stock simile for bitter cold (肌を刺すような寒さ／冷気). It is the skin that is stung, not the eyes, so the line is about cold rather than glare; putting the colour 白 where 寒さ is expected turns a feeling into something seen (synaesthesia). The white is most naturally snow or a snowy scene (line 010 mentions 雪 "snow"), but the line itself names only a colour.
- **予報通り(に).** Noun + 通り means "in accordance with": 予定通り "as planned", 思い通り "as one wishes". Directly after a noun it is voiced to どおり; after の or a verb it stays とおり and is usually written in kana (予報のとおり, 言ったとおり). The に is optional in prose (気温は予報通り下がった is also fine); with だ the phrase becomes a predicate (気温は予報通りだ "the temperature is as forecast"), and with の it modifies a noun (予報通りの寒さ "cold, just as forecast").
- **Register.** Plain, written style. Neither line ends in a predicate, so the song opens with images rather than statements.
