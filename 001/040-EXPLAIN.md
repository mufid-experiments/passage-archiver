## Line 039 · もう迷わない

### Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — complete sentence (subject omitted).

This is a grammatical, complete sentence: the adverb もう plus the plain negative predicate 迷わない. Only the subject is dropped, as is normal in Japanese when it is obvious. The subject is the speaker, who uses 僕 for "I" (line 027 こんな僕を). Restored: （僕は）もう迷わない. Line 038 closes its own sentence with the past-tense 振り向いた, so this line starts a new sentence, and line 040 attaches back to it (see Notes).

### Reading

- Kana: もう まよわない
- Romaji: mō mayowanai

### Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | もう | もう (mō) | adverb (副詞) | with a negative: "(not) anymore, no longer"; with an affirmative it means "already" |
| 2 | 迷わ | まよわ (mayowa) | godan verb 迷う, irrealis / negative stem (未然形) | to lose one's way; to be undecided, to waver, to hesitate. Godan verbs ending in う take わ before ない (迷う → 迷わない, like 買う → 買わない) |
| 3 | ない | ない (nai) | auxiliary verb (助動詞) ない, plain non-past, sentence-final form (終止形) | negation "not"; with a first-person subject, the plain non-past reads as a declaration: "(I) won't …" |

### How it fits together

`（僕は） もう [迷わ ない]`

The omitted subject is the speaker (僕). もう applies to the whole negated predicate, and もう…ない implies a change of state: the speaker did waver before, and from now on will not. The line doesn't say what the speaker won't waver about. Line 040 supplies a circumstance instead: "with the days I spent with you in my heart".

### Translation

- Literal: "(I) will no longer be lost / hesitate."
- Natural: "I won't hesitate anymore."

## Line 040 · 君と過ごした日々を胸に

### Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: verbless 「〜を胸に」 phrase (verb omitted), modifying line 039 by inversion (倒置法).

The line is grammatical, but it is not a sentence on its own. It has an object (日々を) and a location (胸に) but no main verb; 過ごした only belongs to the relative clause describing 日々. This is the set pattern 「XをYに」. The verb is left unsaid (something like 抱いて "holding" or 刻んで "engraving"), and the phrase works adverbially, meaning "with X in Y". This is the last line of the song, so nothing follows it. The phrase attaches back to line 039's 迷わない and is placed after it for effect. Restored prose: 君と過ごした日々を胸に（抱いて）、（僕は）もう迷わない. This is idiomatic, not an error.

### Reading

- Kana: きみ と すごした ひび を むね に
- Romaji: kimi to sugoshita hibi o mune ni

### Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 君 | きみ (kimi) | pronoun (代名詞) | "you"; the usual intimate "you" of song lyrics |
| 2 | と | と (to) | case particle (格助詞) — companion | "with": marks 君 as the person the time was spent with. A verb follows, so this is not the listing "and" と of line 034 (出会いと別れ) |
| 3 | 過ごし | すごし (sugoshi) | godan verb 過ごす, continuative form (連用形) | to spend (time). す-verbs take た with no sound change: 過ごす → 過ごした |
| 4 | た | た (ta) | auxiliary verb (助動詞) た, past, attributive form (連体形) | past tense; closes the relative clause 君と過ごした, which modifies 日々 |
| 5 | 日々 | ひび (hibi) | noun | days; 々 repeats the kanji (日日), and the second ひ is voiced to び (rendaku) |
| 6 | を | を (o) | case particle (格助詞) — object marker | marks 日々 as the object of the unspoken verb (抱いて etc.) |
| 7 | 胸 | むね (mune) | noun | chest; figuratively the heart, where feelings and memories are kept |
| 8 | に | に (ni) | case particle (格助詞) — location / target | "in (my heart)": where the days are kept. The line stops here; the verb that would follow is omitted |

### How it fits together

`[[君 と 過ごし た] 日々] を 胸 に （抱いて）`

君と過ごした is a relative clause (連体修飾節) modifying 日々. Its subject, the speaker (僕), is omitted. 日々を (object) and 胸に (location) belong to an unspoken verb. The whole phrase is adverbial (連用修飾): it describes the state in which the speaker "won't hesitate" in line 039.

### Translation

- Literal: "the days (I) spent with you, in (my) chest"
- Natural: "with the days I spent with you held in my heart"

## Notes

- **How the two lines connect.** 039 is a complete sentence. 040 has no main verb, and because it is the song's last line it can only attach back to 039. Japanese normally puts modifiers before the predicate, so the prose order is 君と過ごした日々を胸に、もう迷わない, "With the days I spent with you in my heart, I won't hesitate anymore." Placing the phrase after the verb (倒置法) lets the song end on the memory instead of on a verb, and leaves the ending open. 040 can also be heard as a tag with its own understood verb ("(I'll go on) holding…"), with much the same meaning.
- **The 「〜を胸に」 construction.** 「XをYに」 with no verb is a standard pattern: を marks the thing, に marks the place it is held, and the verb is understood. With 胸に the natural fillers are 抱いて (いだいて, "cherishing"; だいて is the reading for a physical hug), 刻んで ("engraving"), 秘めて ("keeping inside") or しまって ("storing away"). 胸に alone suggests all of them. Other examples: 花束を手に ("bouquet in hand"), 夕日を背に ("with the setting sun at one's back"), 夢を胸に ("with a dream in one's heart"). With 手に and 背に the dropped verb is usually just して (駅を背にして → 駅を背に). The pattern sounds slightly literary and resolute, and it is very common in lyrics, speeches and headlines.
- **Link to line 038** (just before the window). 038 どこか懐かしくて振り向いた ("(the spring breeze) felt somehow nostalgic, and I turned around") is a finished sentence, and 039 starts a new one. The last three lines move from turning to look back (振り向いた, past) to a resolve about what comes next (迷わない, non-past), held up by the memory in 040.
- **Same words, new position.** 039–040 are identical to 017–018. The final chorus (033–040) differs from the first (011–018) only at line 035, which has 大人になってく where line 013 had 大人になって. After 018 the song goes on (019 神様は僕らを切り離して), but 040 closes it. This chorus also comes after lines 029–032: ここはきっとスタートラインで ("this is surely the starting line"), and beyond the first step waits a 新しい世界 where only 君 is missing. After that, the resolve reads as stepping forward into that world.
- もう flips meaning with the polarity of the verb: もう決めた "I've already decided" vs. もう迷わない "I won't hesitate anymore". 迷う means both "lose one's way" (道に迷う) and "be unable to decide" (どうするか迷う). Both senses fit a song about walking a 道 you chose yourself (lines 007–008). 迷わない is a statement, not a command; "Don't hesitate!" would be 迷うな.
- The past tense た in 過ごした marks those days as over: 君 is ここにはいない ("not here", line 024). 胸 (literally "chest") is the usual Japanese seat of feelings and memories, where English says "heart".
