# Line 003 · 凍えた両手をそっと

## Context

- Line 002 (previous): 肌を刺すような白
- **Line 003 (explained here): 凍えた両手をそっと**
- Line 004 (next): 包み込む

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: continues on line 004.

The line is grammatical but stops just before its verb. 凍えた両手を is a direct object (marked by を) and そっと is an adverb; both are waiting for a verb, which arrives alone on line 004: 包み込む. Joined, 凍えた両手をそっと包み込む is a complete, well-formed clause in which only the subject (and the owner of the hands) is left unstated, as Japanese freely allows. Nothing is reordered: object, adverb, verb is the normal order, and the line break simply falls before the verb.

## Reading

- Kana: こごえた りょうて を そっと
- Romaji: kogoeta ryōte o sotto

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 凍え | こごえ (kogoe) | ichidan verb 凍える (こごえる), continuative form (連用形) | to go numb or stiff with cold (of the body, hands, feet) |
| 2 | た | た (ta) | auxiliary verb (助動詞) た, attributive form (連体形) | completion, here a resulting state: 凍えた = "frozen, numbed"; 凍えた modifies 両手 |
| 3 | 両手 | りょうて (ryōte) | noun | both hands |
| 4 | を | を (o) | case particle (格助詞), object marker | marks 凍えた両手 as the object of 包み込む on line 004 |
| 5 | そっと | そっと (sotto) | adverb (副詞) | gently, softly, carefully; modifies 包み込む on line 004 |

## How it fits together

`[[凍え た] 両手 を] そっと ／ 包み込む (line 004)`

凍えた modifies 両手 as a noun-modifying clause. 凍えた両手を is the object, and そっと the manner adverb, of the verb on the next line; the subject (who or what does the wrapping) is not stated.

## Translation

- Literal: "frozen both-hands (object), gently …"
- Natural: "the frozen hands, gently…" (completed on line 004: "gently enfold(s) the frozen hands")

## Notes

- **Lines 003–004 are one clause split across the line break (confirmed).** Line 003 has an object and an adverb but no verb; line 004 has a transitive verb and nothing else: `凍えた両手を (object) + そっと (adverb) ／ 包み込む (verb)` → 凍えた両手をそっと包み込む "gently enfold(s) the frozen hands". The を on line 003 has no verb until 包み込む, and 包み込む has no object except 凍えた両手を.
- **Link to line 002.** Line 002 ended on a noun (体言止め), so line 003 starts a fresh clause rather than continuing it. The cold carries over: after the white that "stings the skin", the hands are 凍えた.
- **Omitted subject and possessor.** The lines never say who (or what) does the wrapping, or whose hands these are. They leave open whether the speaker's own hands are being warmed or one person is enfolding another's. Hands come back on line 009 (君の手を背中に感じて "feeling your hand on (my) back"), but lines 003–004 themselves do not decide it.
- **凍える vs. 凍る.** 凍える (こごえる) is mainly for living bodies: hands, feet or a whole person going numb or stiff with cold. 凍る (こおる) is for water and objects freezing solid. So 凍えた両手 is "hands numb with cold", not hands turned to ice.
- **た before a noun often marks a state.** In noun-modifying position, た often describes a present state rather than a past event: 凍えた両手 ≈ 凍えている両手 "hands that are frozen", like 疲れた顔 "a tired face" or 曲がった道 "a winding road".
- **そっと.** Softly, gently, quietly, so as not to disturb or hurt. Its care contrasts with the stinging cold of line 002.
