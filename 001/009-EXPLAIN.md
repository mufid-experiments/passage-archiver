# Line 009 · 大丈夫 君の手を背中に感じて

## Context

- Line 008 (previous): 道踏みしめてゆく
- **Line 009 (explained here): 大丈夫 君の手を背中に感じて**
- Line 010 (next): ほどけた雪の隙間 咲いた花笑う

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — poetic: copula だ omitted; open te-form in inverted order (倒置法)

The space splits the line into two phrases, much as a comma would in prose. 大丈夫 is a na-adjective (形容動詞) used alone as a predicate with the copula dropped: 大丈夫（だ）, "(it's) all right". That is ordinary casual speech. The second phrase is a grammatical te-form clause. Its subject (the narrator, who says 僕 on line 027) is omitted, and no main verb follows 感じて, so the line is a fragment, not a complete sentence (b). Read together, the te-clause gives the reason for 大丈夫, placed after it. Prose order would be: 君の手を背中に感じて（いるから）、大丈夫（だ）。

## Reading

- Kana: だいじょうぶ / きみ の て を せなか に かんじて
- Romaji: daijōbu / kimi no te o senaka ni kanjite

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 大丈夫 | だいじょうぶ (daijōbu) | na-adjective (形容動詞) 大丈夫だ, stem used alone; copula だ omitted | all right, fine, safe; here a whole utterance: "It's okay" |
| 2 | 君 | きみ (kimi) | pronoun | you (familiar); the person the song addresses |
| 3 | の | の (no) | case particle (格助詞), possessive | "your": links 君 to 手 |
| 4 | 手 | て (te) | noun | hand |
| 5 | を | を (o) | case particle (格助詞), object marker | marks 君の手 as what is felt |
| 6 | 背中 | せなか (senaka) | noun | back (of the body); the narrator's own back |
| 7 | に | に (ni) | case particle (格助詞), location | "on (my) back": where the touch is felt |
| 8 | 感じ | かんじ (kanji) | ichidan verb 感じる, continuative form (連用形) | to feel, to sense |
| 9 | て | て (te) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞), te-form | "feeling… / …and": links the clause back to 大丈夫 (as its reason) or onward; the sentence is left open |

## How it fits together

`大丈夫 (だ) ／ [[君 の] 手] を [背中 に] 感じ て …`

Phrase 1 is a one-word predicate whose subject is unstated ("I'm okay" or "it's all right"). Phrase 2 has an omitted subject (僕), the object 君の手を, the location 背中に and the verb 感じて. The te-form points back to 大丈夫 as its reason and also leaves the clause open toward line 010.

## Translation

- Literal: "All right. Feeling your hand on (my) back…"
- Natural: "I'm okay — I can feel your hand on my back"

## Notes

- Line 008 (道踏みしめてゆく) ends the sentence begun on line 007: the narrator treading, for the first time, a path they chose themselves. Line 009 starts fresh with 大丈夫 and gives the reassurance behind that step.
- Because 感じて is left open, the clause also runs on into line 010 as a loose "and…", with the subject switching to 花 there: "…I feel your hand on my back, and in the gaps of the thawing snow, flowers smile."
- A hand on the back calls up the idiom 背中を押す (lit. "push someone's back" = encourage someone to take the step), though the line doesn't use the idiom itself. Later lines say 君 is absent (ここにはいない君, line 024; 君だけがいない世界, line 032), so the hand is most likely felt in memory or imagination.
- Who says 大丈夫? Most naturally the narrator, either to themselves or to the absent 君 ("I'm okay"). It could also be heard as 君's remembered words. The song marks 君's imagined speech with 「」 and って on line 028, though, and 大丈夫 has no such marking.
- 〜を背中に感じる "to feel X on one's back" is a standard pattern (背中に視線を感じる "to feel eyes on your back"). に marks where the sensation is felt.
- 感じる comes from the older サ変 verb 感ずる, but today it conjugates as an ichidan verb: 感じ + て. A te-form has no tense and doesn't close a sentence, which is why the line feels suspended.
