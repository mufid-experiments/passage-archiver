# Line 013 · 繰り返し 大人になって

## Context

- Line 012 (previous): 幾つもの出会いと別れを
- **Line 013 (explained here): 繰り返し 大人になって**
- Line 014 (next): ちゃんと笑えているかな

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: takes its object from line 012 and continues on line 014.

The line is grammatical. It holds two linked clauses, and their subject (僕, "I") is not stated. 繰り返し is the continuative form (連用形) of the verb 繰り返す, used by itself to join clauses (連用中止法). Its object is the whole of line 012, 幾つもの出会いと別れを. なって is the te-form of なる, which leaves the sentence open for line 014. The space marks a pause where prose would put a comma. Restored prose: 幾つもの出会いと別れを繰り返し、大人になって、（ちゃんと笑えているかな）.

## Reading

- Kana: くりかえし おとな に なって
- Romaji: kurikaeshi otona ni natte

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 繰り返し | くりかえし (kurikaeshi) | godan verb 繰り返す, continuative form (連用形) used as a clause link (連用中止法) | to repeat; compound of 繰る (to reel, wind) + 返す (back, again); takes 幾つもの出会いと別れを (line 012) as its object: "(I) repeat …, and" |
| 2 | 大人 | おとな (otona) | noun; special whole-word reading (熟字訓) | adult, grown-up (the other readings, たいじん and だいにん, belong to other uses) |
| 3 | に | に (ni) | case particle (格助詞) | marks the result of a change: 〜になる = "become ~" |
| 4 | なっ | なっ (nat-) | godan verb なる, continuative form (連用形) with sound change to small っ (促音便) before て | to become |
| 5 | て | て (te) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞) | te-form link ("…and / having …"); leaves the sentence open for line 014 |

## How it fits together

`[(幾つもの出会いと別れを) 繰り返し]、 [[大人 に] なっ て] → line 014`

Two clauses share the unstated subject 僕 "I". Clause 1 is the object from line 012 + 繰り返し, and its 連用形 links it to clause 2, 大人に + なる, where に marks what the speaker becomes. The te-form なって is still open and attaches to the main predicate 笑えている on line 014.

## Translation

- Literal: "(many meetings and partings) repeating, becoming an adult, and…"
- Natural: "…I've gone through (so many meetings and partings) again and again, and grown up — (am I smiling properly now?)"

## Notes

- The sentence starts on line 011 (巡りゆく季節の隅っこで, "in a corner of the circling seasons", which sets the scene). Line 012 gives the object, this line gives its verb 繰り返し plus 大人になって, and line 014 ends the sentence with the question 笑えているかな. Prose: 巡りゆく季節の隅っこで、幾つもの出会いと別れを繰り返し、大人になって、ちゃんと笑えているかな。
- **なって vs なってく.** When the chorus comes back, this line reads 繰り返し 大人になってく (line 035). Here, なって is the te-form of なる. It presents growing up as a change already made, and the sentence runs straight on into line 014 ("having grown up, am I smiling properly?"). なってく is a casual contraction of なっていく. The subsidiary verb いく makes it a change still moving forward ("I go on growing up"). Because く is a terminal form, that version ends its sentence on line 035, and the question on line 036 stands alone.
- On its own, 繰り返し can also be a noun ("repetition") or an adverb ("repeatedly", as in 繰り返し言う). Here the を on line 012 needs a verb, so it is the verb's 連用形. 出会いと別れを繰り返す ("go through meeting and parting again and again") is a stock phrase. 繰り返して would mean the same thing in a more conversational style. Using the bare 連用形 for the first link and the te-form for the second avoids a repetitive 〜て、〜て.
- 大人になる is the everyday way to say "grow up". It is about maturing, not about reaching legal age. The unstated subject is the speaker's 僕 (こんな僕を, line 027).
