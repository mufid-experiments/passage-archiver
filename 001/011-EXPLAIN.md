# Line 011 · 巡りゆく季節の隅っこで

## Context

- Line 010 (previous): ほどけた雪の隙間 咲いた花笑う
- **Line 011 (explained here): 巡りゆく季節の隅っこで**
- Line 012 (next): 幾つもの出会いと別れを

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: place phrase that opens a new sentence; its verb comes on line 013.

The line is grammatical but has no predicate of its own. It is one noun phrase, 巡りゆく季節の隅っこ ("the corner of the circling seasons"), which the particle で turns into a place adverbial. The verb it modifies comes two lines later: 繰り返し on line 013, whose object is line 012 (幾つもの出会いと別れを). Line 010 ends in the terminal form 笑う, so this line starts a new sentence, which runs to line 014. Nothing inside the line is omitted or reordered. The sentence's subject, the speaker (僕), is left unstated, as is normal. The one poetic touch is the image: 隅っこ, a word for a physical corner, is applied to the seasons, that is, to time. Restored prose: 巡りゆく季節の隅っこで、幾つもの出会いと別れを繰り返し、大人になって、ちゃんと笑えているかな。

## Reading

- Kana: めぐりゆく きせつ の すみっこ で
- Romaji: meguriyuku kisetsu no sumikko de

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 巡りゆく | めぐりゆく (meguriyuku) | compound verb (複合動詞) 巡りゆく = 巡る "go round" (godan, 連用形 巡り) + ゆく (行く) "go", conjugating like godan 行く; attributive form (連体形) | "to go on circling". 巡る is used for cycles (季節が巡る "the seasons come round"), and ゆく adds onward movement. Modifies 季節 |
| 2 | 季節 | きせつ (kisetsu) | noun | season(s); plural is unmarked. It is the seasons that circle |
| 3 | の | の (no) | case particle (格助詞) — links two nouns | "of": 季節の隅っこ "a corner of the seasons" |
| 4 | 隅っこ | すみっこ (sumikko) | noun; casual form of 隅 (すみ), with the suffix (接尾語) っこ | corner, nook: a spot off to the side, away from the centre. っこ adds a casual, familiar tone (compare 端っこ "the very edge", 根っこ "root") |
| 5 | で | で (de) | case particle (格助詞) — place of action (動作の場所) | "in, at"; marks the corner as the place where the actions that follow happen, first of all 繰り返し (line 013) |

## How it fits together

`[[巡りゆく 季節] の 隅っこ] で` → (幾つもの出会いと別れを) 繰り返し (line 013)

巡りゆく is a relative clause (連体修飾節) on 季節. Its subject is the head noun itself: the seasons are what circle, so it modifies 季節, not 隅っこ. 季節の隅っこ is "a corner of the seasons", and で makes the whole noun phrase an adverbial of place. A で-phrase modifies a predicate, not a noun (for that it would need での), so it cannot attach to 出会いと別れ on line 012. It waits for the verb 繰り返し on line 013. As the opening phrase, it also sets the scene for 大人になって and the question on line 014.

## Translation

- Literal: "in the corner of the seasons that go on circling"
- Natural: "In a little corner of the circling seasons"

## Notes

- This line opens the chorus (lines 011–018), which holds three sentences: 011–014, 015–016, and 017 with 018 attached by inversion. In the first, this line sets the scene, line 012 gives the object, line 013 the verbs (繰り返し, 大人になって), and line 014 the closing question.
- Line 010 ends in a verb, so in theory this line could be an inverted place phrase (倒置法) for 笑う: "the flowers smile, in a corner of the circling seasons". The forward reading is more natural: modifiers normally come before their verb, and 繰り返し follows on line 013. The chorus also opens this way on line 033, where the line before ends in a を with no main verb, so there the phrase can only look forward.
- **From one spring to many years.** Line 010 shows a single change of season: the snow comes undone and flowers bloom, winter turning into spring. This line widens the view to the whole cycle, in which that spring is one turn among many. The circling seasons stand for the years going by, in which the speaker meets, parts and grows up (lines 012–013). 巡る ("come round again") and 繰り返し ("repeat", line 013) share the idea of recurrence: the seasons come round, and so do meetings and partings.
- **隅 vs 角.** 隅 (すみ) is an inside corner, the part of an enclosed space away from its centre: 部屋の隅 "the corner of a room". An outside corner, such as a street corner or the corner of a desk, is 角 (かど). 隅 is also used for abstract spaces: 心の隅 "a corner of one's heart", 頭の隅 "the back of one's mind". 季節の隅っこ extends this to time. The image is a small spot off to the side while the seasons turn; the lyric says no more than that.
- **Compound ゆく.** 巡りゆく joins ゆく directly to the 連用形, like 過ぎ行く (line 005). 踏みしめてゆく (line 008) goes through the te-form instead. Both are literary; everyday speech would say 巡っていく and 踏みしめていく. As the second part of a compound, ゆく adds onward movement or gradual change: 移りゆく "keep changing", 消えゆく "fade away". Compare 巡り来る (めぐりくる), as in 巡り来る春 "the spring that comes round again": くる brings the seasons toward the speaker, and ゆく sends them on.
- The line returns word for word as line 033. There it follows 君だけがいない世界を (line 032), and its sentence ends at なってく on line 035 (see line 033).
