# Line 012 · 幾つもの出会いと別れを

## Context

- Line 011 (previous): 巡りゆく季節の隅っこで
- **Line 012 (explained here): 幾つもの出会いと別れを**
- Line 013 (next): 繰り返し 大人になって

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: object phrase whose verb 繰り返し is on line 013.

The line is grammatical but has no predicate of its own. It is one noun phrase, 幾つもの出会いと別れ ("many meetings and partings"), followed by the object marker を. The verb that takes it, 繰り返し, opens line 013. Nothing inside the line is omitted, reordered or poetic; the line break simply falls between the object and its verb. Line 011 sets the scene (…隅っこで, "in a corner of …"), and the sentence does not stop on line 013 either: the te-form なって carries it on to the main predicate on line 014. The subject, the speaker (僕), is never stated. Restored prose: 巡りゆく季節の隅っこで、幾つもの出会いと別れを繰り返し、大人になって、ちゃんと笑えているかな。

## Reading

- Kana: いくつ も の であい と わかれ を
- Romaji: ikutsu mo no deai to wakare o

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 幾つ | いくつ (ikutsu) | noun (名詞), interrogative number word (疑問詞) | "how many" (also "how old"); いく "how many" (as in 幾日 いくにち, "how many days") + つ, the native counter of ひとつ, ふたつ. Often written in kana, いくつ |
| 2 | も | も (mo) | binding particle (係助詞) | not "also" here. After a question word with a counter, in an affirmative sentence, it gives "any number of", i.e. "many": 幾つも = "many (of them)". With a negative it means "not many" (幾つもない) |
| 3 | の | の (no) | case particle (格助詞), attributive | lets the quantity 幾つも modify the nouns that follow: 幾つもの出会い "many meetings", like たくさんの人 "a lot of people" |
| 4 | 出会い | であい (deai) | noun (名詞) made from the continuative form (連用形) of the godan verb 出会う (転成名詞) | meeting, encounter. 出会う is "to come across, to meet", often for the first time or by chance (see line 022, また出会える) |
| 5 | と | と (to) | case particle (格助詞) — listing (並立) | "and": joins 出会い and 別れ and names the whole set, just these two. 出会いや別れ would mean "meetings, partings and the like". Not the companion と "with" of line 018 (君と過ごした), which is followed by a verb |
| 6 | 別れ | わかれ (wakare) | noun (名詞) made from the 連用形 of the ichidan (下一段) verb 別れる (転成名詞) | parting, farewell; separating from a person. For things that divide or branch, the verb is written 分かれる (道が分かれる "the road forks") |
| 7 | を | を (o) | case particle (格助詞) — object marker | marks the whole phrase 幾つもの出会いと別れ as the object of 繰り返し at the start of line 013. The line stops before its verb |

## How it fits together

`[[幾つ も] の [出会い と 別れ]] を` → 繰り返し (line 013)

幾つ + も gives the quantity "many", and の lets it modify the noun phrase 出会いと別れ, in which と joins two nouns. The natural reading is that 幾つもの covers both nouns: many meetings and many partings. Grammatically it could cover 出会い alone, but here the two come as a pair. を marks the whole noun phrase as the object of 繰り返し on line 013. The one who goes through them, the speaker (僕), is not stated.

## Translation

- Literal: "any number of meetings and partings (object)…"
- Natural: "…so many meetings and partings…" (the object of 繰り返し, "go through … again and again", on the next line)

## Notes

- Line 010 ends in the terminal form 笑う, so line 011 starts a new sentence, which runs to line 014. Line 011 gives the setting, this line the object, line 013 the verb 繰り返し and then 大人になって, and line 014 the main predicate 笑えているかな. The whole chain has one unstated subject, the speaker.
- **Time.** Neither 繰り返し (連用形) nor なって (te-form) is marked for tense. Both take their time from the main predicate 笑えている, and the chain places them before it. So in this chorus the meetings and partings read as lying behind the speaker, who asks whether, after all of them, they are smiling properly now. The final chorus ends the sentence at なってく instead, which changes this (see line 034).
- **幾つも.** It is いく + the counter つ + も. A number + も stresses a large amount (三つも "as many as three"). Put a question word in place of the number and the amount is left open, so it means "many": 幾つも, 何度も (なんども) "many times", 何人も (なんにんも) "many people". With a negative it means "not many": 残りはもう幾つもない "there aren't many left". Question words without a counter give "every" or "none" instead (どれも "every one of them", 誰もいない "nobody is there"). いくつか is "some, a few".
- A quantity word needs の to modify a noun, as here. It can also float to just before the verb as an adverb: 出会いと別れを幾つも繰り返し means much the same. Numbers work the same way: 三つのりんごを買う / りんごを三つ買う ("buy three apples").
- 出会いと別れ is a set pair. 春は出会いと別れの季節 ("spring is the season of meetings and partings") is a stock phrase in Japan, where graduations fall in March and the new school and work year starts in April. The song is moving from winter into spring (thawing snow on line 010, 春風 on line 015), and this sentence opens with 巡りゆく季節, "the circling seasons" (line 011).
