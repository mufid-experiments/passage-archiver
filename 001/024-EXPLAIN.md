# Line 024 · ここにはいない君を映し出す桜

## Context

- Line 023 (previous): 舞い上がる花びらに微笑む横顔
- **Line 024 (explained here): ここにはいない君を映し出す桜**
- Line 025 (next): 晴れ渡る景色の真ん中で

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — poetic: noun-ending fragment (体言止め)

The line is a grammatical noun phrase with no predicate. It is the second of two 体言止め lines, paired with line 023. It has a relative clause inside a relative clause: ここにはいない modifies 君, and ここにはいない君を映し出す modifies 桜. As a full sentence: 桜が、ここにはいない君を映し出す "The cherry blossoms project you, who aren't here." Cherry blossoms "projecting" an absent person is a metaphor (the blossoms call up 君's image), not a grammatical oddity. Nothing is omitted apart from the predicate a noun ending always lacks.

## Reading

- Kana: ここ に は いない きみ を うつしだす さくら
- Romaji: koko ni wa inai kimi o utsushidasu sakura

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | ここ | ここ (koko) | demonstrative pronoun (指示代名詞) | "here, this place" |
| 2 | に | に (ni) | case particle (格助詞), place of existence | "in, at"; with いる, marks where someone is (or isn't) |
| 3 | は | は (wa) | binding particle (係助詞), contrast | contrastive は: "here, at least"; implies 君 is somewhere else. Very common with negatives |
| 4 | い | い (i) | ichidan verb いる, irrealis form (未然形) | "to be, to exist" (people, animals); the shape いる takes before ない |
| 5 | ない | ない (nai) | auxiliary verb (助動詞) ない, attributive form (連体形) | negation "not"; いない "is not (here)" modifies 君 |
| 6 | 君 | きみ (kimi) | pronoun (代名詞) | "you" (familiar; the standard "you" in lyrics) |
| 7 | を | を (o) | case particle (格助詞), object marker | marks 君 as what is projected |
| 8 | 映し出す | うつしだす (utsushidasu) | godan compound verb (複合動詞) 映し出す, attributive form (連体形) | "to project, to reflect, to bring into view"; 映し (連用形 of 映す "to reflect, to project an image") + 出す "to bring out". Modifies 桜 |
| 9 | 桜 | さくら (sakura) | noun | "cherry blossom(s), cherry tree"; head noun of the whole line |

## How it fits together

`[[[ここ に は い ない] 君] を 映し出す] 桜`

ここにはいない modifies 君 ("you, who aren't here"). That noun phrase, marked by を, is the object of 映し出す. The clause ここにはいない君を映し出す modifies 桜, which is both the head noun and the one doing the projecting (the logical subject of 映し出す). Nothing follows as a predicate.

## Translation

- Literal: "cherry blossoms that project you, who are not here"
- Natural: "the cherry blossoms that show me you, though you're no longer here"

## Notes

- **Second half of the pair:** This line runs parallel to line 023, and both are 体言止め. Read together, the cherry blossoms (024) call up 君, and the smiling profile among the swirling petals (023) can be taken as that projected image.
- **ここには and line 021:** The contrastive は in ここには means "not here, but somewhere else". It echoes line 021's どこか遠い空 "some faraway sky", where the speaker hopes to meet 君 again. ここにはいない is also the usual wording with a negative. ここにいない is grammatical but plainer, with no contrast.
- **映し出す:** 映す means "to reflect, to project (an image)", and 出す adds "bring out into view". Together: "to project (onto a screen), to show clearly". Here it's a metaphor: the blossoms make the absent 君 appear. Don't confuse 映す with 写す "to copy, to photograph".
- **いる vs. ある:** Use いる for people and animals (君) and ある for things.
- **"No longer" comes from context:** いない just means "is not (here)". The song's context implies 君 was once here: 君と過ごした日々 "the days I spent with you" (line 018) and 君だけがいない世界 "a world with only you missing" (line 032).
- **After this line:** Line 025 (晴れ渡る景色の真ん中で) starts a new sentence that ends on line 026 (春風と夢を見ていた). The image pair 023–024 closes here.
