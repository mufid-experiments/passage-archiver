# Line 025 · 晴れ渡る景色の真ん中で

## Context

- Line 024 (previous): ここにはいない君を映し出す桜
- **Line 025 (explained here): 晴れ渡る景色の真ん中で**
- Line 026 (next): 春風と夢を見ていた

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: place phrase whose verb is on line 026.

The line is grammatical but has no predicate of its own: it is a single adverbial phrase ("in the middle of …") ending in the location particle で, and the action it locates — 夢を見ていた "was dreaming" — comes on line 026. Restored as one prose sentence: （僕は）晴れ渡る景色の真ん中で春風と夢を見ていた. Nothing is missing inside the line itself. The one mildly poetic touch is that 晴れ渡る, normally said of the sky (空が晴れ渡る), is applied to the whole 景色 "scenery" — a natural extension, not an error.

## Reading

- Kana: はれわたる けしき の まんなか で
- Romaji: harewataru keshiki no mannaka de

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 晴れ渡る | はれわたる (harewataru) | compound godan verb (複合動詞) 晴れ渡る, attributive form (連体形) | "to be clear all over, cloudless as far as the eye can see"; 晴れ (連用形 of 晴れる "to clear up") + 渡る (as a second element: "across the whole area"); modifies 景色 |
| 2 | 景色 | けしき (keshiki) | noun | scenery, landscape, view |
| 3 | の | の (no) | case particle (格助詞) — links two nouns | "of": 景色の真ん中 "the middle of the scenery" |
| 4 | 真ん中 | まんなか (mannaka) | noun | the very middle, dead centre (from まなか 真中 with an inserted ん) |
| 5 | で | で (de) | case particle (格助詞) — place of action | "in, at"; marks where the action on line 026 (夢を見ていた) takes place |

## How it fits together

`[[晴れ渡る 景色] の 真ん中] で` → 夢を見ていた (line 026)

晴れ渡る is a relative clause describing 景色, and 景色の真ん中 is "the middle of that scenery". で turns the whole noun phrase into an adverbial of place; since a で-phrase can only modify a predicate (to modify a noun it would need での), it attaches to 見ていた on line 026.

## Translation

- Literal: "in the middle of the scenery that is clear all across"
- Natural: "Right in the middle of a landscape under a cloudless sky"

## Notes

- Line 024 closes its own image with a noun (…映し出す桜, 体言止め); line 025 opens a new sentence that ends on line 026: 晴れ渡る景色の真ん中で春風と夢を見ていた. The omitted subject is most naturally the narrator, who calls himself 僕 on line 027 (a less likely reading takes the particle-less 桜 of line 024 as the subject).
- で vs に: で marks where an action takes place (真ん中で夢を見る), に marks where something exists (真ん中にいる). Don't confuse it with line 029's ここはきっとスタートラインで, where で is the continuative form of the copula だ ("is … and").
- 渡る as the second part of a compound verb means "throughout, across the whole area": 響き渡る "to resound throughout", 知れ渡る "to become widely known", 行き渡る "to reach everyone".
- Relative tense (相対テンス): 晴れ渡る is non-past although the main verb 見ていた (line 026) is past. Tense in a relative clause is usually judged against the main clause, so the clear sky is simultaneous with the dreaming. The resultative 晴れ渡った景色 is a common prose alternative.
