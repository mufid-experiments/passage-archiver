# Line 035 · 繰り返し 大人になってく

## Context

- Line 034 (previous): 幾つもの出会いと別れを
- **Line 035 (explained here): 繰り返し 大人になってく**
- Line 036 (next): ちゃんと笑えているかな

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: takes its object from line 034 and ends the sentence (colloquial てく = ていく).

The line is grammatical and has the same shape as line 013. 繰り返し is the continuative form (連用形) of 繰り返す, used to join clauses (連用中止法). It takes line 034 (幾つもの出会いと別れを) as its object, and the subject 僕 "I" is not stated. The difference is the ending: なってく is the casual contraction of なっていく (te-form なって + subsidiary verb いく with its い dropped). く is a terminal form, so the sentence ends here instead of running on into line 036. Restored prose: 幾つもの出会いと別れを繰り返し、大人になっていく。 The contraction is ordinary casual speech, not an error. The space marks a pause where prose would put a comma.

## Reading

- Kana: くりかえし おとな に なってく
- Romaji: kurikaeshi otona ni natteku

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 繰り返し | くりかえし (kurikaeshi) | godan verb 繰り返す, continuative form (連用形) used as a clause link (連用中止法) | to repeat; compound of 繰る (to reel, wind) + 返す (back, again); takes 幾つもの出会いと別れを (line 034) as its object: "(I) repeat …, and" |
| 2 | 大人 | おとな (otona) | noun; special whole-word reading (熟字訓) | adult, grown-up (the other readings, たいじん and だいにん, belong to other uses) |
| 3 | に | に (ni) | case particle (格助詞) | marks the result of a change: 〜になる = "become ~" |
| 4 | なっ | なっ (nat-) | godan verb なる, continuative form (連用形) with sound change to small っ (促音便) before て | to become |
| 5 | て | て (te) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞) | te-form; joins なる to the subsidiary verb いく |
| 6 | く | く (ku) | subsidiary verb (補助動詞) いく (行く), contracted (ていく → てく), terminal form (終止形) | "go on ~ing": the change keeps moving forward from now on; ends the sentence |

## How it fits together

`[(幾つもの出会いと別れを) 繰り返し]、 [[大人 に] なっ て く]。`

This is the same two-clause chain as line 013, with the unstated subject 僕 "I". Clause 1 (the object from line 034 + 繰り返し) leads into clause 2, 大人に + なっていく. Clause 2 now ends in the terminal form く, so the sentence closes at the end of this line.

## Translation

- Literal: "(many meetings and partings) repeating, (I) go on becoming an adult."
- Natural: "…I go through (so many meetings and partings) again and again, and keep on growing up."

## Notes

- In the final chorus, the sentence runs from line 033 (巡りゆく季節の隅っこで) through line 034 (the object of 繰り返し) and ends here. Prose: 巡りゆく季節の隅っこで、幾つもの出会いと別れを繰り返し、大人になっていく。 Line 036, ちゃんと笑えているかな, is then a separate sentence.
- **なってく vs なって.** In the first chorus this line reads 繰り返し 大人になって (line 013). That なって is a te-form. It presents growing up as a change already made, and the sentence keeps going into line 014's question ("having grown up, am I smiling properly?"). なってく is non-past and adds the subsidiary verb いく, "go on ~ing". The change is still under way and heading into the future ("I keep growing up"), and the verb closes its sentence. The shift fits the forward-looking lines just before this chorus (ここはきっとスタートラインで … 新しい世界, lines 029–031).
- 〜ていく vs 〜てくる: なっていく looks forward from now ("go on becoming"). なってきた looks back up to now ("has gradually become"). Don't confuse てく with てる: なってる is short for なっている ("has become, is"), which is a different form.
- Dropping the い (ていく → てく) is casual speech. Elsewhere the lyrics use the full, more literary ゆく (巡りゆく on line 033, 踏みしめてゆく on line 008). Because the verb is non-past, the line can also be read as a general truth: "this is how one grows up."
