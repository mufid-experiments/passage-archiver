# Line 027 · こんな僕をたぶん君は

## Context

- Line 026 (previous): 春風と夢を見ていた
- **Line 027 (explained here): こんな僕をたぶん君は**
- Line 028 (next): 「情けないな」って笑うだろう

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: object and topic; the verb 笑うだろう is on line 028.

Grammatical, but not a sentence on its own: the line holds an object (こんな僕を), an adverb (たぶん) and a topic (君は), and the predicate they all depend on — 「情けないな」って笑うだろう — only arrives on line 028. The object comes before the topic; Japanese allows this reordering freely (scrambling), and here it puts こんな僕 up front for emphasis. The neutral prose order would be 君はたぶん、こんな僕を「情けないな」って笑うだろう. Nothing is omitted and nothing is ungrammatical.

## Reading

- Kana: こんな ぼく を たぶん きみ は
- Romaji: konna boku o tabun kimi wa

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | こんな | こんな (konna) | pre-noun adjectival (連体詞), こ-series demonstrative | "this kind of, like this"; modifies 僕. Before a first-person pronoun it is usually self-deprecating: "someone like me" |
| 2 | 僕 | ぼく (boku) | pronoun (代名詞), first person | I, me (the narrator) |
| 3 | を | を (o) | case particle (格助詞) — object marker | marks こんな僕 as the object of 笑う on line 028: 僕を笑う "to laugh at me" |
| 4 | たぶん | たぶん (tabun) | adverb (副詞) | probably (kanji 多分); anticipates the conjectural だろう at the end of line 028 |
| 5 | 君 | きみ (kimi) | pronoun (代名詞), second person | you (familiar) |
| 6 | は | は (wa) | binding particle (係助詞) — topic marker | makes 君 the topic; 君 is also the one who does the laughing on line 028 |

## How it fits together

`[[こんな 僕] を] [たぶん] [君 は] … 「情けないな」って 笑う だろう (line 028)`

こんな modifies 僕, and こんな僕を is the object of 笑う. 君は is the topic and in effect the subject ("you" are the one who laughs); たぶん covers the whole predicate and pairs with だろう.

## Translation

- Literal: "me like this (object) — probably — you (topic) …"
- Natural: "Me, the way I am now — you'd probably …" (completed on line 028: "You'd probably laugh at me, the way I am now, and say, 'Man, how pathetic.'")

## Notes

- The sentence begins here and ends on line 028: こんな僕をたぶん君は「情けないな」って笑うだろう. Line 026 is already a finished sentence (…夢を見ていた), so this を does not look back to 見ていた; it belongs to 笑う on the next line.
- こんな most naturally points to the narrator as just portrayed — dreaming in the spring scene, still seeing the absent 君 in the cherry blossoms (lines 023–026). It belongs to the こんな / そんな / あんな / どんな series and is usually listed as a 連体詞 (some grammars treat it as the attributive of the na-adjective こんなだ).
- たぶん … だろう is a standard correlation (呼応): adverbs of conjecture (たぶん, きっと, おそらく) are typically answered by だろう / でしょう at the end of the clause. Because 君 is "not here" (line 024), the narrator can only guess at the reaction.
- Word order: putting the object first foregrounds "me, like this" before the person judging it, and たぶん floats in the middle as it often does in speech. This is ordinary reordering before the verb, not 倒置法 (which moves material after the predicate).
