# Line 034 · 幾つもの出会いと別れを

## Context

- Line 033 (previous): 巡りゆく季節の隅っこで
- **Line 034 (explained here): 幾つもの出会いと別れを**
- Line 035 (next): 繰り返し 大人になってく

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: object phrase whose verb 繰り返し is on line 035.

The line is grammatical but has no predicate of its own. It is one noun phrase, 幾つもの出会いと別れ ("many meetings and partings"), followed by the object marker を. The verb that takes it, 繰り返し, opens line 035. Nothing inside the line is omitted, reordered or poetic. The words are the same as line 012, but the sentence around them is shorter this time: it starts on line 033 and ends at the terminal form なってく on line 035, so line 036 is a question of its own. Unlike the を of line 032, which never gets a verb, this を is completed on the very next line. The subject, the speaker (僕), is never stated. Restored prose: 巡りゆく季節の隅っこで、幾つもの出会いと別れを繰り返し、大人になっていく。

## Reading

- Kana: いくつ も の であい と わかれ を
- Romaji: ikutsu mo no deai to wakare o

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 幾つ | いくつ (ikutsu) | noun (名詞), interrogative number word (疑問詞) | "how many" (also "how old"); いく "how many" (as in 幾日 いくにち, "how many days") + つ, the native counter of ひとつ, ふたつ. Often written in kana, いくつ |
| 2 | も | も (mo) | binding particle (係助詞) | not "also" here. After a question word with a counter, in an affirmative sentence, it gives "any number of", i.e. "many": 幾つも = "many (of them)". With a negative it means "not many" (幾つもない) |
| 3 | の | の (no) | case particle (格助詞), attributive | lets the quantity 幾つも modify the nouns that follow: 幾つもの出会い "many meetings", like たくさんの人 "a lot of people" |
| 4 | 出会い | であい (deai) | noun (名詞) made from the continuative form (連用形) of the godan verb 出会う (転成名詞) | meeting, encounter. 出会う is "to come across, to meet", often for the first time or by chance (see line 022, また出会える) |
| 5 | と | と (to) | case particle (格助詞) — listing (並立) | "and": joins 出会い and 別れ and names the whole set, just these two. 出会いや別れ would mean "meetings, partings and the like". Not the companion と "with" of line 040 (君と過ごした), which is followed by a verb |
| 6 | 別れ | わかれ (wakare) | noun (名詞) made from the 連用形 of the ichidan (下一段) verb 別れる (転成名詞) | parting, farewell; separating from a person. For things that divide or branch, the verb is written 分かれる (道が分かれる "the road forks") |
| 7 | を | を (o) | case particle (格助詞) — object marker | marks the whole phrase 幾つもの出会いと別れ as the object of 繰り返し at the start of line 035. The line stops before its verb |

## How it fits together

`[[幾つ も] の [出会い と 別れ]] を` → 繰り返し (line 035)

幾つ + も gives the quantity "many", and の lets it modify the noun phrase 出会いと別れ, in which と joins two nouns. The natural reading is that 幾つもの covers both nouns: many meetings and many partings. Grammatically it could cover 出会い alone, but here the two come as a pair. を marks the whole noun phrase as the object of 繰り返し on line 035. The one who goes through them, the speaker (僕), is not stated.

## Translation

- Literal: "any number of meetings and partings (object)…"
- Natural: "…so many meetings and partings…" (the object of 繰り返し, "go through … again and again", on the next line)

## Notes

- Lines 033–040 repeat the first chorus (011–018) word for word, except that line 035 has 大人になってく where line 013 has 大人になって. That extra く (the terminal form of contracted いく) closes the sentence on line 035. So in this chorus the sentence covers lines 033–035 only, and line 036 (ちゃんと笑えているかな) is a separate question. In the first chorus the same line sat inside a four-line sentence (lines 011–014).
- **Time.** 繰り返し is not marked for tense. It takes its time from the verb that ends the sentence, here the non-past なってく, "go on becoming". So the meetings and partings read as an ongoing cycle that continues from now on: "I go through so many meetings and partings, and keep on growing up." In the first chorus the same words led into 笑えているかな and looked back on them (see line 012).
- In this chorus the sentence also begins and ends with the same verb, 行く: 巡りゆく on line 033 (巡る + literary ゆく) and なってく on line 035 (なって + いく, contracted). This line sits between them. The seasons keep circling, the meetings and partings keep repeating, and the speaker keeps growing up.
- **After line 032.** This chorus comes straight after lines 029–032: this is surely the starting line, and what waits beyond the step is 新しい世界, 君だけがいない世界 ("a new world, a world where only you are missing"). Heard after that, the partings can include the one from 君, and the meetings still ahead will take place in a world without 君. The middle of the song has told of both: a parting (神様は僕らを切り離して, "God cut us apart", line 019) and a hoped-for meeting (また出会えるから, "because we can meet again", line 022, with 出会う, the verb behind 出会い).
- Line 032's を-phrase cannot be the object of 繰り返し. That verb already has its を-phrase on this line, and Japanese does not normally let one verb take two を-phrases (the "double-o constraint", 二重ヲ格制約). The cross-boundary hearing that line 032 mentions is with 巡りゆく on line 033, not with this verb.
