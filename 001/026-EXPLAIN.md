# Line 026 · 春風と夢を見ていた

## Context

- Line 025 (previous): 晴れ渡る景色の真ん中で
- **Line 026 (explained here): 春風と夢を見ていた**
- Line 027 (next): こんな僕をたぶん君は

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — completes the sentence begun on line 025 (subject omitted).

Grammatical and complete: the line has a companion (春風と), an object (夢を) and a sentence-final predicate (見ていた), with the place of the action supplied by line 025 (…真ん中で). The only unstated element is the subject — most naturally the narrator, 僕 — and dropping a subject that context makes clear is ordinary Japanese, not a poetic liberty. Full prose sentence: （僕は）晴れ渡る景色の真ん中で春風と夢を見ていた. What is poetic is the personification: the spring breeze is treated as someone you can dream *with*.

## Reading

- Kana: はるかぜ と ゆめ を みて いた
- Romaji: harukaze to yume o mite ita

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 春風 | はるかぜ (harukaze) | noun | spring breeze; the Sino-Japanese reading しゅんぷう (shunpū) exists but is literary, so はるかぜ is the likely sung reading |
| 2 | と | と (to) | case particle (格助詞) — companion | "with, together with"; marks 春風 as the partner in the dreaming |
| 3 | 夢 | ゆめ (yume) | noun | dream |
| 4 | を | を (o) | case particle (格助詞) — object marker | marks 夢 as the object; 夢を見る = "to dream" (lit. "to see a dream") |
| 5 | 見 | み (mi) | ichidan verb 見る, continuative form (連用形) | to see; with 夢を, "to dream" |
| 6 | て | て (te) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞) | makes the te-form 見て, which connects to the subsidiary verb いる |
| 7 | い | い (i) | subsidiary verb (補助動詞) いる, continuative form (連用形) | progressive aspect: 見ている "be dreaming" |
| 8 | た | た (ta) | auxiliary verb (助動詞) た, terminal form (終止形) | past tense: 見ていた "was dreaming" |

## How it fits together

`(僕は) (晴れ渡る景色の真ん中で) [春風 と] [夢 を] [見 て い た]`

春風と (with whom) and 夢を (what) both depend on the predicate 見ていた; the place phrase comes from line 025 and the subject is left unstated. The sentence ends here.

## Translation

- Literal: "(I) was seeing a dream together with the spring breeze"
- Natural: "I was dreaming along with the spring breeze" (with line 025: "Right in the middle of a landscape under a cloudless sky, I was dreaming along with the spring breeze")

## Notes

- The sentence runs over lines 025–026 and ends with 見ていた. Line 027 starts a new one in which the narrator refers to himself as 僕 (こんな僕を…); its こんな "like this" most naturally points back to the dreaming self shown here. A less likely reading takes the particle-less 桜 that ends line 024 as the subject ("the cherry tree was dreaming with the spring breeze"), but line 024 reads as a self-contained noun-ending image, parallel to line 023.
- と is best read as the companion "with", personifying the breeze. A listing "and" reading — [春風と夢]を見ていた "was watching the spring breeze and a dream" — is grammatically possible but unlikely: 夢を見る is a set phrase, and wind is not normally something you "see".
- 春風 also appears on line 015 (頬を掠める春風が): the breeze grazing the cheek feels somehow nostalgic and the narrator turns around. Here it has become his companion.
- 夢を見る covers both a sleeping dream and a waking reverie; since line 024 has the cherry blossoms "projecting" the absent 君, a daydream is the natural reading. Don't confuse it with the single verb 夢見る (ゆめみる) "to dream of, to long for".
- ていた is past progressive, "was (in the middle of) dreaming". It frames the dream as a state the narrator was inside and is now looking back on, which leads into the self-deprecating こんな僕 of line 027.
