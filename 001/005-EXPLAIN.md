# Line 005 · 改札過ぎ行く人のように

## Context

- Line 004 (previous): 包み込む
- **Line 005 (explained here): 改札過ぎ行く人のように**
- Line 006 (next): 流されるように生きて

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: particle を omitted; the simile continues on line 006.

Grammatical, but not a sentence by itself. 〜のように is an adverbial phrase ("like…"), so it needs a verb to modify, and that verb only arrives on line 006 (流される… 生きて). The object particle を after 改札 is dropped; the prose version is 改札を過ぎ行く人のように. Leaving out を before a verb is common in speech and lyrics, so this is a stylistic omission, not an error.

## Reading

- Kana: かいさつ すぎゆく ひと の ように
- Romaji: kaisatsu sugiyuku hito no yō ni

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 改札 | かいさつ (kaisatsu) | noun | ticket gate(s) at a station (also 改札口 かいさつぐち) |
| – | （を） | を (o) | case particle (格助詞) — **omitted** | would mark 改札 as the point passed through: 改札を過ぎる "go through the gates" |
| 2 | 過ぎ行く | すぎゆく (sugiyuku) | compound verb (複合動詞) 過ぎ行く = 過ぎる "pass" (連用形 過ぎ) + 行く "go", conjugating like godan 行く; attributive form (連体形) | "to pass by and move on"; 改札（を）過ぎ行く is a relative clause modifying 人 |
| 3 | 人 | ひと (hito) | noun | person / people (plural is unmarked; here, the people going through the gates) |
| 4 | の | の (no) | case particle (格助詞), attributive の | links the noun 人 to ようだ (nouns take の before ようだ) |
| 5 | ように | ように (yō ni) | auxiliary verb (助動詞) ようだ, continuative form (連用形) | "like…, in the same way as…"; makes the phrase an adverb of manner for the verb on line 006 |

## How it fits together

`[[改札（を） 過ぎ行く] 人] の ように` → 流される / 生きて (line 006)

改札（を）過ぎ行く describes 人: "people who pass through the ticket gates". 人のように then acts as an adverb of manner. It says how the unstated subject (the speaker, 僕, who appears at line 027) has lived, and line 006 completes it: 流されるように生きて.

## Translation

- Literal: "like people passing (through) the ticket gates"
- Natural: "like the people streaming through the ticket gates"

## Notes

- Line 004 closed the previous sentence (凍えた両手をそっと包み込む), so a new one starts here. It runs over four lines and ends on line 008: **改札（を）過ぎ行く人のように** / 流されるように生きて / 今初めて自分で選ぶ / 道（を）踏みしめてゆく. Prose: 改札を過ぎ行く人のように流されるように生きて、今初めて自分で選ぶ道を踏みしめてゆく。 "I've lived as if swept along, like the people streaming through the ticket gates, and now, for the first time, I walk on with firm steps along a path of my own choosing."
- This line gives that sentence its comparison. The crowd flowing through the gates sets up line 006's 流される ("to be swept along by a current"). The speaker describes their life until now as that kind of passive drifting.
- 人のように can be read as modifying 流される ("swept along, as those people are") or as a second manner phrase for 生きて beside 流されるように. The meaning is the same either way.
- Line 004 ends in a verb, so 人のように could in theory be an inverted modifier (倒置法) of 包み込む ("I wrap my hands, like the people passing the gates"). The paired 〜のように / 〜ように and the "flow" image make the link to line 006 much more natural.
- Reading: the compound 過ぎ行く is normally read すぎゆく. The song writes ゆく in kana elsewhere (踏みしめてゆく, 巡りゆく), so すぎゆく is the likely sung reading. すぎいく is possible but rare.
- Grammar point: ようだ takes の after nouns (人のように) but attaches directly to verbs (流されるように). Use ような before a noun and ように before a verb or adjective.
