# Line 019 · 神様は僕らを切り離して

## Context

- Line 018 (previous): 君と過ごした日々を胸に
- **Line 019 (explained here): 神様は僕らを切り離して**
- Line 020 (next): 別々の線を描いた

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: continues on line 020

The line is grammatical, and nothing is omitted inside it: topic and subject 神様は, object 僕らを, verb 切り離して. It is not a complete sentence, though. The te-form (て形) leaves it open, and the main predicate 描いた arrives on line 020. The full prose sentence is 神様は僕らを切り離して、別々の線を描いた。 This is category (b): a grammatical fragment, with one sentence split across two lines.

## Reading

- Kana: かみさま は ぼくら を きりはなして
- Romaji: kamisama wa bokura o kirihanashite

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 神様 | かみさま (kamisama) | noun: 神 + honorific suffix 様 | God, the gods; here fate personified |
| 2 | は | は (wa) | binding particle (係助詞), topic marker | marks 神様 as the topic, and as the doer of both 切り離す and 描く (line 020) |
| 3 | 僕ら | ぼくら (bokura) | pronoun: 僕 + plural suffix ら | we, us: the narrator (僕) and 君 |
| 4 | を | を (o) | case particle (格助詞), object marker | marks 僕ら as what is cut apart |
| 5 | 切り離し | きりはなし (kirihanashi) | godan verb 切り離す, continuative form (連用形); compound of 切る "cut" (連用形 切り) + 離す "separate" | to cut off, to sever, to separate something that was joined |
| 6 | て | て (te) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞), te-form | "…and (then)": chains this action to 描いた on line 020 |

## How it fits together

`神様 は [[僕ら を] 切り離し て] → (line 020) [[[別々 の] 線] を 描い た]`

神様は is the topic and subject of both verbs in the two-line sentence, and 僕らを is the object of 切り離して. The te-form joins two actions in sequence: God first cut us apart, then drew separate lines.

## Translation

- Literal: "God (topic) cut us apart, and…"
- Natural: "God cut the two of us apart, and…"

## Notes

- Line 018 (君と過ごした日々を胸に) belongs with line 017 (もう迷わない), roughly "I won't waver any more, (holding) the days I spent with you in my heart". It closes that section and does not connect grammatically to 神様は. Line 019 starts a new sentence with its own topic, which finishes on line 020.
- A te-form clause has no tense of its own; it takes the tense of the final verb. Because line 020 ends in the past tense 描いた, 切り離して means "cut apart" (past), not "cuts apart".
- 切り離す implies that the two were once joined as one piece and were cut off from each other. It is the verb for detaching parts or uncoupling train cars (車両を切り離す). That makes it stronger than 離す "separate" or 別れる "part". Making 神様 the one who cuts presents the separation as fate, beyond the pair's control.
- 神様 is the ordinary, slightly familiar way to say "God" or "the gods" in songs and everyday speech. It doesn't necessarily point to a particular religion.
- 僕ら is a casual, boyish "we". The plural suffix ら is less formal than たち (僕たち), and here it includes 君: "you and I".
