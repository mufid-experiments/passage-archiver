# Line 037 · 頬を掠める春風が

## Context

- Line 036 (previous): ちゃんと笑えているかな
- **Line 037 (explained here): 頬を掠める春風が**
- Line 038 (next): どこか懐かしくて振り向いた

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: subject phrase whose predicate is on line 038.

The line is grammatical but not a sentence by itself. It is a single noun phrase, 頬を掠める春風 ("the spring breeze that grazes my cheek"), followed by the subject marker が. Its predicate comes on line 038. The words and the grammar are the same as line 015. Line 036 ends its own sentence with かな, so this line starts a new one, and that sentence ends with 振り向いた on line 038. Nothing inside the line is dropped, reordered or poetic. The only thing left unsaid is whose cheek it is (the speaker's), which is normal in Japanese. Restored prose: （僕は）頬を掠める春風がどこか懐かしくて振り向いた。

## Reading

- Kana: ほほ を かすめる はるかぜ が
- Romaji: hoho o kasumeru harukaze ga

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 頬 | ほほ (hoho); standard ほお (hō) | noun | cheek |
| 2 | を | を (o) | case particle (格助詞) — object marker | marks 頬 as what gets grazed |
| 3 | 掠める | かすめる (kasumeru) | ichidan (下一段) verb 掠める, attributive form (連体形) | to graze, to brush lightly past; the clause 頬を掠める modifies 春風 |
| 4 | 春風 | はるかぜ (harukaze) | noun, compound of 春 "spring" + 風 "wind" | spring breeze |
| 5 | が | が (ga) | case particle (格助詞) — subject marker | marks the whole phrase 頬を掠める春風 as the subject of 懐かしくて on line 038 |

## How it fits together

- This line: `[[頬 を 掠める] 春風] が`
- Whole sentence: `[[[頬 を 掠める] 春風] が どこか 懐かしくて] (僕 は) 振り向いた`

頬を掠める is a relative clause (連体修飾節) on 春風, and its subject is the head noun itself: the breeze is what grazes the cheek. The whole phrase is then the が-subject of 懐かしくて on line 038. The last verb, 振り向いた, has a different subject that is never stated: the speaker (僕), not the breeze. As on line 015, nothing from the line before attaches here.

## Translation

- Literal: "the spring breeze that grazes (my) cheek…" (が marks it as the subject of what follows)
- Natural: "The spring breeze brushing past my cheek…" (line 038 completes it: "…felt somehow nostalgic, and I turned around.")

## Notes

- Lines 037–038 repeat lines 015–016 word for word. The final chorus differs from the first only at line 035 (大人になってく for 大人になって), and that change does not reach this sentence. Line 036 ends with the sentence-final particle (終助詞) かな ("I wonder"), as line 014 does, so this line starts a new sentence in both choruses. What differs is the sentence before it. Line 014 closes the long sentence of lines 011–014, but line 036 is a question on its own, because line 035 already ended its sentence with the terminal form なってく. The new sentence ends with 振り向いた on line 038, and line 039 (もう迷わない) starts the next one.
- Subject of each predicate in 頬を掠める春風がどこか懐かしくて振り向いた:
  - 掠める → 春風. The breeze brushes the cheek.
  - 懐かしくて → 春風, marked by が. With feeling adjectives (感情形容詞) such as 懐かしい, が marks what triggers the feeling. The person who feels it goes unsaid; here that is the speaker.
  - 振り向いた → the unstated speaker, 僕 (line 027 こんな僕を). A breeze cannot turn around, so after the reason clause 〜くて the subject switches to the one who felt the nostalgia.
- Why が and not は: a が-subject stays inside the 〜くて clause. 春風は would tend to be read as the topic of the whole sentence, and so of 振り向いた too. This が follows a noun, so it is the subject marker. The conjunction が ("but") attaches to a predicate instead (〜だが, 〜ますが).
- 春風 appears three times in the song: line 015, line 026 (春風と夢を見ていた, "I was dreaming along with the spring breeze") and here, so in the final chorus it comes after that dream. The particle shows its role each time: と marks a companion on line 026, and が marks the subject on lines 015 and 037.
- 掠める is non-past although the sentence ends in the past. Tense in a relative clause is usually judged against the main clause, so the breeze is brushing the cheek at the moment of turning ("the breeze that was brushing my cheek"). The past 頬を掠めた春風 would also be natural, but it presents the touch as already over.
- Readings: dictionaries list 頬 under ほお, the only reading in the 常用漢字表. ほほ is a very common variant, especially in songs. The kana above follows line 015 in using ほほ, but only the recording can settle which is sung. 春風 can also be read しゅんぷう (literary), but はるかぜ is the natural reading in lyrics. There is no rendaku (not はるがぜ), because かぜ already contains a 濁音 (ぜ), and that blocks it (Lyman's law, ライマンの法則). Compare 青空 (あおぞら), where そら has no 濁音 and becomes ぞら.
- 掠める describes a light touch in passing, and 風が頬を掠める is a common phrase. It is also used for thoughts that flash through the mind (嫌な予感が脳裏を掠める, "a bad feeling flashes through my mind"). Another sense, "to pilfer" (掠め取る, "to snatch, skim off"), does not apply here. 掠 is not one of the 常用漢字, so the verb is often written in kana, かすめる.
