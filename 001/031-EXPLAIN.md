# Line 031 · 新しい世界

## Context

- Line 030 (previous): 踏み出した先に待つのは
- **Line 031 (explained here): 新しい世界**
- Line 032 (next): 君だけがいない世界を

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — poetic: noun-ending fragment (体言止め); completes line 030, copula omitted.

This is (b): grammatical, but a fragment. On its own the line is a bare noun phrase with no predicate. It is the X of the cleft sentence begun on line 030 (〜のは X だ), with the final copula dropped: 踏み出した先に待つのは新しい世界（だ）. Dropping だ after a sentence-final noun is ordinary in casual speech, headlines and captions, and ending a line on a noun (体言止め) is a stock device in lyrics, so this is natural, not broken. Inside the phrase the grammar is a plain adjective + noun.

## Reading

- Kana: あたらしい せかい
- Romaji: atarashii sekai

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 新しい | あたらしい (atarashii) | i-adjective 新しい, attributive form (連体形; same shape as the dictionary form) | "new"; modifies 世界 |
| 2 | 世界 | せかい (sekai) | noun | world |

## How it fits together

`(line 030: 踏み出した先に待つのは) [新しい 世界]（だ）`

新しい modifies 世界, and the noun phrase is the predicate (the focus) of the cleft from line 030: "what awaits beyond the step is a new world". In plain word order it is also the subject of 待つ (新しい世界が待っている). The copula that would close the sentence (だ; polite です; written である) is left unspoken.

## Translation

- Literal: "(is) a new world"
- Natural (with line 030): "…what awaits beyond that step is a new world."

## Notes

- The sentence that began on line 029 ends here: ここはきっとスタートラインで、踏み出した先に待つのは新しい世界（だ）。 "I'm sure this is the starting line, and what awaits beyond the step is a new world."
- Line 032 immediately re-describes the same thing with the same head noun: 新しい世界 → 君だけがいない世界を. The "new world" turns out to be a world where only "you" are missing. Line 032 switches to を, so it is not a simple apposition; see line 032 for what that を hangs on.
- Leaving out だ is not only a lyric device: 「待ってるのは新しい世界。」 sounds perfectly natural in casual speech. Careful prose would add だ／である, and polite speech です.
- 体言止め gives a clipped ending and leaves the noun ringing. Here that noun is the answer to line 030's "what awaits…?"
- い-adjectives modify nouns directly, in the same form as the dictionary form (新しい世界); no な or の is needed (compare the な-adjective 静かな世界).
