# Line 014 · ちゃんと笑えているかな

## Context

- Line 013 (previous): 繰り返し 大人になって
- **Line 014 (explained here): ちゃんと笑えているかな**
- Line 015 (next): 頬を掠める春風が

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — complete sentence (subject omitted); ends the sentence begun on line 011.

The line is grammatical and complete on its own: an adverb, a potential verb in the ている form, and the sentence-final particles かな. Only the subject 僕 "I" is left out, which is normal in Japanese. In this chorus it is also the main clause for lines 011–013, because the te-form なって at the end of line 013 attaches to 笑えている. Prose: 巡りゆく季節の隅っこで、幾つもの出会いと別れを繰り返し、大人になって、（僕は）ちゃんと笑えているかな。 The register is casual (ちゃんと, かな). Nothing is poetic or unusual.

## Reading

- Kana: ちゃんと わらえて いる かな
- Romaji: chanto waraete iru kana

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | ちゃんと | ちゃんと (chanto) | adverb (副詞) | properly, the way it should be (casual); modifies 笑えている |
| 2 | 笑え | わらえ (warae) | godan verb 笑う → potential verb 笑える (可能動詞, conjugates like an ichidan verb), continuative form (連用形) before て | to be able to smile / laugh |
| 3 | て | て (te) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞) | te-form; joins 笑え to the subsidiary verb いる |
| 4 | いる | いる (iru) | subsidiary verb (補助動詞) いる, terminal form (終止形) | 〜ている: a continuing state — "am (currently) managing to smile" |
| 5 | か | か (ka) | sentence-final particle (終助詞) | question / doubt |
| 6 | な | な (na) | sentence-final particle (終助詞) | musing, talking to oneself; か + な = "I wonder (if)…" |

## How it fits together

`(line 013: …大人に なって →) [ちゃんと [笑え て いる]] か な`

ちゃんと modifies 笑えている, and か + な turn the whole clause into a self-directed question. The subject 僕 "I" is unstated. In this first chorus, 笑えている is also the main predicate that the te-form なって on line 013 attaches to.

## Translation

- Literal: "(am I) properly being able to smile, I wonder"
- Natural: "(…having grown up,) am I smiling properly now, I wonder?"

## Notes

- This line closes the sentence that began on line 011. Line 013 ends in the te-form なって, so the growing up leads straight into the question: "now that I've grown up through all those meetings and partings, am I smiling properly?"
- Line 015 (頬を掠める春風が, "the spring breeze brushing my cheek") starts a new sentence with its own subject, marked by が. Nothing from this line carries over.
- 笑える is the potential form of 笑う ("can smile"). Adding ている turns an ability into an actual, present state: 笑えている = "am managing to smile (these days)". Compare 笑えるかな, "I wonder if I'll be able to smile", which looks ahead instead. (笑える can also mean "funny", as in それ笑える, but not here.)
- 笑う covers both "laugh" and "smile". Here it means smiling, being all right. ちゃんと suggests a real smile rather than a forced one.
- かな is casual and mostly self-directed: the speaker is musing rather than asking anyone for an answer.
- The same words come back as line 036. There, line 035 ends in the sentence-final なってく, so the question stands on its own instead of completing a te-form chain.
