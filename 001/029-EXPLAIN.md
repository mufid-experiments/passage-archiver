# Line 029 · ここはきっとスタートラインで

## Context

- Line 030 (next): 踏み出した先に待つのは

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: continues on line 030.

This is (b): grammatical, but a fragment. The line is a complete topic–comment clause (ここは … スタートライン), but the copula is in its continuative form で ("is …, and") instead of a final だ, so the sentence carries on into lines 030–031. Nothing inside the line is omitted or reordered. Restored prose: ここはきっとスタートラインで、踏み出した先に待つのは新しい世界だ。

## Reading

- Kana: ここ は きっと スタートライン で
- Romaji: koko wa kitto sutātorain de

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | ここ | ここ (koko) | demonstrative pronoun (指示代名詞), こ-series | "here, this place"; figuratively, this point in the singer's life |
| 2 | は | は (wa) | binding particle (係助詞) — topic marker | makes ここ the topic: "as for here…" |
| 3 | きっと | きっと (kitto) | adverb (副詞) of conviction | "surely, I'm sure"; the speaker's strong belief; qualifies the predicate スタートラインで |
| 4 | スタートライン | スタートライン (sutātorain) | noun, loanword (外来語) from English "start line" | starting line; figuratively, a new beginning |
| 5 | で | で (de) | copula だ (断定の助動詞), continuative form (連用形; te-form) | "is …, and"; closes this clause and links it to the next one |

## How it fits together

`[ここ は] [きっと [スタートライン で]] → line 030`

ここ is the topic (and subject), and スタートライン + copula で is its predicate: "here is the starting line". きっと qualifies that predicate. Because the copula is continuative, this clause is the first half of an "A, and B" sentence, and B is 踏み出した先に待つのは新しい世界 (lines 030–031).

## Translation

- Literal: "Here is surely the starting line, and…"
- Natural: "I'm sure this is the starting line, and…"

## Notes

- A new sentence starts here. The previous one closed on line 028 with だろう (こんな僕をたぶん君は「情けないな」って笑うだろう). This one runs to line 031: ここはきっとスタートラインで、踏み出した先に待つのは新しい世界（だ）. Line 032 then adds a を-phrase whose verb is never sung.
- で is the copula, equivalent to であり／であって (ここはスタートラインであり、…). It is not the case particle で "at, by". Don't read across the line break as スタートラインで踏み出した "stepped out at the starting line": ここは still needs a predicate, and スタートライン(で) is that predicate.
- The te-form of the copula mainly sets two statements side by side ("this is the start" + "what lies beyond is a new world"). It can also carry a loose "and so", as in 今日は日曜日で、店が混んでいる "it's Sunday, so the shops are crowded".
- きっと expresses strong conviction ("surely, I bet"). It often pairs with だろう or はず but can stand alone, as here. Compare たぶん "probably" on line 027, which is weaker.
- スタートラインに立つ ("to stand at the starting line") is a common set phrase for a fresh start. The image sets up 踏み出した "stepped forward" on the next line.
