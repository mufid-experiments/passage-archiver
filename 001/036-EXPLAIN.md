# Line 036 · ちゃんと笑えているかな

## Context

- Line 035 (previous): 繰り返し 大人になってく
- **Line 036 (explained here): ちゃんと笑えているかな**
- Line 037 (next): 頬を掠める春風が

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — complete sentence (subject omitted); stands on its own after line 035.

The line is grammatical and complete: an adverb, a potential verb in the ている form, and the sentence-final particles かな. Only the subject 僕 "I" is left out, which is normal in Japanese. The words are the same as line 014, but the link is different. Line 035 ends in the terminal form なってく, so this question is a separate sentence that follows the statement "I keep growing up". It is not the main clause of a te-form chain. Prose: …大人になっていく。（僕は）ちゃんと笑えているかな。 The register is casual (ちゃんと, かな). Nothing is poetic or unusual.

## Reading

- Kana: ちゃんと わらえて いる かな
- Romaji: chanto waraete iru kana

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | ちゃんと | ちゃんと (chanto) | adverb (副詞) | properly, the way it should be (casual); modifies 笑えている |
| 2 | 笑え | わらえ (warae) | godan verb 笑う → potential verb 笑える (可能動詞, conjugates like an ichidan verb), continuative form (連用形) before て | to be able to smile / laugh |
| 3 | て | て (te) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞) | te-form; joins 笑え to the subsidiary verb いる |
| 4 | いる | いる (iru) | subsidiary verb (補助動詞) いる, terminal form (終止形) | 〜ている: a continuing state — "am (currently) managing to smile" |
| 5 | か | か (ka) | sentence-final particle (終助詞) | question / doubt |
| 6 | な | な (na) | sentence-final particle (終助詞) | musing, talking to oneself; か + な = "I wonder (if)…" |

## How it fits together

`(line 035: …大人に なってく。) [ちゃんと [笑え て いる]] か な`

ちゃんと modifies 笑えている, and か + な turn the whole clause into a self-directed question. The subject 僕 "I" is unstated. Unlike line 014, nothing from the previous line attaches here, so this is a sentence of its own.

## Translation

- Literal: "(am I) properly being able to smile, I wonder"
- Natural: "(…I keep growing up.) Am I smiling properly now, I wonder?"

## Notes

- Line 035 closes its sentence with なってく ("I keep growing up"), so this question comes right after a statement. It reads as a check-in while that process is still going on. In the first chorus (lines 013–014), the te-form なって led straight into the same question, which there looked back on growing up as already done.
- Line 037 (頬を掠める春風が, "the spring breeze brushing my cheek") starts the next sentence with its own subject, marked by が, just as line 015 does in the first chorus. Nothing from this line carries over.
- 笑える is the potential form of 笑う ("can smile"). With ている it becomes an actual, present state: 笑えている = "am managing to smile (these days)". Compare 笑えるかな, "I wonder if I'll be able to smile", which looks ahead instead. (笑える can also mean "funny", as in それ笑える, but not here.)
- 笑う covers both "laugh" and "smile". Here it means smiling, being all right. ちゃんと suggests a real smile rather than a forced one.
- かな is casual and mostly self-directed: the speaker is musing rather than asking anyone for an answer.
