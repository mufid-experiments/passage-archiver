# Line 033 · 巡りゆく季節の隅っこで

## Context

- Line 032 (previous): 君だけがいない世界を
- **Line 033 (explained here): 巡りゆく季節の隅っこで**
- Line 034 (next): 幾つもの出会いと別れを

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: place phrase that restarts the chorus; its verb comes on line 035.

The words and the grammar are the same as on line 011. The line is one noun phrase, 巡りゆく季節の隅っこ ("the corner of the circling seasons"), made into a place adverbial by で, and it has no predicate of its own. Its verb is 繰り返し on line 035, whose object is line 034 (幾つもの出会いと別れを). This time line 035 ends in the terminal form なってく, so the sentence closes there, and the question on line 036 stands apart. Line 032 ends in a を whose verb is never sung (言いさし). In the main reading, this line starts the chorus afresh and does not supply that verb. Sung straight through, though, the two lines can also be heard as 君だけがいない世界を巡りゆく季節 (see Notes). Nothing inside the line is omitted or reordered, and the sentence's subject, the speaker (僕), is unstated. The one poetic touch is the "corner" of the seasons, a spatial word applied to time. Restored prose: 巡りゆく季節の隅っこで、幾つもの出会いと別れを繰り返し、大人になっていく。

## Reading

- Kana: めぐりゆく きせつ の すみっこ で
- Romaji: meguriyuku kisetsu no sumikko de

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 巡りゆく | めぐりゆく (meguriyuku) | compound verb (複合動詞) 巡りゆく = 巡る "go round" (godan, 連用形 巡り) + ゆく (行く) "go", conjugating like godan 行く; attributive form (連体形) | "to go on circling". 巡る is used for cycles (季節が巡る "the seasons come round"), and ゆく adds onward movement. Modifies 季節 |
| 2 | 季節 | きせつ (kisetsu) | noun | season(s); plural is unmarked. It is the seasons that circle |
| 3 | の | の (no) | case particle (格助詞) — links two nouns | "of": 季節の隅っこ "a corner of the seasons" |
| 4 | 隅っこ | すみっこ (sumikko) | noun; casual form of 隅 (すみ), with the suffix (接尾語) っこ | corner, nook: a spot off to the side, away from the centre. っこ adds a casual, familiar tone (compare 端っこ "the very edge", 根っこ "root") |
| 5 | で | で (de) | case particle (格助詞) — place of action (動作の場所) | "in, at"; marks the corner as the place where the actions that follow happen, first of all 繰り返し (line 035) |

## How it fits together

`[[巡りゆく 季節] の 隅っこ] で` → (幾つもの出会いと別れを) 繰り返し (line 035)

The structure is the same as on line 011. 巡りゆく is a relative clause (連体修飾節) on 季節, and its subject is the head noun: the seasons are what circle. 季節の隅っこ is "a corner of the seasons", and で makes the whole noun phrase an adverbial of place. A で-phrase modifies a predicate, not a noun (for that it would need での), so it waits for 繰り返し on line 035. This time the sentence ends on line 035 with なってく, so the phrase frames the growing up but not the question on line 036.

## Translation

- Literal: "in the corner of the seasons that go on circling"
- Natural: "In a little corner of the circling seasons"

## Notes

- **Line 032's を.** Line 032 (君だけがいない世界を) is a を-phrase whose verb is never sung (言いさし); a prose version would add something like 生きていく or 歩いていく. The main reading takes this line as a fresh start, as on line 011, which follows a line with no を (line 010 ends in 笑う). But 巡る can take a path-を (世界を巡る "go around the world"), so when the lines are sung straight through they can also be heard as 君だけがいない世界を巡りゆく季節, "the seasons that circle through a world where only you are missing". That parse is grammatical but crosses a section boundary. Treat it as a possible double meaning, not the main reading.
- In both hearings 季節 is what circles, and the phrase still ends in で and waits for 繰り返し on line 035. The second hearing only moves line 032's を inside the relative clause, giving the circling a route: [君だけがいない世界を巡りゆく] 季節. The rest of the sentence is unchanged.
- **Where the sentence ends.** The final chorus (lines 033–040) repeats the first word for word except on line 035, where なってく replaces なって. なってく is a terminal form, so this sentence is lines 033–035, and the question on line 036 is a sentence of its own. The final chorus holds four sentences: 033–035, 036, 037–038, and 039 with 040 attached by inversion. In the first chorus, the same phrase opened a sentence that ran on to the question on line 014 (see line 011).
- **巡りゆく季節 after line 032.** In the first chorus the phrase followed the thaw of line 010 and widened one spring into the whole cycle of years. Here it follows "a world where only you are missing", and the turning seasons read as time that keeps moving in that world, even without 君. On line 035 the speaker keeps growing up (なってく) as the seasons turn.
- **隅っこ vs 真ん中.** Line 025 has the same shape with the opposite place noun: 晴れ渡る景色の真ん中で ("right in the middle of a landscape under a cloudless sky"), where the speaker was dreaming with the spring breeze (line 026). Both are [verb + noun] の [place noun] で, and both wait for a verb on a later line.
- **Register.** 隅 is the neutral word for this kind of corner, and 片隅 (かたすみ) is frequent in writing and lyrics (心の片隅 "a corner of one's heart"). 隅っこ is the casual one, like the song's ちゃんと and かな. The line itself mixes registers: literary 巡りゆく, casual 隅っこ.
- **Two kinds of で.** On line 029 (ここはきっとスタートラインで), で is the continuative form of the copula だ: ここは is its topic, and the line means "this is the starting line, and…". Here で is the place particle, as on line 011. The line doesn't say that something is a corner; it says where the meetings and partings happen.
