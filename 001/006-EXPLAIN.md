# Line 006 · 流されるように生きて

## Context

- Line 005 (previous): 改札過ぎ行く人のように
- **Line 006 (explained here): 流されるように生きて**
- Line 007 (next): 今初めて自分で選ぶ

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: te-form clause; the sentence continues on line 007.

Fully grammatical, but 生きて is a te-form, so the sentence does not end here; it leads into the main clause on lines 007–008. Nothing is reordered. Two things are left unstated, as is normal in Japanese: the subject (the speaker, 僕) and the agent of the passive 流される (whatever does the sweeping). The back-to-back comparisons 人のように (line 005) and 流されるように would feel a little repetitive in prose, but they are grammatical.

## Reading

- Kana: ながされる ように いきて
- Romaji: nagasareru yō ni ikite

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 流さ | ながさ (nagasa) | godan verb 流す (transitive), irrealis form (未然形) | "to let flow, to wash away"; the stem the passive attaches to |
| 2 | れる | れる (reru) | auxiliary verb (助動詞) れる — passive (受身); attributive form (連体形) before ように | 流さ + れる = 流される "to be carried off, to be swept along" |
| 3 | ように | ように (yō ni) | auxiliary verb (助動詞) ようだ, continuative form (連用形) | "as if, like" (simile/manner, not the "so that" ように); turns 流される into an adverb of manner for 生きて |
| 4 | 生き | いき (iki) | ichidan verb 生きる, continuative form (連用形) | "to live"; the shape before て |
| 5 | て | て (te) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞) — te-form | "…and"; links this clause to the main clause 今初めて自分で選ぶ道（を）踏みしめてゆく |

## How it fits together

`[[流さ れる] ように] 生き て` → 踏みしめてゆく (line 008)

流されるように tells how the speaker lived, and line 005's 人のように attaches here too (改札過ぎ行く人のように流されるように生きて). 生きて is not the main verb. The te-form passes the sentence on to its main predicate, 踏みしめてゆく on line 008.

## Translation

- Literal: "living as if being swept along, and…"
- Natural: "I've lived as if carried along by the current, and (now)…"

## Notes

- Second line of the four-line sentence (005–008): 改札（を）過ぎ行く人のように / **流されるように生きて** / 今初めて自分で選ぶ / 道（を）踏みしめてゆく. Prose: 改札を過ぎ行く人のように流されるように生きて、今初めて自分で選ぶ道を踏みしめてゆく。 "I've lived as if swept along, like the people streaming through the ticket gates, and now, for the first time, I walk on with firm steps along a path of my own choosing."
- The te-form has no tense of its own. 今初めて ("now, for the first time") on line 007 shows that 生きて covers life up to now. The te-link carries a contrast: "lived like that… (and) now…". Prose would often use 生きてきて or 生きてきたけれど (補助動詞 くる, "have been living until now"). The bare て is more compact but still correct.
- 流される is the direct passive of 流す: 波が人を流す "the waves carry a person off" → 人が波に流される "a person is carried off by the waves". The agent (…に) is unstated here. With line 005 in mind, think of 人波に流される "to be swept along by the crowd".
- The figurative use is very common: 周りに流される "to go along with those around you", 時代に流される "to be carried along by the times". The rest of the sentence answers it: 自分で選ぶ ("choose by myself", line 007) and the firm footing of 踏みしめる (line 008), set against drifting in a current.
