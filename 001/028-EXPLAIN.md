# Line 028 · 「情けないな」って笑うだろう

## Context

- Line 027 (previous): こんな僕をたぶん君は
- **Line 028 (explained here): 「情けないな」って笑うだろう**
- Line 029 (next): ここはきっとスタートラインで

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — completes the sentence begun on line 027 (colloquial quotative って).

Grammatical: the line supplies the predicate for the object and topic of line 027, and together they make one complete sentence: こんな僕をたぶん君は「情けないな」って笑うだろう. The register is casual and speech-like: 「」 mark a direct quote, って is the colloquial form of the quotative と, and な is a casual sentence-final particle inside the quote. Nothing is omitted; in neutral written style the sentence would be 君はたぶん、こんな僕を「情けないな」と笑うだろう.

## Reading

- Kana: 「なさけない な」って わらう だろう
- Romaji: "nasakenai na" tte warau darō

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 情けない | なさけない (nasakenai) | i-adjective (形容詞) 情けない, terminal form (終止形) | pathetic, miserable, shameful; historically 情け "compassion" + 無い, but now one word, so this ない is not a separate negative auxiliary |
| 2 | な | な (na) | sentence-ending particle (終助詞) — exclamation (詠嘆) | "…, huh / man, …"; casual, adds feeling to the remark inside the quote |
| 3 | って | って (tte) | quotative particle — colloquial form of the quotative case particle と (格助詞, 引用) | marks 「情けないな」 as the words said while laughing ("saying '…'") |
| 4 | 笑う | わらう (warau) | godan verb 笑う, terminal form (終止形) | to laugh; with こんな僕を (line 027), "to laugh at me" |
| 5 | だろう | だろう (darō) | auxiliary verb (助動詞) — conjecture (推量) | "probably (will)"; the copula だ in its 未然形 だろ + the conjecture auxiliary う; answers たぶん on line 027 |

## How it fits together

`(こんな僕を たぶん 君は) [「情けない な」 って] 笑う だろう`

The quote plus って gives the content of the laughter ("laugh, saying '…'"), and 笑う takes its object (こんな僕を) and its topic/subject (君は) from line 027. だろう has scope over the whole clause and, together with たぶん, presents the scene as the narrator's guess.

## Translation

- Literal: "(you) will probably laugh (at me), (saying) 'pathetic, huh'"
- Natural: "…would probably laugh and say, 'Man, how pathetic.'" (with line 027: "You'd probably laugh at me, the way I am now, and say, 'Man, how pathetic.'")

## Notes

- The sentence spans lines 027–028 and ends with だろう. Line 029 starts a new sentence (ここはきっとスタートラインで) that turns from this imagined scene to looking ahead.
- The quoted words are 君's: the narrator imagines what the absent 君 (ここにはいない君, line 024) would say, which is why the line is a conjecture (たぶん … だろう) rather than a statement of fact.
- This な follows an adjective, so it is the exclamatory 終助詞 (情けないな "how pathetic"). It is neither the prohibitive な, which attaches to a verb's dictionary form (泣くな "don't cry"), nor the な of na-adjectives, which links to a noun (静かな夜 "a quiet night").
- って has several jobs in casual speech. Right after a quote and before a verb like 笑う it is the quotative (= と / と言って); compare the topic-marking って in 君って優しいね "you're kind, aren't you".
- 人を笑う can be mocking ("laugh at, ridicule") or fond ("laugh at someone teasingly"). The line itself doesn't decide, though the tender tone of the surrounding lines leans towards teasing.
