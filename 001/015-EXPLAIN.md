# Line 015 · 頬を掠める春風が

## Context

- Line 014 (previous): ちゃんと笑えているかな
- **Line 015 (explained here): 頬を掠める春風が**
- Line 016 (next): どこか懐かしくて振り向いた

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: subject phrase whose predicate is on line 016.

The line is grammatical but not a sentence by itself. It is a single noun phrase, 頬を掠める春風 ("the spring breeze that grazes my cheek"), followed by the subject marker が, and the predicate comes on line 016. Read across the line break, it is ordinary prose: （僕は）頬を掠める春風がどこか懐かしくて振り向いた。 Nothing inside the line is dropped or reordered. The only thing left unsaid is whose cheek it is (the speaker's), which is normal in Japanese.

## Reading

- Kana: ほほ を かすめる はるかぜ が
- Romaji: hoho o kasumeru harukaze ga

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 頬 | ほほ (hoho); standard ほお (hō) | noun | cheek |
| 2 | を | を (o) | case particle (格助詞) — object marker | marks 頬 as what gets grazed |
| 3 | 掠める | かすめる (kasumeru) | ichidan (下一段) verb 掠める, attributive form (連体形) | to graze, to brush lightly past; the clause 頬を掠める modifies 春風 |
| 4 | 春風 | はるかぜ (harukaze) | noun, compound of 春 "spring" + 風 "wind" | spring breeze |
| 5 | が | が (ga) | case particle (格助詞) — subject marker | marks the whole phrase 頬を掠める春風 as the subject of 懐かしくて on line 016 |

## How it fits together

- This line: `[[頬 を 掠める] 春風] が`
- Whole sentence: `[[[頬 を 掠める] 春風] が どこか 懐かしくて] (僕 は) 振り向いた`

頬を掠める is a relative clause (連体修飾節) on 春風, and its subject is the head noun itself: the breeze is what grazes the cheek. The whole phrase is then the が-subject of 懐かしくて on line 016. The last verb, 振り向いた, has a different subject that is never stated: the speaker (僕), not the breeze.

## Translation

- Literal: "the spring breeze that grazes (my) cheek…" (が marks it as the subject of what follows)
- Natural: "The spring breeze brushing past my cheek…" (line 016 completes it: "…felt somehow nostalgic, and I turned around.")

## Notes

- Line 014 ends the previous sentence with the sentence-final particle (終助詞) かな ("I wonder"), so line 015 starts a new one. That sentence ends with 振り向いた on line 016.
- Subject of each predicate in 頬を掠める春風がどこか懐かしくて振り向いた:
  - 掠める → 春風. The breeze brushes the cheek.
  - 懐かしくて → 春風, marked by が. With feeling adjectives (感情形容詞) such as 懐かしい, が marks what triggers the feeling. The person who feels it goes unsaid; here that is the speaker.
  - 振り向いた → the unstated speaker, 僕 (the speaker's "I" on line 027). A breeze cannot turn around, so the subject switches after the reason clause 〜くて.
- Why が and not は: a が-subject stays inside the 〜くて clause. 春風は would tend to be read as the topic of the whole sentence, and so of 振り向いた too.
- This が comes after a noun, so it is the subject marker. The conjunction が ("but") attaches to a predicate instead (〜だが, 〜ますが).
- 掠める is non-past although the sentence ends in the past. A relative clause takes its tense from the main event, so the breeze is brushing the cheek at the moment of turning ("the breeze that was brushing my cheek").
- Readings: dictionaries list 頬 under ほお. ほほ is a very common variant, especially in songs, and only the recording can settle which is sung here. 春風 can also be read しゅんぷう (literary), but はるかぜ is the natural reading in lyrics. There is no rendaku (not はるがぜ), because かぜ already contains a voiced consonant, and that blocks it.
- 掠める describes a light touch in passing. It is also used for thoughts that flash through the mind (嫌な予感が脳裏を掠める, "a bad feeling flashes through my mind"). 掠 is not one of the 常用漢字, so the verb is often written in kana, かすめる.
