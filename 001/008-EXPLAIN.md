# Line 008 · 道踏みしめてゆく

## Context

- Line 007 (previous): 今初めて自分で選ぶ
- **Line 008 (explained here): 道踏みしめてゆく**
- Line 009 (next): 大丈夫 君の手を背中に感じて

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — poetic: particle を omitted; closes the sentence begun on line 005.

Grammatical, with one poetic omission: the object particle を after 道 is dropped (prose: 道を踏みしめてゆく). 踏みしめてゆく is in the sentence-final form (終止形), so this line completes the sentence that started on line 005. Its subject (the speaker, 僕) stays unstated. ～てゆく is the literary, lyrical variant of ～ていく.

## Reading

- Kana: みち ふみしめて ゆく
- Romaji: michi fumishimete yuku

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 道 | みち (michi) | noun | road, path; figuratively one's path in life. Head noun of line 007's relative clause (今初めて自分で選ぶ道) |
| – | （を） | を (o) | case particle (格助詞) — **omitted** | would mark 道 as the object of 踏みしめる |
| 2 | 踏みしめ | ふみしめ (fumishime) | compound verb (複合動詞) 踏みしめる (also 踏み締める) = 踏む "step on" + 締める "tighten, firm up"; ichidan, continuative form (連用形) | "to tread firmly, to step down hard and feel the ground"; the shape before て |
| 3 | て | て (te) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞) — te-form | joins 踏みしめ to the subsidiary verb ゆく |
| 4 | ゆく | ゆく (yuku) | subsidiary verb (補助動詞) ゆく (= いく), sentence-final form (終止形), non-past | "go on (doing)": the action continues forward from now on; here it also keeps some literal sense of walking onward. Main predicate of lines 005–008 |

## How it fits together

`[[今初めて自分で選ぶ] 道]（を） [踏みしめ て ゆく]`

道 is the object of 踏みしめる and the head of the relative clause on line 007. 踏みしめてゆく is the main predicate of the whole sentence; the te-clause 流されるように生きて (line 006) leads into it. The subject, the speaker, is never stated.

## Translation

- Literal: "(I) go on treading (the) path firmly"
- Natural: "I walk on, step by steady step, along (that) path"

## Notes

- Last line of the four-line sentence (005–008): 改札（を）過ぎ行く人のように / 流されるように生きて / 今初めて自分で選ぶ / **道（を）踏みしめてゆく**. Prose: 改札を過ぎ行く人のように流されるように生きて、今初めて自分で選ぶ道を踏みしめてゆく。 "I've lived as if swept along, like the people streaming through the ticket gates, and now, for the first time, I walk on with firm steps along a path of my own choosing."
- The sentence ends here. Line 009 opens a new one with 大丈夫 ("it's all right"); 踏みしめてゆく does not modify anything on line 009.
- 踏みしめる means pressing each foot down and feeling the ground: deliberate, steady steps. It contrasts with line 006's 流される: carried by a current vs. walking on firm ground by one's own choice.
- 踏みしめる is also common for walking on snow (雪を踏みしめる), and snow appears at line 010. A literal snowy path may be in the picture too, but the main sense here is figurative.
- ～てゆく / ～ていく: the action carries on from the present into the future ("keep on…, go on…"). The song keeps to the literary ゆく (過ぎ行く, 巡りゆく); everyday speech would say 踏みしめていく.
