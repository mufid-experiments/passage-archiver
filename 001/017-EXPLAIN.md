# Line 017 · もう迷わない

## Context

- Line 016 (previous): どこか懐かしくて振り向いた
- **Line 017 (explained here): もう迷わない**
- Line 018 (next): 君と過ごした日々を胸に

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — complete sentence (subject omitted).

This is a grammatical, complete sentence: the adverb もう plus the plain negative predicate 迷わない. Only the subject is dropped, as is normal in Japanese when it is obvious. The subject is the speaker, who uses 僕 for "I" (line 027 こんな僕を). Restored: （僕は）もう迷わない. Line 016 closes its own sentence with the past-tense 振り向いた, so this line starts a new sentence. Line 018 has no main verb and is best read as attaching back to this line (see Notes). Nothing here is unusual.

## Reading

- Kana: もう まよわない
- Romaji: mō mayowanai

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | もう | もう (mō) | adverb (副詞) | with a negative: "(not) anymore, no longer"; with an affirmative it means "already" |
| 2 | 迷わ | まよわ (mayowa) | godan verb 迷う, irrealis / negative stem (未然形) | to lose one's way; to be undecided, to waver, to hesitate. Godan verbs ending in う take わ before ない (迷う → 迷わない, like 買う → 買わない) |
| 3 | ない | ない (nai) | auxiliary verb (助動詞) ない, plain non-past, sentence-final form (終止形) | negation "not"; with a first-person subject, the plain non-past reads as a declaration: "(I) won't …" |

## How it fits together

`（僕は） もう [迷わ ない]`

The omitted subject is the speaker (僕). もう applies to the whole negated predicate, and もう…ない implies a change of state: the speaker did waver before, and from now on will not. The line doesn't say what the speaker won't waver about. Line 018 supplies a circumstance instead: "with the days I spent with you in my heart".

## Translation

- Literal: "(I) will no longer be lost / hesitate."
- Natural: "I won't hesitate anymore."

## Notes

- Line 016 ("(the spring breeze) felt somehow nostalgic, and I turned around") is a finished sentence, and 017 starts a new one. The two lines move from turning to look back (振り向いた, past) to a resolve about what comes next (迷わない, non-past).
- Line 018 has no main verb, and it can't run on into line 019, which opens the next section with a new topic (神様は). So it attaches back to this line, placed after it by inversion (倒置法). Prose order: 君と過ごした日々を胸に、もう迷わない, "With the days I spent with you in my heart, I won't hesitate anymore." Saying 迷わない first puts the resolve up front, and the line that follows says what supports it.
- もう flips meaning with the polarity of the verb: もう決めた "I've already decided" vs. もう迷わない "I won't hesitate anymore".
- 迷う covers both "lose one's way" (道に迷う) and "be unable to decide" (どうするか迷う), and both senses fit the song. The speaker once lived 流されるように ("as if swept along", line 006). Now they walk a 道 they have chosen for the first time (lines 007–008), and later they say ここはきっとスタートラインで ("this is surely the starting line", line 029).
- 迷わない is a statement about the speaker, not a command. "Don't hesitate!" would be the prohibitive 迷うな. The plain form matches the song's casual register (僕, 君); the polite version would be もう迷いません.
