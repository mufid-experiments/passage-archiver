# Line 022 · また出会えるから

## Context

- Line 021 (previous): いつかどこか遠い空で
- **Line 022 (explained here): また出会えるから**
- Line 023 (next): 舞い上がる花びらに微笑む横顔

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: から clause with its main clause left unsaid (言いさし)

The line is grammatical. It is a subordinate reason clause, "because (we) can meet again", and the main clause that から normally leads into never comes. Line 023 starts a separate noun phrase instead. Stopping on から like this is called 言いさし ("trailing off"). It is completely natural in speech and in lyrics, so this is a deliberate, idiomatic fragment and not a mistake. With line 021 and the omitted parts filled in, the sentence reads: （僕らは）いつかどこか遠い空でまた出会えるから、（大丈夫だ／悲しくはない）.

## Reading

- Kana: また であえる から
- Romaji: mata deaeru kara

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | また | また (mata) | adverb (副詞) | "again"; modifies 出会える |
| 2 | 出会える | であえる (deaeru) | potential verb (可能動詞) of godan 出会う, terminal form (終止形) | "can meet, will be able to meet". 出会う is a compound: 出 (連用形 of 出る "to come out") + 会う "to meet", giving "to encounter, to come across". A godan verb forms its potential by moving to the e-row and adding る (出会う → 出会える). The result conjugates like an ichidan verb; the auxiliary れる isn't used |
| 3 | から | から (kara) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞), reason (原因・理由) | "because, since"; attaches to the terminal form. Here it ends the line and its main clause is left unsaid |

## How it fits together

`[(僕らは) (line 021: いつか どこか遠い空で) また 出会える] から、（…main clause unsaid…）`

Everything up to 出会える is one clause. It has an omitted subject (僕ら), the time and place from line 021, the adverb また, and the verb 出会える. から turns that clause into a reason, which would normally be followed by a main clause (「X から、Y」). Here Y is never said.

## Translation

- Literal: "because (we) can meet again"
- Natural: "'cause we'll meet again" (with line 021: "'Cause someday, somewhere in a faraway sky, we'll meet again.")

## Notes

- **Sentence-final から (言いさし):** Normally「X から、Y」means "because X, Y". Stopping after から and leaving Y unsaid is everyday Japanese, and some grammars treat it as a sentence-final use (終助詞的用法). It usually carries reassurance, a promise, or firm resolve: 大丈夫、すぐ戻るから "Don't worry, I'll be right back"; ずっと待ってるから "I'll be waiting (for you)". The listener fills in Y from context.
- **What is left unsaid here:** Lines 019–020 describe a separation (神様は僕らを切り離して / 別々の線を描いた "God cut us apart and drew separate lines"). Lines 021–022 answer it with a reason for hope, so the unspoken Y is something like だから大丈夫 "so it's all right" or だから悲しまない "so I won't grieve". Reading it as an inverted reason for line 020 (倒置法: "God drew separate lines because we'll meet again") makes little sense. Line 023 is a noun phrase (体言止め), so it can't be the main clause either.
- **から vs. ので:** から voices the speaker's own conviction, which suits this kind of reassurance. ので would sound more like a neutral, objective explanation.
- **Which から?** After a plain-form verb, から is the conjunctive particle "because". The case particle から "from" follows nouns (空から "from the sky").
- **出会う vs. 会う:** 会う is "to meet, to see (someone)". 出会う is "to encounter, to come across", often a first meeting or a meeting by chance or fate. So また出会える presents the reunion as a new encounter, not just seeing each other again (また会える). It also echoes 出会い on lines 012 and 034 (幾つもの出会いと別れを "many meetings and partings").
- **Potential form:** 出会える "can meet" states a confident possibility, close to "we will meet".
- **Omitted subject:** 僕ら "we" (from line 019), meeting each other. If you take 僕 "I" as the subject instead, then the omitted part is the partner, 君と or 君に.
