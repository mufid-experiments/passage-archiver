# Line 039 · もう迷わない

## Context

- Line 038 (previous): どこか懐かしくて振り向いた
- **Line 039 (explained here): もう迷わない**
- Line 040 (next): 君と過ごした日々を胸に

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — complete sentence (subject omitted).

This is a grammatical, complete sentence: the adverb もう plus the plain negative predicate 迷わない. Only the subject is dropped, as is normal in Japanese when it is obvious. The subject is the speaker, who uses 僕 for "I" (line 027 こんな僕を). Restored: （僕は）もう迷わない. Line 038 closes its own sentence with the past-tense 振り向いた, so this line starts a new sentence. Line 040, the last line of the song, has no main verb and attaches back to this line (see Notes). Nothing here is unusual.

## Reading

- Kana: もう まよわない
- Romaji: mō mayowanai

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | もう | もう (mō) | adverb (副詞) | with a negative: "(not) anymore, no longer"; with an affirmative it means "already" |
| 2 | 迷わ | まよわ (mayowa) | godan verb 迷う, irrealis / negative stem (未然形) | to lose one's way; to be undecided, to waver, to hesitate. Godan verbs ending in う take わ before ない (迷う → 迷わない, like 買う → 買わない) |
| 3 | ない | ない (nai) | auxiliary verb (助動詞) ない, plain non-past, sentence-final form (終止形) | negation "not"; with a first-person subject, the plain non-past reads as a declaration: "(I) won't …" |

## How it fits together

`（僕は） もう [迷わ ない]`

The omitted subject is the speaker (僕). もう applies to the whole negated predicate, and もう…ない implies a change of state: the speaker did waver before, and from now on will not. The line doesn't say what the speaker won't waver about. Line 040 supplies a circumstance instead: "with the days I spent with you in my heart".

## Translation

- Literal: "(I) will no longer be lost / hesitate."
- Natural: "I won't hesitate anymore."

## Notes

- Lines 033–040 repeat the first chorus (011–018) word for word, except that line 035 has 大人になってく where line 013 had 大人になって. So this line and its neighbours match 016–018 (see line 017). Line 038 ("(the spring breeze) felt somehow nostalgic, and I turned around") is a finished sentence, and 039 starts a new one. The two lines move from turning to look back (振り向いた, past) to a resolve about what comes next (迷わない, non-past).
- Line 040 is the last line of the song. It has no main verb and nothing follows it, so it attaches back to this line, placed after it by inversion (倒置法). Prose order: 君と過ごした日々を胸に、もう迷わない, "With the days I spent with you in my heart, I won't hesitate anymore." That makes 迷わない the last main predicate of the song.
- This time the chorus comes after lines 029–032. Those lines say ここはきっとスタートラインで ("this is surely the starting line") and describe what waits beyond the first step: 新しい世界, 君だけがいない世界 ("a new world, a world where only you are missing"). Read after that, もう迷わない sounds like resolve to step forward into that world.
- もう flips meaning with the polarity of the verb: もう決めた "I've already decided" vs. もう迷わない "I won't hesitate anymore".
- 迷う covers both "lose one's way" (道に迷う) and "be unable to decide" (どうするか迷う), and both senses fit the song. The speaker once lived 流されるように ("as if swept along", line 006) and now walks a 道 they have chosen for the first time (lines 007–008).
- 迷わない is a statement about the speaker, not a command. "Don't hesitate!" would be the prohibitive 迷うな. The polite version would be もう迷いません.
