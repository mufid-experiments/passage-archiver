# Line 007 · 今初めて自分で選ぶ

## Context

- Line 006 (previous): 流されるように生きて
- **Line 007 (explained here): 今初めて自分で選ぶ**
- Line 008 (next): 道踏みしめてゆく

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: relative clause modifying 道 on line 008.

Grammatical. Read alone, it looks like a complete sentence ("Now, for the first time, I choose by myself"), because 選ぶ has the same shape in the attributive form (連体形) as in the sentence-final form (終止形). But line 008 starts with the noun 道, so 選ぶ is attributive: 今初めて自分で選ぶ道 "the path I choose myself for the first time". The line is therefore a relative clause inside the long sentence of lines 005–008. The subject (僕) is omitted, which is normal; nothing is reordered or dropped beyond that.

## Reading

- Kana: いま はじめて じぶん で えらぶ
- Romaji: ima hajimete jibun de erabu

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 今 | いま (ima) | noun (名詞) used adverbially — time nouns need no particle | now |
| 2 | 初めて | はじめて (hajimete) | adverb (副詞) | for the first time |
| 3 | 自分 | じぶん (jibun) | noun — reflexive pronoun (再帰代名詞) | oneself; here the speaker, "myself" |
| 4 | で | で (de) | case particle (格助詞) — doer / means | 自分で = "by oneself, on one's own (decision)" |
| 5 | 選ぶ | えらぶ (erabu) | godan verb 選ぶ, attributive form (連体形) | "to choose"; modifies 道 at the start of line 008 |

## How it fits together

`[今 初めて [自分 で] 選ぶ]` → 道 (line 008)

The whole line is a relative clause describing 道 ("the path that I, now, for the first time, choose myself"). 道 is the thing chosen, and the clause's subject (僕が) is omitted. 今初めて most naturally goes with 選ぶ, though it can also be read as setting the time of the main clause (今初めて … 踏みしめてゆく); the meaning is the same either way.

## Translation

- Literal: "(the path) that (I) now, for the first time, choose by myself"
- Natural: "(a path) that now, for the first time, I choose for myself"

## Notes

- Third line of the four-line sentence (005–008): 改札（を）過ぎ行く人のように / 流されるように生きて / **今初めて自分で選ぶ** / 道（を）踏みしめてゆく. Prose: 改札を過ぎ行く人のように流されるように生きて、今初めて自分で選ぶ道を踏みしめてゆく。 "I've lived as if swept along, like the people streaming through the ticket gates, and now, for the first time, I walk on with firm steps along a path of my own choosing."
- This is the turning point. The te-form 生きて (line 006) links into it, and 今初めて sets "now" against the drifting life before. 自分で ("by my own choice") answers the passive 流される ("be swept along") of line 006.
- The line break falls between 選ぶ and 道. For a moment 選ぶ can be heard as a finished statement, "I choose", until line 008 turns it into a modifier.
- 初めて (adverb, "for the first time") and 始めて (te-form of 始める, "to begin") sound the same but are different words. 自分で means "by my own will or effort", not "alone"; "alone" is 一人で.
