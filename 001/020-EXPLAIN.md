# Line 020 · 別々の線を描いた

## Context

- Line 019 (previous): 神様は僕らを切り離して
- **Line 020 (explained here): 別々の線を描いた**
- Line 021 (next): いつかどこか遠い空で

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — completes the sentence begun on line 019

On its own, the line is an object plus a past-tense verb with no subject; the subject is 神様は on line 019. Read together, the two lines form one grammatical, complete sentence (a): 神様は僕らを切り離して、別々の線を描いた。 No particle is omitted and the word order is normal. The only poetic element is the metaphor of 線 (lines) for the separate courses of the two lives.

## Reading

- Kana: べつべつ の せん を えがいた
- Romaji: betsubetsu no sen o egaita

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 別々 | べつべつ (betsubetsu) | noun / na-adjective (名詞・形容動詞); 別 + repetition mark 々 | separate, apart, each its own |
| 2 | の | の (no) | case particle (格助詞), attributive | links 別々 to 線: "separate lines" |
| 3 | 線 | せん (sen) | noun | line; here the course of each life |
| 4 | を | を (o) | case particle (格助詞), object marker | marks 別々の線 as what was drawn |
| 5 | 描い | えがい (egai) | godan verb 描く (えがく), continuative form with euphonic change (イ音便: 描き → 描い) | to draw, to trace, to depict; alternative reading かい (kai) |
| 6 | た | た (ta) | auxiliary verb (助動詞) た, terminal form (終止形) | past: "drew"; ends the sentence begun on line 019 |

## How it fits together

`(神様 は — line 019) [[別々 の] 線] を 描い た`

別々 modifies 線 through の, and 別々の線 is the object of 描いた. The subject 神様 comes from line 019, and 描いた is the main predicate of the whole sentence: [神様は] [僕らを切り離して] [別々の線を描いた].

## Translation

- Literal: "(God) drew separate lines"
- Natural (with line 019): "God cut the two of us apart and drew a separate line for each of us"

## Notes

- This line completes the sentence from line 019, and its past tense also covers the te-form 切り離して. Line 021 (いつかどこか遠い空で) starts a new sentence that ends on line 022 (また出会えるから).
- The "lines" are the two lives, or fates, set on separate courses. Lines 021–022 answer this: someday, in some faraway sky, they can meet again, so separate lines may still cross.
- 描く can be read えがく or かく. えがく is given here as the likelier sung reading: it is the traditional reading, common in lyrics, and the usual one for tracing a path or curve (弧を描く こをえがく "to trace an arc"). かいた (kaita) is also possible; only the sung audio can settle it.
- For simply drawing a line, everyday Japanese says 線を引く (ひく). 描く makes God's act sound like sketching out the shape of two lives. 線 can also mean a railway line (山手線), which may echo 切り離す (uncoupling train cars) and the ticket gate (改札) of line 005. But 描いた keeps the main image one of lines drawn on a page or map.
- 々 is a repetition mark (踊り字), not a kanji with its own reading. It repeats the previous kanji, sometimes with voicing (日々 ひび on line 018), but 別々 is べつべつ, with no voicing.
- 別々 usually takes の before a noun (別々の道 "separate ways") and に as an adverb (別々に暮らす "to live apart").
