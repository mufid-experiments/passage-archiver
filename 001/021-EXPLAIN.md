# Line 021 · いつかどこか遠い空で

## Context

- Line 020 (previous): 別々の線を描いた
- **Line 021 (explained here): いつかどこか遠い空で**
- Line 022 (next): また出会えるから

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: continues on line 022

The line is grammatical, but it is not a complete sentence. It holds only adverbials: a time word (いつか "someday") and a place phrase (どこか遠い空で "somewhere in a faraway sky"). Its verb, 出会える "can meet", comes on line 022. The subject is also left out. From line 019 (僕らを) it is 僕ら "we". The full sentence is （僕らは）いつかどこか遠い空でまた出会えるから. No particle is dropped and nothing is reordered; the only break is the line break.

## Reading

- Kana: いつか どこか とおい そら で
- Romaji: itsuka dokoka tōi sora de

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |

## How it fits together

`[いつ か] [[どこ か] [遠い 空]] で  →  (line 022) また 出会える から`

いつか is a time adverbial. どこか and 遠い空 stand side by side in apposition ("somewhere", namely "a faraway sky"), and で marks the whole phrase as the place of the action. Both adverbials modify 出会える on line 022. The subject, 僕ら "we", is not stated.

## Translation

- Literal: "someday, somewhere, in a distant sky"
- Natural: "Someday, somewhere in a faraway sky (we'll meet again…)"

## Notes

- **Where the sentence starts and ends:** Line 020 finishes the previous sentence (神様は僕らを切り離して / 別々の線を描いた "God cut us apart and drew separate lines"). Line 021 starts a new sentence that ends with から on line 022. The "we" of that sentence is the 僕ら of line 019.
- **Past or future:** いつか can point to the future ("someday") or the past ("once, at some point", as in いつか見た景色 "a view I once saw"). Here it is future, because line 022 has the non-past potential verb 出会える and また "again".
- **どこか here vs. line 016:** Here どこか has its literal meaning, "somewhere". On line 016 (どこか懐かしくて) the same word is the adverb "somehow, in some way". Both come from どこ + か.
- **Pattern: question word + か + modifier + noun.** どこか遠い空 works like 誰か優しい人 "someone kind" or 何か温かいもの "something warm". The noun phrase after the indefinite narrows it down.
- **で, not に:** で marks the place where an action or event happens (公園で出会う "meet in the park"). に would mark where something exists, or a destination. 出会う is an event, so で is correct.
- **遠い空:** "a faraway sky" is a stock image for a far-off place (compare 遠い空の下 "under a distant sky", meaning far away). The lyric doesn't say whether this is a real place or something more figurative. Pairing a vague time with a vague place (いつか…どこか…) makes the line sound like a hope, not a plan.
