# Line 004 · 包み込む

## Context

- Line 003 (previous): 凍えた両手をそっと
- **Line 004 (explained here): 包み込む**
- Line 005 (next): 改札過ぎ行く人のように

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: completes the clause begun on line 003.

On its own the line is a bare transitive verb with no subject or object. Its object (凍えた両手を) and its adverb (そっと) are on line 003; read together, 凍えた両手をそっと包み込む is a complete, grammatical clause, with only the subject left unstated, which is normal in Japanese. 包み込む is in the plain non-past form (終止形) and ends the sentence. Apart from the line break, nothing is omitted or reordered.

## Reading

## How it fits together

`(omitted subject) [[凍え た] 両手 を] そっと (line 003) ／ 包み込む`

包み込む is the predicate. Its object 凍えた両手を and its manner adverb そっと are on line 003; the subject is not stated on either line.

## Translation

- Literal: "wrap(s) up completely"
- Natural: "enfold(s)" (with line 003: "gently enfold(s) the frozen hands")

## Notes

- **Lines 003–004 are one clause split across the line break (confirmed).** Line 003 has an object and an adverb but no verb; line 004 has a transitive verb and nothing else: `凍えた両手を (object) + そっと (adverb) ／ 包み込む (verb)` → 凍えた両手をそっと包み込む "gently enfold(s) the frozen hands". The を on line 003 has no verb until 包み込む, and 包み込む has no object except 凍えた両手を.
- **The sentence ends here.** The 終止形 and 連体形 look identical in modern Japanese, but 包み込む does not modify anything on line 005 (包み込む改札 would make no sense). Line 005 starts a new sentence: 改札過ぎ行く人のように most naturally pairs with 流されるように生きて on line 006, "like people passing through the ticket gates, living as if swept along". Hooking line 005 back onto 包み込む as an inversion (倒置法) is conceivable, but the matching …ように ／ …ように favours the forward link.
- **包む vs. 包み込む.** 包む is "wrap, cover". Adding 込む ("into, thoroughly") stresses enclosing something completely, all the way round, and the compound is a favourite verb for warmth or kindness enveloping someone (温もりに包み込まれる "to be enfolded in warmth"). With そっと from line 003, the gesture is gentle and protective.
- **Tense.** The non-past narrates the scene as present, "enfold(s)"; the opening verse stays in the non-past (選ぶ on line 007, 踏みしめてゆく on line 008).
- **Omitted subject.** Neither line says who does the wrapping or whose hands these are; the text leaves open whether the speaker's own hands are being warmed or one person is enfolding another's.
