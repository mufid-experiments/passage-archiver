# Line 019 · 光と影に包まれていく

## Context

- Line 018 (previous): 本当が待っているんだろう
- **Line 019 (explained here): 光と影に包まれていく**
- Line 020 (next): まほろのうたごえだけ　響く

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — repeats line 007: passive 包まれる + 〜ていく; here it is most naturally a relative clause describing the voice on line 020.

The line is grammatical and repeats line 007 word for word. 光と影に ("by light and shadow") gives what does the wrapping, 包まれる is the passive of 包む ("wrap"), and 〜ていく adds "go on, more and more". As on line 007, 包まれていく has the same form whether it ends a sentence or modifies a noun. What differs is the context. Line 007 came straight after an open "if" (知ってしまったら), so it could be heard as the result. Here, line 018 has closed its sentence (待っているんだろう), and line 020 brings a noun with a verb of its own: まほろのうたごえだけ　響く. The most natural reading is therefore one sentence across the two lines: 光と影に包まれていくまほろのうたごえだけ（が）響く "only the singing voice of mahoro, enfolded in light and shadow, resounds". It is still possible to hear this line as a sentence of its own, "(everything) is wrapped, more and more, in light and shadow", followed by a second sentence on line 020. Nothing needs correcting.

## Reading

- Kana: ひかり と かげ に つつまれて いく
- Romaji: hikari to kage ni tsutsumarete iku

## How it fits together

`[[[光 と 影] に] [包ま れ て いく]] → [まほろ の うたごえ] だけ　響く (line 020)`

光と影に包まれていく is a relative clause, and its head is うたごえ on line 020. The subject of 包まれていく is that voice: it is the voice that is wrapped in light and shadow. だけ and 響く on line 020 then complete the sentence.

## Translation

- Literal: "(that) goes on being wrapped in light and shadow"
- Natural: "enfolded in light and shadow…" (with line 020: "Only the singing voice of mahoro, enfolded in light and shadow, rings out.")

## Notes

- **The same line in a new setting.** Lines 017–020 follow the plan of lines 005–008: a sentence about 本当, this line, then the voice of mahoro. Line 007 sat between an open conditional and a bare noun, so it could face either way. This time the sentence before it is closed, and the noun after it has its own verb. The repeat sounds the same, but its grammar settles: it most naturally describes the voice.
- **Light and shadow after 本当.** Both times the song mentions 本当 "what's real", this line follows. After 本当を知ってしまったら (line 006), light and shadow could be what the truth brings. After 本当が待っているんだろう (line 018), they surround the voice that rings out once the truth is in sight. Either way, the truth does not come as pure light.
- **〜に包まれる.** The passive of 包む "wrap" is often used for being surrounded completely: 炎に包まれる "be engulfed in flames", 謎に包まれる "be shrouded in mystery", 光に包まれる "be bathed in light". The line takes the familiar 光に包まれる and pairs the light with shadow (see line 007).
- **〜ていく.** The subsidiary verb いく puts the change on a line running forward: the wrapping grows as time goes on. The song writes this いく in kana. Line 016 used the literary variant ゆく (流れてゆく), and the two are the same word.
- **Link to line 020.** Line 020 (まほろのうたごえだけ　響く) adds two things to line 008's bare noun phrase: だけ "only" and the verb 響く "resound". Where lines 007–008 ended on an image, lines 019–020 end on a sound filling the space.
