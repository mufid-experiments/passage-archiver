# Line 009 · それは「魔法」じゃない　想いを分け合って

## Context

- Line 008 (previous): まほろのうたごえ
- **Line 009 (explained here): それは「魔法」じゃない　想いを分け合って**
- Line 010 (next): それは「最後」じゃない　時間を乗り越えて

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — two parts: a complete negative sentence (それは「魔法」じゃない) and an open te-form clause (想いを分け合って) that says what "it" is instead.

The line is grammatical and falls into two parts at the full-width space. それは「魔法」じゃない "it isn't 'magic'" is a complete sentence: the topic それは and the noun predicate 魔法, negated with じゃない, the spoken form of ではない. The brackets 「」 set 魔法 off as a word, "what you might call 'magic'". The second part, 想いを分け合って "sharing our feelings with each other", is a te-form clause with nothing after it. Placed after the denial, it gives the real explanation: not magic, but feelings shared. Prose would join the two with ではなく, roughly 魔法ではなく、想いを分け合って（生まれたもの）だ "it's not magic: it came from sharing our feelings". The subject of 分け合う is unstated. It is "we", the ふたり "two of us" of line 011. それ points back to something just mentioned, most naturally line 008's singing voice. The te-form leaves the line open, and lines 010–011 repeat the pattern until the chorus ends on line 012. The line returns unchanged at lines 025 and 029. Nothing needs correcting.

## Reading

- Kana: それ は まほう じゃ ない / おもい を わけあって
- Romaji: sore wa mahō ja nai / omoi o wakeatte

## How it fits together

`[それ は [「魔法」 じゃ ない]]　／　[想い を [分け 合っ て]] → (line 010: それは「最後」じゃない　時間を乗り越えて)`

The first half is a whole sentence: topic, noun predicate, negation. The second half has an unstated subject ("we"), an object and a te-form verb, and it is set against the first: the denial says what it isn't, and the te-form clause says what it is. The te-form does not end the sentence, so the chorus carries on.

## Translation

- Literal: "it is not 'magic'; sharing (our) feelings with each other, …"
- Natural: "It isn't 'magic': it's us sharing what we feel…"

## Notes

- **The chorus pattern.** Lines 009–011 share a shape: それは「X」じゃない, a space, then a phrase giving what it is instead. Each X is a word for something that comes from outside, or that ends: 「魔法」 "magic", 「最後」 "the end", 「奇跡」 "a miracle". Each answer is something people do or are: sharing feelings (009), getting past time (010), the two of us (011). Line 012, 日が昇る場所 "the place where the sun rises", closes the chorus. In prose, "not X but Y" is usually 〜ではなく〜: 魔法ではなく、想いを分け合った結果だ "it's not magic, it's the result of sharing our feelings".
- **「」 as quotation marks for a word.** Japanese brackets mark direct speech, but they also set off a single word, often one used in a special sense or not accepted at face value, like English scare quotes: いわゆる「魔法」 "so-called 'magic'". Here they suggest that someone would call it magic, and the song turns that label down. 「最後」 and 「奇跡」 on lines 010–011 get the same treatment.
- **What それ points to.** The nearest candidate is line 008's まほろのうたごえ, the last thing named, and the same order recurs later: the voice on line 020, then それは未来につなぐ on line 021. それ can also be heard more broadly, as whatever the two people share, or the place the chorus ends on (日が昇る場所, line 012). The song never names it, so each listener fills it in.
- **分け合う, another 〜合う.** 分ける "divide, split" + 合う "with each other" gives 分け合う "share between us": 一つのパンを分け合う "share one piece of bread", 喜びを分け合う "share joy". Line 001 had 信じ合う "believe together". In both, what is shared cannot be held in the hand: something invisible (見えないもの) there, and feelings (想い) here.
- **想い and 思い.** Both are read おもい. 思い is the general word, "thought, feeling", and is the standard spelling. 想い is used for heartfelt feelings, longing and love, and it is very common in songs. The official Jōyō table gives 想 only the readings ソウ and ソ, so newspapers write 思い. 想い is an expressive spelling chosen for its feeling.
- **The open te-form.** Like 乗り越えて on line 010, 分け合って does not close the sentence. It can be heard as means ("(it comes) from sharing our feelings"), and it also carries the chorus forward to the next line. A line-final te-form can be a soft request too ("share (it)"), but with 合う "with each other" and a song about "we", the descriptive reading is the natural one.
- **Link to line 010.** Line 010 (それは「最後」じゃない　時間を乗り越えて) repeats the pattern with a new denial and a new te-form: "it isn't 'the end': getting past time…". The two te-forms, 分け合って and 乗り越えて, line up as things the two people do together.
