# Line 024 · 幻なんかじゃない　僕たちは知ってるはず

## Context

- Line 023 (previous): 羽ばたける　導かれる　まだ見ぬ先へ
- **Line 024 (explained here): 幻なんかじゃない　僕たちは知ってるはず**
- Line 025 (next): それは「魔法」じゃない　想いを分け合って

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — colloquial: two sentences, 幻なんかじゃない (negative noun predicate with dismissive なんか) and 僕たちは知ってるはず (はず with だ left out); the first is also what "we" know.

The line is grammatical and falls into two parts at the full-width space. 幻なんかじゃない "it's no illusion" is a complete sentence with its subject unstated: "it", the melody and the flight of lines 022–023, or everything the song has been describing. なんか after 幻 is casual and dismissive: "the likes of an illusion". 僕たちは知ってるはず "we must know" is the second sentence. 知ってる is the spoken contraction of 知っている, and はず "should, must (by all reason)" ends the line without its copula だ. 知る needs an object, and none is given. The natural one is the first half of the line: 幻なんかじゃない（って）僕たちは知ってるはず（だ） "we must know it's no illusion". 僕たち is the only personal pronoun in the song. The register is casual throughout. Line 025 brings back the chorus and its row of じゃない denials. Restored prose: それは幻なんかじゃないって、僕たちは知っているはずだ。 Nothing needs correcting.

## Reading

- Kana: まぼろし なんか じゃ ない / ぼくたち は しってる はず
- Romaji: maboroshi nanka ja nai / bokutachi wa shitteru hazu

## How it fits together

`[(それ は) 幻 なんか じゃ ない]（って） ／ [僕たち は] [[知っ て (い)る] はず]（だ）`

The first half is a whole sentence with its subject left out. In the second half, 僕たちは is the topic and 知ってるはず the predicate. The thing known is most naturally the first half, so the line can be heard as one thought said in reverse order: "it's no illusion, and we know it".

## Translation

- Literal: "(it's) not some illusion; we should know (it)"
- Natural: "It's no illusion. We know that, don't we."

## Notes

- **A fourth denial.** The chorus is built on じゃない: 「魔法」じゃない, 「最後」じゃない, 「奇跡」じゃない (lines 009–011). Just before it returns at line 025, this line adds one more, 幻なんかじゃない. The chorus words were in brackets, labels someone might use. This one has なんか instead, which pushes the word away. After a melody "like a fairy tale" (line 022) and a flight toward the unseen (line 023), this is the denial the song most needs to make: none of it is a dream.
- **なんか after a noun.** なんか is "the likes of, something like" and often belittles: 幻なんかじゃない "it's no mere illusion", 僕なんか "someone like me". The neutral, more formal form is など, and なんて is close but often adds surprise or scorn. Plain 幻じゃない would be a simple denial. なんか rejects the very idea.
- **幻.** 幻 is an illusion, a vision that vanishes when you look again: 幻を見る "see things", 幻のように消える "vanish like a phantom". 幻の〜 also means "legendary, almost never seen": 幻の名酒 "a near-mythical sake". Here it is the plain sense, something that isn't really there.
- **僕たち, the song's only personal pronoun.** The song talked about 人 "people" (line 002) and ふたり "the two of us" (lines 011, 031), and only here says "we". 僕 is a casual first person, traditionally male, but in J-pop it is common in songs by singers of any gender, and it can sound young and sincere. 僕たち may be the ふたり of the chorus, or a wider "we" that includes the listener.
- **知ってる.** In speech, ている often loses its い: 知っている → 知ってる, 見ている → 見てる. 知る is the moment of finding out, and 知っている (知ってる) the state of knowing. The song has both. 本当を知ってしまったら (line 006) feared the moment of finding out, and 本当が待っているんだろう (line 018) guessed what lay ahead. Here, 知ってるはず says the knowing is already there.
- **はず.** はず expresses what should be so, by reasoning or by what one knows: 彼はもう着いているはずだ "he should be there by now". Negated, はずがない means "can't possibly be". 知ってるはず is firm, but it is not a flat 知っている "we know". It has a note of reminding, to oneself or to the other: "we know this, don't we?". Dropping だ after はず is ordinary in casual speech.
- **Link to line 025.** The chorus returns: それは「魔法」じゃない　想いを分け合って. Coming right after "it's no illusion, and we know it", its denials sound less like answers to a doubt and more like the things the two of them know.
