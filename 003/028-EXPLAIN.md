# Line 028 · 日が昇る場所

## Context

- Line 027 (previous): それは「奇跡」じゃない　透き通った世界で
- **Line 028 (explained here): 日が昇る場所**
- Line 029 (next): それは「魔法」じゃない　想いを分け合って

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — completes the phrase begun on line 027: 透き通った世界で + 日が昇る form one relative clause on 場所 (体言止め); it closes the second chorus.

The line is a grammatical noun phrase and repeats line 012 word for word, but here it is joined to the line before. 日が昇る "the sun rises" is a relative clause on 場所 "place". Line 027 ended on 透き通った世界で "in a clear world", a place phrase waiting for a verb, and 昇る is that verb. The whole phrase is 透き通った世界で日が昇る場所 "the place where the sun rises, in a world grown clear". It answers line 027's denial: それは「奇跡」じゃない、（それは）透き通った世界で日が昇る場所（だ） "it isn't 'a miracle': it is the place where the sun rises in a clear world". At lines 012 and 032, the noun ふたり stands between the two lines, so this line is a separate phrase there. Here nothing intervenes. The line ends on a noun (体言止め), and line 029 starts the chorus again. Nothing needs correcting.

## Reading

- Kana: ひ が のぼる ばしょ
- Romaji: hi ga noboru basho

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 日 | ひ (hi) | noun | the sun (also "day"); here the sun |
| 2 | が | が (ga) | case particle (格助詞), subject | marks 日 as the subject inside the relative clause; の could replace it (日の昇る場所) |
| 3 | 昇る | のぼる (noboru) | godan verb 昇る, attributive form (連体形) | to rise, to go up into the sky; takes 透き通った世界で (line 027) as its setting, and the whole clause modifies 場所 |
| 4 | 場所 | ばしょ (basho) | noun | place; the head of the phrase (体言止め) |

## How it fits together

`(line 027: それは「奇跡」じゃない　→) [[透き通った 世界 で (line 027)] [日 が 昇る]] 場所`

透き通った世界で gives the setting, and 日が昇る the event. Together they form the relative clause, and 場所 is its head. The noun phrase is what "it" is, after the denial on line 027. The copula is not said.

## Translation

- Literal: "the place where the sun rises (in a transparent world)"
- Natural: "the place where the sun comes up" (with line 027: "It isn't 'a miracle': it's the place where the sun rises, in a world grown clear.")

## Notes

- **One phrase across two lines.** In the first chorus, 透き通った世界でふたり / 日が昇る場所 were two images side by side, the two people and a place (lines 011–012). Without ふたり, line 027 and this line become one connected phrase: the clear world is where the sun rises, and that place is the answer to 「奇跡」じゃない.
- **Two places in one phrase.** 世界で sets the wide scene, and 場所 is the spot within it. Japanese uses this freely in relative clauses: 世界で一番星がきれいに見える場所 "the place in the world where the stars look the most beautiful". The head noun 場所 needs no "where": being the head of the clause is enough to make it the place where the sun rises.
- **Sunrise, again.** As at line 012, the chorus ends on a beginning, after denying that this is 「最後」 "the end" (line 026). The details of 日 "sun", 昇る and the が/の alternation are at line 012.
- **Straight into the last chorus.** Line 029 restarts the chorus at once: それは「魔法」じゃない. The text marks no stanza breaks anywhere (there are no blank lines in the passage), but the repeats make the shape clear: this ends the second chorus, and lines 029–032 are the third and last.
