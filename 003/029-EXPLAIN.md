# Line 029 · それは「魔法」じゃない　想いを分け合って

## Context

- Line 028 (previous): 日が昇る場所
- **Line 029 (explained here): それは「魔法」じゃない　想いを分け合って**
- Line 030 (next): それは「最後」じゃない　時間を乗り越えて

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — repeats lines 009 and 025: a complete negative sentence (それは「魔法」じゃない) and an open te-form clause (想いを分け合って) that says what "it" is instead.

The line is grammatical and repeats lines 009 and 025 word for word. It opens the third and last chorus (lines 029–032). それは「魔法」じゃない "it isn't 'magic'" is a complete sentence: the topic それは and the noun predicate 魔法 in brackets, negated with the spoken じゃない. After the full-width space, 想いを分け合って "sharing our feelings with each other" is a te-form clause with nothing after it. It gives the real explanation for what the denial rejected: not magic, but feelings shared. The subject of 分け合う is "we", the 僕たち of line 024. The chorus follows straight on from the second one, so this time the nearest thing before それ is 日が昇る場所 (line 028). The "it" the chorus has been talking about all along is still there, and the line adds nothing new to its grammar. Nothing needs correcting.

## Reading

- Kana: それ は まほう じゃ ない / おもい を わけあって
- Romaji: sore wa mahō ja nai / omoi o wakeatte

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | それ | それ (sore) | demonstrative pronoun (指示代名詞) | "that, it": the same "it" as in the earlier choruses (see Notes) |
| 2 | は | は (wa) | binding particle (係助詞), topic | "as for it": the topic of the denial |
| 3 | 「 | — | opening quotation bracket (かぎかっこ) | sets 魔法 off as a word: a label someone might use |
| 4 | 魔法 | まほう (mahō) | noun (Sino-Japanese, 漢語) | magic, sorcery |
| 5 | 」 | — | closing quotation bracket | closes the quoted word |
| 6 | じゃ | じゃ (ja) | contraction of では: copula だ in its continuative form で + binding particle は | spoken では in a negative noun predicate (魔法ではない → 魔法じゃない) |
| 7 | ない | ない (nai) | auxiliary adjective (補助形容詞) ない, terminal form (終止形) | "is not": closes the first sentence |
| 8 | 想い | おもい (omoi) | noun, from the verb 想う (思う) | feelings, what is in one's heart; love, longing |
| 9 | を | を (o) | case particle (格助詞), direct object | marks 想い as what is shared |
| 10 | 分け | わけ (wake) | ichidan verb 分ける, continuative form (連用形) | to divide, to share out; first element of 分け合う |
| 11 | 合っ | あっ (at-) | godan verb 合う as the second element of a compound verb (複合動詞), continuative form with sound change to small っ (促音便: 合い → 合っ) before て | "with each other": 分け合う = "share (between us)" |
| 12 | て | て (te) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞), te-form | "…ing, by ~ing": the clause stays open and leads on to line 030 |

## How it fits together

`[それ は [「魔法」 じゃ ない]]　／　[(僕たち が) 想い を [分け 合っ て]] → (line 030: それは「最後」じゃない　時間を乗り越えて)`

The first half denies a label, and the second half says what is true instead. The te-form keeps the chorus open for the next two denials.

## Translation

- Literal: "it is not 'magic'; sharing (our) feelings with each other, …"
- Natural: "It isn't 'magic': it's us sharing what we feel…"

## Notes

- **The last chorus.** Lines 029–032 repeat lines 009–012 exactly, including ふたり on line 031, which the second chorus left out (line 027). The full analysis of this line is at line 009.
- **A different neighbour for それ each time.** In the first chorus, それ came right after the singing voice (まほろのうたごえ, line 008). In the second, it came after 幻なんかじゃない (line 024), whose "it" was the melody of lines 021–022. Here it comes after 日が昇る場所 (line 028). The pronoun never changes, but each chorus places it after something new, so by the last chorus それ has gathered up all of them: the voice, the melody, what the two of them share, and the place where the sun rises.
- **Repetition and 「魔法」.** Hearing the chorus a third time gives the denial more weight. What returns again and again is not magic, something that happens once and from outside. It is something kept up, like the sharing in 想いを分け合って.
- **分け合う and 想い.** 分け合う is 分ける "divide" + 合う "with each other". 想い is an expressive spelling of 思い, used for heartfelt feelings. The song began with another 〜合う verb: 見えないものを信じ合う "we believe together in what we can't see" (line 001). Its last chorus still turns on something shared that can't be held in the hand.
- **Link to line 030.** Line 030 (それは「最後」じゃない　時間を乗り越えて) repeats lines 010 and 026. In the last chorus, "it isn't 'the end'" is sung as the song itself is ending.
