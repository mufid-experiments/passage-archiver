## Line 031 · それは「奇跡」じゃない　透き通った世界でふたり

### Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — poetic: a complete negative sentence (それは「奇跡」じゃない), then a phrase ending on the bare noun ふたり with no predicate.

The line is grammatical and repeats line 011 word for word. それは「奇跡」じゃない "it isn't 'a miracle'" is a complete sentence: the topic それは and the noun predicate 奇跡 in brackets, negated with the spoken じゃない. The brackets mark 奇跡 as a label the song turns down. After the full-width space, 透き通った世界で "in a clear, transparent world" is a place phrase, and で needs a verb to belong to. None comes. The line ends on the noun ふたり "the two of us". Prose would need a particle and a predicate, for example 透き通った世界でふたり（でいる） "(we are) together, the two of us, in a clear world". The second half answers the denial: not a miracle, but the two of us. The second chorus left ふたり out (line 027). The last chorus brings it back, so ふたり stands between 世界で and line 032, which is therefore a separate noun phrase. Nothing needs correcting.

### How it fits together

The first half denies the label. In the second half, 透き通った世界で sets the scene and ふたり names who is in it. The predicate that で is waiting for is not given.

### Translation

- Literal: "it is not 'a miracle'; in a transparent world, two (of us)"
- Natural: "It isn't 'a miracle': it's the two of us, in a world grown clear…"

## Line 032 · 日が昇る場所

### Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — poetic: noun-ending phrase (体言止め), a relative clause (日が昇る) on 場所; the song ends here.

The line is a grammatical noun phrase, not a sentence, and it is the last line of the song. 日が昇る "the sun rises" is a relative clause on 場所 "place": "the place where the sun rises". No word for "where" is needed, because 場所, as the head noun, is the place in which the clause happens. There is no predicate, so the song ends on a noun (体言止め). Line 031 ended on ふたり, so this line stands beside it as a second noun phrase, as at line 012. It is not the end of the で-phrase, as it was at line 028. How the two images relate is not said. The place may be where the two of them are, where they are going, or what "it" (それ) is in the end. The song stops on the image of sunrise. Nothing needs correcting.

### Reading

- Kana: ひ が のぼる ばしょ
- Romaji: hi ga noboru basho

### Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |

| 3 | 昇る | のぼる (noboru) | godan verb 昇る, attributive form (連体形) | to rise, to go up into the sky; the clause 日が昇る modifies 場所 |

### How it fits together

`(line 031: …透き通った世界でふたり →) [[日 が 昇る] 場所]`

日が昇る is a complete little clause, and it describes 場所. The whole line is one noun phrase, and grammatically it stands beside line 031's phrase without depending on it. The two are linked as images, not by grammar.

### Translation

- Literal: "the place where the sun rises"
- Natural: "the place where the sun comes up" (with line 031: "It isn't 'a miracle': it's the two of us, in a world grown clear: the place where the sun rises.")

## Notes

- **How the two lines connect.** They are two noun phrases side by side: 透き通った世界でふたり and 日が昇る場所. The first stops on ふたり without the verb its で is waiting for. The second has its own verb inside, 昇る, but no predicate. Nothing in the grammar joins them, so the listener does: two people in a clear world, and a place where the sun rises, perhaps the same place.
- **Three endings for one chorus.** 日が昇る場所 closes every chorus. At line 012 it follows ふたり and stands apart. At line 028, with no ふたり before it, it completes the で-phrase as one connected phrase (透き通った世界で日が昇る場所). Here it stands apart again, as the last line of the song. The song's final picture keeps the people and the place side by side.
- **From the unseen to the sunrise.** The song opened on 見えないものを信じ合う "we believe together in what we can't see" (line 001). It ends on a world that is 透き通った, clear enough to see through, and on the moment the sun comes up and everything becomes visible. Heard that way, the song moves from believing without seeing toward light. The lyric never says so directly. It only sets the images in that order.
- **Ending on a beginning.** Two lines earlier, the chorus denied that "it" is 「最後」 "the end" (line 030). The song keeps its word and ends not on a closing word but on sunrise, the start of a day.
- **Where the sun rises.** "The place where the sun rises" is also the east, and an old way of naming Japan (日本, "origin of the sun"). Together with the ancient word まほろ on line 008, a listener may hear the echo of an old, beloved homeland. The song does not press it (see line 012).
- **Spelling and words.** For 奇跡, 透き通った and ふたり, see line 011. For 日 "sun", 昇る versus 上る and 登る, and 日が昇る versus 日の昇る, see line 012.
