### Is it valid Japanese?

{% include explain/verdict.html n="028" %}

The line is a grammatical noun phrase and repeats line 012 word for word, but here it is joined to the line before. <ruby>日<rt>ひ</rt></ruby>が<ruby>昇<rt>のぼ</rt></ruby>る “the sun rises” is a relative clause on <ruby>場<rt>ば</rt>所<rt>しょ</rt></ruby> “place”. Line 027 ended on <ruby>透<rt>す</rt></ruby>き<ruby>通<rt>とお</rt></ruby>った<ruby>世<rt>せ</rt>界<rt>かい</rt></ruby>で “in a clear world”, a place phrase waiting for a verb, and <ruby>昇<rt>のぼ</rt></ruby>る is that verb. The whole phrase is <ruby>透<rt>す</rt></ruby>き<ruby>通<rt>とお</rt></ruby>った<ruby>世<rt>せ</rt>界<rt>かい</rt></ruby>で<ruby>日<rt>ひ</rt></ruby>が<ruby>昇<rt>のぼ</rt></ruby>る<ruby>場<rt>ば</rt>所<rt>しょ</rt></ruby> “the place where the sun rises, in a world grown clear”. It answers line 027’s denial: それは「<ruby>奇<rt>き</rt>跡<rt>せき</rt></ruby>」じゃない、（それは）<ruby>透<rt>す</rt></ruby>き<ruby>通<rt>とお</rt></ruby>った<ruby>世<rt>せ</rt>界<rt>かい</rt></ruby>で<ruby>日<rt>ひ</rt></ruby>が<ruby>昇<rt>のぼ</rt></ruby>る<ruby>場<rt>ば</rt>所<rt>しょ</rt></ruby>（だ） “it isn’t ‘a miracle’: it is the place where the sun rises in a clear world”. At lines 012 and 032, the noun ふたり stands between the two lines, so this line is a separate phrase there. Here nothing intervenes. The line ends on a noun (<ruby>体<rt>たい</rt>言<rt>げん</rt>止<rt>ど</rt></ruby>め), and line 029 starts the chorus again. Nothing needs correcting.

## Notes

### One phrase across two lines

In the first chorus, <ruby>透<rt>す</rt></ruby>き<ruby>通<rt>とお</rt></ruby>った<ruby>世<rt>せ</rt>界<rt>かい</rt></ruby>でふたり / <ruby>日<rt>ひ</rt></ruby>が<ruby>昇<rt>のぼ</rt></ruby>る<ruby>場<rt>ば</rt>所<rt>しょ</rt></ruby> were two images side by side, the two people and a place (lines 011–012). Without ふたり, line 027 and this line become one connected phrase: the clear world is where the sun rises, and that place is the answer to 「<ruby>奇<rt>き</rt>跡<rt>せき</rt></ruby>」じゃない.

### Two places in one phrase

<ruby>世<rt>せ</rt>界<rt>かい</rt></ruby>で sets the wide scene, and <ruby>場<rt>ば</rt>所<rt>しょ</rt></ruby> is the spot within it. Japanese uses this freely in relative clauses: <ruby>世<rt>せ</rt>界<rt>かい</rt></ruby>で<ruby>一<rt>いち</rt>番<rt>ばん</rt>星<rt>ほし</rt></ruby>がきれいに<ruby>見<rt>み</rt></ruby>える<ruby>場<rt>ば</rt>所<rt>しょ</rt></ruby> “the place in the world where the stars look the most beautiful”. The head noun <ruby>場<rt>ば</rt>所<rt>しょ</rt></ruby> needs no “where”: being the head of the clause is enough to make it the place where the sun rises.

### Sunrise, again

As at line 012, the chorus ends on a beginning, after denying that this is 「<ruby>最<rt>さい</rt>後<rt>ご</rt></ruby>」 “the end” (line 026). The details of <ruby>日<rt>ひ</rt></ruby> “sun”, <ruby>昇<rt>のぼ</rt></ruby>る and the が/の alternation are at line 012.

### Straight into the last chorus

Line 029 restarts the chorus at once: それは「<ruby>魔<rt>ま</rt>法<rt>ほう</rt></ruby>」じゃない. The text marks no stanza breaks anywhere (there are no blank lines in the passage), but the repeats make the shape clear: this ends the second chorus, and lines 029–032 are the third and last.
