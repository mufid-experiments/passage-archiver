# Line 021 · それは未来につなぐ　過去につなぐ

## Context

- Line 020 (previous): まほろのうたごえだけ　響く
- **Line 021 (explained here): それは未来につなぐ　過去につなぐ**
- Line 022 (next): お伽噺みたいな調べ

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — topic それは + two parallel predicates (未来につなぐ, 過去につなぐ) with the object left unsaid; the verbs can end the sentence or modify 調べ on line 022.

The line is grammatical. それは is the topic, and the nearest thing it can point to is the singing voice that rang out on line 020. Two short phrases follow, set side by side with only a space between them: 未来につなぐ "connects to the future" and 過去につなぐ "connects to the past". つなぐ is transitive ("connect X to Y"), but no を-object is given. What gets connected, whether us, our feelings or the present moment, is left to the listener. Like other plain verb forms, つなぐ looks the same whether it ends a sentence or modifies a noun, so there are two readings. (a) Each つなぐ ends a short sentence: "It links (us) to the future; it links (us) to the past." Line 022 then adds a noun phrase describing それ, "a melody like a fairy tale". (b) Both つなぐ modify 調べ on line 022, and the two lines form one "A is B" sentence with the copula left out: それは、未来につなぐ、過去につなぐ、お伽噺みたいな調べ（だ） "it is a melody like a fairy tale, one that links to the future and links to the past". Nothing needs correcting.

## Reading

- Kana: それ は みらい に つなぐ / かこ に つなぐ
- Romaji: sore wa mirai ni tsunagu / kako ni tsunagu

| 5 | つなぐ | つなぐ (tsunagu) | godan verb つなぐ (繋ぐ), terminal form (終止形) or attributive form (連体形), which look the same | to connect, to link, to pass on; the object is unsaid |

## How it fits together

`[それ は] [未来 に つなぐ]　／　[過去 に つなぐ]`
`(a) …つなぐ。 → お伽噺みたいな調べ (line 022) as an added description`
`(b) それ は [[未来につなぐ] [過去につなぐ] [お伽噺みたいな] 調べ]（だ）`

In (a), the verbs are predicates of それ, and line 022 stands beside them. In (b), the verbs are modifiers, and the predicate of それは is the noun 調べ on line 022. Either way, それ is the voice, and the voice is what links the times.

## Translation

- Literal: "it connects to the future, connects to the past"
- Natural: "It links us to the future, and to the past…" (with line 022, reading (b): "It's a melody like a fairy tale, linking us to the future and to the past.")

## Notes

- **Two readings, one picture.** Read as (a), the line makes two statements and line 022 adds a description afterwards. Read as (b), lines 021–022 are a single sentence, それは〜調べ(だ) "it is a ~ melody", with three modifiers stacked before the noun. Japanese verbs give no sign of which is meant, and either way the voice links times.
- **What それ is.** The voice of line 020 is the last thing named, and line 022 names it again as 調べ "melody". That makes this それ the clearest one in the song. The chorus's それは (lines 009–011) also came right after the voice (line 008), which supports hearing it the same way there.
- **つなぐ with no object.** つなぐ is "connect X to Y" (XをYにつなぐ): 手をつなぐ "hold hands", 人と人をつなぐ "bring people together", 伝統を未来につなぐ "hand a tradition on to the future". 未来につなぐ is a common phrase for passing something on. 過去につなぐ is rarer, and it makes the link run the other way. Without an object, the listener supplies one: us, our feelings, the present. The intransitive partner is つながる "be connected, lead to" (未来につながる "lead to the future"). With つなぐ, the voice does the linking.
- **The future first.** The song names the future before the past, so the voice looks ahead first and then back. Line 010 said it is not 「最後」 "the end", and that it gets past time (時間を乗り越えて). Here it reaches across time in both directions.
- **Parallel phrases with no conjunction.** 未来につなぐ　過去につなぐ sets two phrases of the same shape side by side, with only the noun changed and no と or そして between them. This kind of parallelism (対句) is common in lyrics. Line 023 goes further and sets three phrases in a row: 羽ばたける　導かれる　まだ見ぬ先へ.
- **Spelling.** 繋 is not on the Jōyō list, so the standard spelling is つなぐ, in kana, as here. 繋ぐ is common in everyday writing all the same.
