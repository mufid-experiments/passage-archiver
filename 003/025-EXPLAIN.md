# Line 025 · それは「魔法」じゃない　想いを分け合って

## Context

- Line 024 (previous): 幻なんかじゃない　僕たちは知ってるはず
- **Line 025 (explained here): それは「魔法」じゃない　想いを分け合って**
- Line 026 (next): それは「最後」じゃない　時間を乗り越えて

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — repeats line 009: a complete negative sentence (それは「魔法」じゃない) and an open te-form clause (想いを分け合って) that says what "it" is instead.

The line is grammatical and repeats line 009 word for word. It opens the second chorus (lines 025–028). それは「魔法」じゃない "it isn't 'magic'" is a complete sentence: the topic それは and the noun predicate 魔法, negated with the spoken じゃない. The brackets set 魔法 off as a label that the song turns down. After the full-width space, 想いを分け合って "sharing our feelings with each other" is a te-form clause with nothing after it. Placed after the denial, it gives the real explanation, roughly 魔法ではなく、想いを分け合って（生まれたもの）だ "it's not magic: it came from sharing our feelings". This time the unstated subject has a name: line 024 has just said 僕たち "we". それ picks up the "it" of line 024 (幻なんかじゃない), the melody of lines 021–022. Nothing needs correcting.

## Reading

- Kana: それ は まほう じゃ ない / おもい を わけあって
- Romaji: sore wa mahō ja nai / omoi o wakeatte

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | それ | それ (sore) | demonstrative pronoun (指示代名詞) | "that, it": here the same "it" as line 024, the melody of lines 021–022 |
| 2 | は | は (wa) | binding particle (係助詞), topic | "as for it": the topic of the denial |
| 3 | 「 | — | opening quotation bracket (かぎかっこ) | sets 魔法 off as a word: a label someone might use |
| 4 | 魔法 | まほう (mahō) | noun (Sino-Japanese, 漢語) | magic, sorcery |
| 5 | 」 | — | closing quotation bracket | closes the quoted word |
| 6 | じゃ | じゃ (ja) | contraction of では: copula だ in its continuative form で + binding particle は | spoken では in a negative noun predicate (魔法ではない → 魔法じゃない) |
| 7 | ない | ない (nai) | auxiliary adjective (補助形容詞) ない, terminal form (終止形) | "is not": closes the first sentence |
| 8 | 想い | おもい (omoi) | noun, from the verb 想う (思う) | feelings, what is in one's heart; love, longing |
| 9 | を | を (o) | case particle (格助詞), direct object | marks 想い as what is shared |
| 10 | 分け | わけ (wake) | ichidan verb 分ける, continuative form (連用形) | to divide, to share out; first element of 分け合う |
| 11 | 合っ | あっ (at-) | godan verb 合う as the second element of a compound verb (複合動詞), continuative form with sound change to small っ (促音便: 合い → 合っ) before て | "with each other": 分け合う = "share (between us)" |
| 12 | て | て (te) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞), te-form | "…ing, by ~ing": the clause stays open and leads on to line 026 |

## How it fits together

`[それ は [「魔法」 じゃ ない]]　／　[(僕たち が) 想い を [分け 合っ て]] → (line 026: それは「最後」じゃない　時間を乗り越えて)`

As on line 009, the first half denies a label and the second half says what is true instead. The subject of 分け合って is the 僕たち of line 024. The te-form leaves the chorus open for the next denial.

## Translation

- Literal: "it is not 'magic'; sharing (our) feelings with each other, …"
- Natural: "It isn't 'magic': it's us sharing what we feel…"

## Notes

- **The chorus returns.** Lines 025–028 repeat lines 009–012, with one change: line 027 ends 透き通った世界で, without ふたり. Lines 029–032 then repeat the first version exactly. The full analysis of this line's grammar is at line 009.
- **After 幻なんかじゃない.** The first time, the chorus followed the singing voice of line 008, and its それ pointed back to that voice. Now it follows line 024, 幻なんかじゃない　僕たちは知ってるはず "it's no illusion, and we know it". The denial 「魔法」じゃない now continues that one: not an illusion, and not magic either. Both words describe something unreal or given from outside, and the song says what the two have is neither.
- **The "we" is named.** At line 009, the subject of 分け合って had to be worked out from 合う "with each other" and from ふたり on line 011. Since line 024, the song has said it outright: 僕たち. The sharing is theirs.
- **What それ points to now.** The nearest "it" is the unstated subject of line 024, which in turn goes back to the melody of lines 021–022 (それは未来につなぐ…お伽噺みたいな調べ). The voice, the melody and whatever the two share all run together here, as they did in the first chorus.
- **分け合う and 想い.** 分け合う is 分ける "divide" + 合う "with each other": 喜びを分け合う "share joy". 想い is an expressive spelling of 思い, used for heartfelt feelings and very common in lyrics. Both are discussed at line 009.
- **Link to line 026.** Line 026 (それは「最後」じゃない　時間を乗り越えて) repeats line 010: "it isn't 'the end': getting past time…".
