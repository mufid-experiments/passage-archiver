# Line 005 · もしもそれが真実じゃなくて

## Context

- Line 004 (previous): 求めてしまうのは　なぜ
- **Line 005 (explained here): もしもそれが真実じゃなくて**
- Line 006 (next): 本当を知ってしまったら

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: first half of a conditional; もしも opens it, and the te-form なくて links on to line 006, where たら closes the "if".

The line is grammatical but is not a sentence. もしも ("if, supposing") announces a hypothesis. それが真実じゃない is a clause, "that is not the truth", and its te-form なくて links it to the next clause instead of ending it. The conditional ending that もしも is waiting for comes on line 006 (知ってしまったら), and it covers both clauses: "if that turned out not to be the truth, and (we) came to know what is real…". じゃ is the spoken contraction of では, so the register is casual. それ points back to something already in mind, most naturally the invisible, intangible things that lines 001–004 were about, or the belief in them. Line 004 ended its sentence with なぜ, and this line starts a new one. Restored prose with line 006: もしもそれが真実ではなくて、本当（のこと）を知ってしまったら… Nothing needs correcting.

## Reading

- Kana: もしも それ が しんじつ じゃ なくて
- Romaji: moshimo sore ga shinjitsu ja nakute

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | もしも | もしも (moshimo) | adverb (副詞), the emphatic form of もし | "if, supposing, by any chance": announces a conditional; its ending is たら on line 006 |
| 2 | それ | それ (sore) | demonstrative pronoun (指示代名詞) | "that, it": points back to something already in mind (see Notes) |
| 3 | が | が (ga) | case particle (格助詞), subject | marks それ as the subject of 真実じゃない |
| 4 | 真実 | しんじつ (shinjitsu) | noun (Sino-Japanese, 漢語) | truth; what is true, as opposed to what is false |
| 5 | じゃ | じゃ (ja) | contraction of では: copula だ in its continuative form で + binding particle は | the spoken form of では in a negative noun predicate: 真実ではない → 真実じゃない |
| 6 | なく | なく (naku) | auxiliary adjective (補助形容詞) ない, continuative form (連用形) | "not": 真実じゃない = "is not the truth" |
| 7 | て | て (te) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞), te-form | "…and": links this clause to line 006, so that the conditional covers both |

## How it fits together

`もしも [それ が [真実 じゃ なく て]] → (line 006: 本当を知ってしまったら)`

もしも sits at the front of the whole hypothesis. それが真実じゃない is its first clause, put into the te-form so that it doesn't end. Line 006 adds the second clause and the conditional ending たら, which closes the "if" for both.

## Translation

- Literal: "if that is not the truth, and…"
- Natural: "What if that weren't the truth…" (with line 006: "If it turned out that wasn't the truth, and we came to know what's real…")

## Notes

- **もしも and the ending it looks for.** もし and もしも look ahead to a conditional ending: 〜たら, 〜なら, 〜ば, or 〜ても for "even if". This kind of adverb is called 呼応の副詞, an adverb that calls for a matching form later in the sentence. Here the match is 知ってしまったら on line 006. もしも is the stronger, slightly more emotional form of もし: "if, by any chance; just supposing". Because it comes first, the listener knows a hypothesis has begun before any verb appears.
- **なくて, linking two conditions.** The te-form doesn't close the clause. It chains it to line 006, and both fall under たら, as in もし雨が降って、試合が中止になったら "if it rains, and the match is called off". The first clause gives the background (what we held to be true isn't), and the second the event (we find out what is real).
- **What それ points to.** それ refers to something both sides already have in mind, but the song does not name it. The nearest candidates are the invisible things people believe in (見えないもの, line 001) and the intangible things they reach for (触れられないもの, line 003), or the belief itself. それ also opens each line of the chorus (それは「魔法」じゃない, line 009), where it most naturally points to something nearer, the singing voice of line 008.
- **じゃ and では.** The negative of a noun predicate is 〜ではない in writing and careful speech, and 〜じゃない in ordinary speech: 真実ではない / 真実じゃない. じゃ is では run together. The te-form of each is 〜ではなくて / 〜じゃなくて. The chorus uses the same じゃない three times (「魔法」じゃない, 「最後」じゃない, 「奇跡」じゃない, lines 009–011), and line 024 has 幻なんかじゃない.
- **真実 and 本当.** Lines 005 and 006 use two words for "truth". 真実 (しんじつ) is the weightier Sino-Japanese word, truth as opposed to falsehood (真実を語る "tell the truth"). 本当 (ほんとう) is the everyday word, "what is really so" (本当の話 "a true story"). The pair can be heard as more than variety: if what we held as 真実 is not true, then what we find out is 本当, how things actually are. 本当 comes back on line 018 (本当が待っているんだろう).
- **Link to line 004.** Line 004 asked why people reach for what they can't touch. This line does not answer. It opens a harder question: what if the thing we believe in is not true at all? The hypothesis runs on through line 006, and what follows it is discussed at lines 006–008.
