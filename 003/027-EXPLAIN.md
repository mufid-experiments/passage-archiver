# Line 027 · それは「奇跡」じゃない　透き通った世界で

## Context

- Line 026 (previous): それは「最後」じゃない　時間を乗り越えて
- **Line 027 (explained here): それは「奇跡」じゃない　透き通った世界で**
- Line 028 (next): 日が昇る場所

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — a complete negative sentence (それは「奇跡」じゃない), then the place phrase 透き通った世界で, which here has no ふたり and attaches to the verb 昇る on line 028.

The line is grammatical. Its first half, それは「奇跡」じゃない "it isn't 'a miracle'", is a complete sentence, as on lines 011 and 031. Its second half differs from those lines: it stops at 透き通った世界で "in a clear, transparent world", without the noun ふたり. A phrase ending in で needs a verb, and line 028 supplies one: 日が昇る "the sun rises". The two halves join into a single relative clause on 場所: 透き通った世界で日が昇る場所 "the place where the sun rises in a world grown clear". On lines 011 and 031, ふたり stands between 世界で and the next line, so the phrase stops on the noun and line 028's phrase stands beside it. Here nothing stands between them, and the grammar runs straight through. The result is the most complete answer the chorus gives: それは「奇跡」じゃない、透き通った世界で日が昇る場所（だ） "it isn't 'a miracle': it is the place where the sun rises, in a world grown clear". Whether the singer drops ふたり here or the transcription does, the line is correct without it. Nothing needs correcting.

## Reading

- Kana: それ は きせき じゃ ない / すきとおった せかい で
- Romaji: sore wa kiseki ja nai / sukitōtta sekai de

## How it fits together

`[それ は [「奇跡」 じゃ ない]]　／　[[[[透き通っ た] 世界] で] (line 028: 日 が 昇る)] 場所`

The first half denies the label. In the second half, 透き通った modifies 世界, and で makes it the setting for the verb on line 028. 透き通った世界で日が昇る is then one relative clause, and its head is 場所. That noun phrase is what "it" is, with だ left unsaid.

## Translation

- Literal: "it is not 'a miracle'; in a transparent world (the sun rises)…"
- Natural: "It isn't 'a miracle': in a world grown clear…" (with line 028: "It isn't 'a miracle': it's the place where the sun rises, in a world grown clear.")

## Notes

- **The one change in the second chorus.** Lines 011 and 031 read 透き通った世界でふたり. This line stops at 世界で. The rest of the second chorus repeats the first exactly. The source text simply has it this way, and the line works without ふたり.
- **How 世界で attaches.** With ふたり, the phrase 透き通った世界で stops on a noun and its verb is never said (lines 011, 031). Without it, the verb is right there on line 028: 世界で日が昇る "the sun rises in the world". Placing a wider setting with で inside a clause whose head is a place is ordinary: 世界で一番星がきれいに見える場所 "the place in all the world where the stars look most beautiful".
- **What the chorus says this time.** In the first and last choruses, the answer to 「奇跡」じゃない is the two people, ふたり, with the place following as a second image. Here the answer is the place alone, in one connected phrase. The two people have already been named just before, as 僕たち on line 024. The final chorus brings ふたり back, so the song ends with both the pair and the place.
- **奇跡 and 透き通った.** 奇跡 "miracle" is, like 魔法 "magic", something that happens to people from outside, and the brackets mark it as a label the song turns down. 透き通った is "clear, transparent", with た for a state rather than a past. Both are discussed at line 011.
- **Link to line 028.** Line 028 (日が昇る場所) completes this line's phrase and ends the second chorus. Line 029 starts the chorus again.
