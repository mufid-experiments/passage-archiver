# Line 011 · それは「奇跡」じゃない　透き通った世界でふたり

## Context

- Line 010 (previous): それは「最後」じゃない　時間を乗り越えて
- **Line 011 (explained here): それは「奇跡」じゃない　透き通った世界でふたり**
- Line 012 (next): 日が昇る場所

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — poetic: a complete negative sentence (それは「奇跡」じゃない), then a phrase ending on the bare noun ふたり with no predicate.

The line is grammatical, but its second half is a fragment. それは「奇跡」じゃない "it isn't 'a miracle'" is a complete sentence with the same frame as lines 009–010. After the full-width space, 透き通った世界で "in a clear, transparent world" is a place phrase, and で needs a verb to belong to. None comes. The line ends on the noun ふたり "two people, the two of us". Prose would need a particle and a predicate, for example 透き通った世界でふたり（でいる） "(we are) together, the two of us, in a clear world". Leaving them out puts all the weight on the two people. As on lines 009–010, the second half answers the denial: not a miracle, but the two of us. Line 012 (日が昇る場所) follows with another noun phrase and closes the chorus. The line returns unchanged at line 031. At line 027 it returns without ふたり. Nothing needs correcting.

## Reading

- Kana: それ は きせき じゃ ない / すきとおった せかい で ふたり
- Romaji: sore wa kiseki ja nai / sukitōtta sekai de futari

| 2 | は | は (wa) | binding particle (係助詞), topic | "as for it" |
| 3 | 「 | — | opening quotation bracket (かぎかっこ) | sets 奇跡 off as a word, a label to be turned down |

## How it fits together

`[それ は [「奇跡」 じゃ ない]]　／　[[[透き通っ た] 世界] で] ふたり（でいる…） → 日が昇る場所 (line 012)`

The first half denies the label. In the second half, 透き通った modifies 世界, and で makes 透き通った世界 the place. The predicate that で is waiting for is not given, and the phrase stops on ふたり. Line 012 continues with a second noun phrase, not a verb.

## Translation

- Literal: "it is not 'a miracle'; in a transparent world, two (of us)"
- Natural: "It isn't 'a miracle': it's the two of us, in a world grown clear…"

## Notes

- **Third of three, and different.** The first two answers in the chorus were te-form verbs, things done: 分け合って "sharing" (009) and 乗り越えて "getting past" (010). This one ends on a noun. After two actions, the chorus arrives at the people who did them: ふたり, the two of us.
- **What is missing after ふたり.** 世界で "in the world" points to a predicate, such as ふたりでいる "be there, the two of us", ふたりで生きていく "live on as two", or ふたりが見つめる "the two of us gaze". The song gives none. A bare noun at the end of a phrase states a fact without making it an event: what is there, in the clear world, is two people.
- **The variant at line 027.** In the second chorus, the line ends 透き通った世界で, without ふたり. There, 世界で attaches straight to the verb on line 028: 透き通った世界で日が昇る場所 "the place where the sun rises in a clear world". The final chorus (lines 031–032) brings ふたり back, so the song ends with the two people in the picture.
- **奇跡.** 奇跡 is "miracle": 奇跡が起きる "a miracle happens", 奇跡的に助かった "survived miraculously". 奇 is "strange, wondrous", and 跡 "trace, sign". Like 「魔法」 on line 009, a miracle is something that happens to people from outside. The brackets mark the word as a label, and the line turns it down: what the two have is their own doing.
- **た for a state.** Before a noun, た often marks the state something is in rather than a past event: 透き通った水 "clear water" (not "water that was clear"), 曲がった道 "a winding road", 澄んだ空 "a clear sky". 透き通った世界 is "a clear world", possibly one that has become clear.
- **透き通る.** Light passes through it: 透く "be see-through" + 通る "pass through". It is used of water and glass (透き通った湖 "a crystal-clear lake"), and of voices (透き通った声 "a clear, pure voice"). A 透き通った世界 has nothing clouded and nothing hidden. After lines 005–006, which imagined finding out what is real, the clear world can be heard as one where nothing is hidden anymore.
- **ふたり in kana.** The kanji spelling 二人 has the special reading ふたり, which is listed in the appendix of the Jōyō table. In lyrics, the kana ふたり is very common for "the two of us" as a pair. The song's people narrow as it goes: 人 "people" (line 002), ふたり (this line), 僕たち "we" (line 024).
- **Link to line 012.** Line 012 (日が昇る場所 "the place where the sun rises") is a second noun phrase. It ends the chorus on an image of beginning, the reverse of 「最後」 on line 010.
