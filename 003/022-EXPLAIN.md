# Line 022 · お伽噺みたいな調べ

## Context

- Line 021 (previous): それは未来につなぐ　過去につなぐ
- **Line 022 (explained here): お伽噺みたいな調べ**
- Line 023 (next): 羽ばたける　導かれる　まだ見ぬ先へ

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — poetic: noun-ending phrase (体言止め) that names the それ of line 021; either the predicate of それは with だ left out, or an added description.

The line is a grammatical noun phrase. お伽噺みたいな "like a fairy tale" modifies 調べ "melody, strains of music". みたいな is the casual form of のような. The phrase has no predicate of its own, and it completes line 021 in one of two ways. (a) If line 021's two つなぐ ended their sentences, this line is an extra description of それ: "…a melody like a fairy tale." (b) If they modify 調べ, lines 021–022 make one "A is B" sentence with the copula left out: それは、未来につなぐ、過去につなぐ、お伽噺みたいな調べ（だ） "it is a melody like a fairy tale, linking to the future and to the past". Either way, 調べ gives the voice of line 020 a new name: now it is a melody. The line mixes registers: お伽噺 is old-fashioned, 調べ literary, and みたいな everyday speech. The kanji 伽 and 噺 are not on the Jōyō list, so the spelling is a deliberate, storybook-like choice. Line 023 starts a new phrase. Nothing needs correcting.

## Reading

- Kana: おとぎばなし みたいな しらべ
- Romaji: otogibanashi mitai na shirabe

## How it fits together

`(line 021: それは 未来につなぐ　過去につなぐ →) [[お伽噺 みたいな] 調べ]（だ）`

お伽噺みたいな modifies 調べ. In reading (b), 未来につなぐ and 過去につなぐ from line 021 modify 調べ too, and the whole noun phrase is the predicate of それは, with だ left out. In reading (a), the phrase stands alone as a description of それ.

## Translation

- Literal: "a melody like a fairy tale"
- Natural: "a melody like something out of a fairy tale" (with line 021: "It's a melody like a fairy tale, linking us to the future and to the past.")

## Notes

- **調べ, a literary word for "melody".** 調べ is used in writing and poetry for the sound of music: 美しい調べ "a beautiful melody", 琴の調べ "the strains of the koto". Everyday words would be メロディー or 曲, and the technical term is 旋律 (せんりつ). The same noun also means "investigation" (取り調べ "questioning by the police"), from 調べる "look into". The musical sense goes back to an older use of 調べる, "tune an instrument, play". The song's words for the sound have changed from うたごえ "singing voice" (lines 008, 020) to 調べ "melody".
- **お伽噺.** お is a polite or beautifying prefix. 伽 (とぎ) originally meant keeping someone company, for example through a long night, with talk. In the 16th century, warlords such as Toyotomi Hideyoshi kept 御伽衆 (おとぎしゅう), companions who entertained them with talk and stories. 噺 is a kanji made in Japan (国字), used for spoken tales and especially for rakugo stories. An お伽噺 is the kind of tale told to children: 桃太郎, 浦島太郎. Neither 伽 nor 噺 is on the Jōyō list, and the usual modern spelling is おとぎ話.
- **みたいな and のような.** Both mean "like". みたいな (みたいだ) is casual. のような (ようだ) is neutral and the usual choice in writing. The song uses both kinds: 星のように and 風のように (lines 013, 015), and here みたいな. With the old-fashioned お伽噺 and the literary 調べ around it, みたいな keeps the line close to speech.
- **Like a fairy tale, but real.** Fairy tales are retold from one generation to the next, which suits a melody that links the future and the past (line 021). But a fairy tale is also something that isn't real. Two lines later the song insists 幻なんかじゃない "it's no illusion" (line 024). The melody sounds like something from a story, and the song says it is true all the same.
- **Link to line 023.** Line 023 (羽ばたける　導かれる　まだ見ぬ先へ) gives what the melody does to those who hear it: they can take wing, they are guided, toward what lies ahead, not yet seen.
