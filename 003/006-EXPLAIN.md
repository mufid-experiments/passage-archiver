# Line 006 · 本当を知ってしまったら

## Context

- Line 005 (previous): もしもそれが真実じゃなくて
- **Line 006 (explained here): 本当を知ってしまったら**
- Line 007 (next): 光と影に包まれていく

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — completes the "if" begun on line 005: object + 知ってしまう in the conditional たら form; the result is not stated as a plain "then" clause.

The line is grammatical. 本当を ("what is real", as object) and 知ってしまったら ("if (we) were to find out") make the second clause of the hypothesis that もしも opened on line 005. たら is the conditional ending もしも was waiting for, and it covers both clauses: "if that turned out not to be the truth, and we came to know what's real…". The subject is unstated, most naturally "we". 本当 is used here as a bare noun, "the real, the truth". Everyday speech would more often say 本当のことを知る, but bare 本当 is established (本当を言うと "to tell the truth") and suits a lyric. What happens "if" is not given as an ordinary main clause. The next verb, 包まれていく on line 007, can be heard as the result, but it can also describe the noun on line 008. The Notes cover both readings. Restored prose with line 005: もしもそれが真実ではなくて、本当（のこと）を知ってしまったら… Nothing needs correcting.

## Reading

- Kana: ほんとう を しって しまったら
- Romaji: hontō o shitte shimattara

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 本当 | ほんとう (hontō) | noun (also a na-adjective, 本当だ) | the truth, what is real; here a bare noun, the object of 知る |
| 2 | を | を (o) | case particle (格助詞), direct object | marks 本当 as what is found out |
| 3 | 知っ | しっ (shit-) | godan verb 知る, continuative form (連用形) with sound change to small っ (促音便: 知り → 知っ) before て | to come to know, to find out, to learn (the moment of learning, not the state of knowing) |
| 4 | て | て (te) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞), te-form | joins 知っ to the subsidiary verb しまう |
| 5 | しまっ | しまっ (shimat-) | subsidiary verb (補助動詞) しまう, continuative form with sound change to small っ (促音便: しまい → しまっ) before た | "end up ~ing; ~ for good": once known, it can't be unknown |
| 6 | たら | たら (tara) | auxiliary verb (助動詞) た, conditional form (仮定形) たら | "if, when": closes the もしも of line 005; covers both clauses of the hypothesis |

## How it fits together

`(line 005: もしも [それが真実じゃなく て] →) [本当 を [知っ て しまっ たら]] → (line 007: 光と影に包まれていく …)`

もしも (line 005) opens the hypothesis and たら closes it. In between are two clauses, the first ending in the te-form (真実じゃなくて) and the second in たら. The result clause that たら leads into is the open question for lines 007–008.

## Translation

- Literal: "(and) if (we) end up knowing the real"
- Natural: "and we came to know what's real…" (with line 005: "If it turned out that wasn't the truth, and we came to know what's real…")

## Notes

- **Completes the "if" of line 005.** The whole hypothesis is もしもそれが真実じゃなくて、本当を知ってしまったら "if that wasn't the truth, and we came to know what's real". もしも, at the start of line 005, is answered by たら here, at the end of line 006.
- **What happens "if"?** No plain "then…" clause follows. Line 007's 光と影に包まれていく is the first verb after たら, and it can be heard as the result: "(we) would go on being wrapped in light and shadow", knowing the truth bringing both light and darkness. But the same form, 包まれていく, can also describe a noun, and line 008 supplies one: 光と影に包まれていく まほろのうたごえ "the singing voice of mahoro, wrapped in light and shadow". Heard that way, the "if" is followed by an image instead of a statement. Either way, the song answers the hypothesis with a picture.
- **〜てしまったら.** しまう adds that something is done and cannot be taken back: 知ってしまったら "once (we) have found out". Plain 知ったら would be the same "if" without that weight. The しまう echoes line 004 (求めてしまう): both times, something happens to people that they can't easily stop or undo.
- **知る, "find out", and 知っている, "know".** 知る is an event, the moment of coming to know. The state of knowing is 知っている, or casually 知ってる, which is how line 024 has it: 僕たちは知ってるはず "we must know". The negative is irregular: "I don't know" is 知らない, not 知っていない.
- **本当 as a noun.** 本当 usually modifies (本当の話 "a true story", 本当に "really") or is a predicate (本当だ "it's true"). As a bare noun object, 本当を知る is less usual than 本当のことを知る "learn the truth", but it is established in fixed phrases (本当を言えば "if I'm honest"). It makes "the real" sound like a thing in itself, which suits a song that sets it against 真実 on line 005. Line 018 uses 本当 the same way, as a subject: 本当が待っているんだろう "what's real is surely waiting".
- **たら, なら, ば.** たら is the conditional for one event that leads to a result: "if/when X happens". なら is "if it is the case that…", and ば leans toward general conditions (春になれば暖かくなる "when spring comes it gets warm"). たら fits a moment of discovery: "once we find out".
