# Line 026 · それは「最後」じゃない　時間を乗り越えて

## Context

- Line 025 (previous): それは「魔法」じゃない　想いを分け合って
- **Line 026 (explained here): それは「最後」じゃない　時間を乗り越えて**
- Line 027 (next): それは「奇跡」じゃない　透き通った世界で

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — repeats line 010: a complete negative sentence (それは「最後」じゃない) and an open te-form clause (時間を乗り越えて).

The line is grammatical and repeats line 010 word for word, as the second line of the second chorus. それは「最後」じゃない "it isn't 'the end'" is a complete sentence: topic それは, noun predicate 最後 in brackets, negated with the spoken じゃない. After the full-width space, 時間を乗り越えて "getting past time" is a te-form clause with nothing after it. It gives the answer to the denial: not the end, because it carries on past time. Its subject is unstated. It can be 僕たち "we" (line 024), or それ itself, the melody that line 021 said links to the future and the past. 時間を乗り越える is not a fixed phrase (the usual "transcend time" is 時を超える), but it is clear, and it treats time as an obstacle to be climbed over. The next line, 027, is the one place where the second chorus differs from the first. Nothing needs correcting.

## Reading

- Kana: それ は さいご じゃ ない / じかん を のりこえて
- Romaji: sore wa saigo ja nai / jikan o norikoete

## How it fits together

`[それ は [「最後」 じゃ ない]]　／　[時間 を [乗り越え て]] → (line 027)`

The first half denies a label, and the second half says what is true instead. The te-form 乗り越えて stands parallel to line 025's 分け合って, and the chorus runs on.

## Translation

- Literal: "it is not 'the last'; overcoming time, …"
- Natural: "It isn't 'the end': we're getting past time itself…"

## Notes

- **The same words, after the bridge.** The full analysis is at line 010. What has changed is what comes before. Line 021 said the melody links to the future and the past (それは未来につなぐ　過去につなぐ), so "getting past time" now has a clearer picture behind it. Something that reaches both ways in time is not bound by an end.
- **「最後」じゃない, in a song that repeats.** The chorus says "it isn't the end", and the chorus itself does not end here: it comes back a third time at lines 029–032. The repetition acts out the denial.
- **Who gets past time?** As on line 010, the te-form has no stated subject. After line 024 the natural "we" is 僕たち. それ itself, the melody that outlasts time, also fits, because the line puts it right after the denial about それ.
- **時間を乗り越える.** 乗り越える usually takes an obstacle: 困難を乗り越える "overcome hardship", 壁を乗り越える "get over a wall". With 時間 as its object, time becomes something to be climbed over with effort, such as a long wait or time apart, rather than simply transcended (時を超える). See line 010.
- **Link to line 027.** Line 027 (それは「奇跡」じゃない　透き通った世界で) is the third denial. Unlike lines 011 and 031, it stops at 世界で, without ふたり, and so leads straight into line 028.
