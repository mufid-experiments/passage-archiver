### Is it valid Japanese?

{% include explain/verdict.html n="030" %}

The line is grammatical and repeats lines 010 and 026 word for word, as the second line of the last chorus. それは「<ruby>最<rt>さい</rt>後<rt>ご</rt></ruby>」じゃない “it isn’t ‘the end’” is a complete sentence: topic それは, noun predicate <ruby>最<rt>さい</rt>後<rt>ご</rt></ruby> in brackets, negated with the spoken じゃない. After the full-width space, <ruby>時<rt>じ</rt>間<rt>かん</rt></ruby>を<ruby>乗<rt>の</rt></ruby>り<ruby>越<rt>こ</rt></ruby>えて “getting past time” is a te-form clause with nothing after it. It gives the answer to the denial: not the end, because it carries on past time. Its subject is unstated. It can be <ruby>僕<rt>ぼく</rt></ruby>たち “we” (line 024) or それ itself. <ruby>時<rt>じ</rt>間<rt>かん</rt></ruby>を<ruby>乗<rt>の</rt></ruby>り<ruby>越<rt>こ</rt></ruby>える is not a fixed phrase (the usual “transcend time” is <ruby>時<rt>とき</rt></ruby>を<ruby>超<rt>こ</rt></ruby>える), but it is clear, and it treats time as an obstacle to be climbed over. Line 031 follows with ふたり restored. Nothing needs correcting.

### How it fits together

{% include explain/tree.html n="030" %}

The first half denies a label, and the second half says what is true instead. The te-form <ruby>乗<rt>の</rt></ruby>り<ruby>越<rt>こ</rt></ruby>えて stands parallel to line 029’s <ruby>分<rt>わ</rt></ruby>け<ruby>合<rt>あ</rt></ruby>って, and the chorus runs on to its third denial.

## Notes

### “Not the end”, near the end

This is the last time the line is sung, two lines before the song finishes. A song that is ending says that “it” is not ending, and the claim reaches past the song. Whatever the two of them share is meant to outlast the last note, as line 021 said the melody does (それは<ruby>未<rt>み</rt>来<rt>らい</rt></ruby>につなぐ　<ruby>過<rt>か</rt>去<rt>こ</rt></ruby>につなぐ).

### The words, once more

The full analysis is at line 010. 「<ruby>最<rt>さい</rt>後<rt>ご</rt></ruby>」 is “the end, the last time”, with the brackets marking it as a label the song turns down. Heard by ear, さいご could also be <ruby>最<rt>さい</rt>期<rt>ご</rt></ruby>, “the end of a life”. The written <ruby>最<rt>さい</rt>後<rt>ご</rt></ruby> shows the general sense.

### <ruby>時<rt>じ</rt>間<rt>かん</rt></ruby>を<ruby>乗<rt>の</rt></ruby>り<ruby>越<rt>こ</rt></ruby>える

<ruby>乗<rt>の</rt></ruby>り<ruby>越<rt>こ</rt></ruby>える usually takes an obstacle (<ruby>困<rt>こん</rt>難<rt>なん</rt></ruby>を<ruby>乗<rt>の</rt></ruby>り<ruby>越<rt>こ</rt></ruby>える “overcome hardship”). With <ruby>時<rt>じ</rt>間<rt>かん</rt></ruby>, time becomes something to be got over with effort, not simply transcended (<ruby>時<rt>とき</rt></ruby>を<ruby>超<rt>こ</rt></ruby>える). Over the course of the song, the singer’s view of the truth has moved from dread (<ruby>本<rt>ほん</rt>当<rt>とう</rt></ruby>を<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>ってしまったら, line 006) through expectation (<ruby>本<rt>ほん</rt>当<rt>とう</rt></ruby>が<ruby>待<rt>ま</rt></ruby>っているんだろう, line 018) to conviction (<ruby>僕<rt>ぼく</rt></ruby>たちは<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>ってるはず, line 024). The chorus, coming back between those stages, can be heard as what carries them through that time.

### Who gets past time?

As before, the te-form has no stated subject. <ruby>僕<rt>ぼく</rt></ruby>たち (line 024) is the natural “we”. それ itself, the voice or melody that links future and past, fits as well.
