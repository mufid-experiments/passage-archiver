### Is it valid Japanese?

{% include explain/verdict.html n="019" %}

The line is grammatical and repeats line 007 word for word. <ruby>光<rt>ひかり</rt></ruby>と<ruby>影<rt>かげ</rt></ruby>に (“by light and shadow”) gives what does the wrapping, <ruby>包<rt>つつ</rt></ruby>まれる is the passive of <ruby>包<rt>つつ</rt></ruby>む (“wrap”), and 〜ていく adds “go on, more and more”. As on line 007, <ruby>包<rt>つつ</rt></ruby>まれていく has the same form whether it ends a sentence or modifies a noun. What differs is the context. Line 007 came straight after an open “if” (<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>ってしまったら), so it could be heard as the result. Here, line 018 has closed its sentence (<ruby>待<rt>ま</rt></ruby>っているんだろう), and line 020 brings a noun with a verb of its own: まほろのうたごえだけ　<ruby>響<rt>ひび</rt></ruby>く. The most natural reading is therefore one sentence across the two lines: <ruby>光<rt>ひかり</rt></ruby>と<ruby>影<rt>かげ</rt></ruby>に<ruby>包<rt>つつ</rt></ruby>まれていくまほろのうたごえだけ（が）<ruby>響<rt>ひび</rt></ruby>く “only the singing voice of mahoro, enfolded in light and shadow, resounds”. It is still possible to hear this line as a sentence of its own, “(everything) is wrapped, more and more, in light and shadow”, followed by a second sentence on line 020. Nothing needs correcting.

## Notes

### The same line in a new setting

Lines 017–020 follow the plan of lines 005–008: a sentence about <ruby>本<rt>ほん</rt>当<rt>とう</rt></ruby>, this line, then the voice of mahoro. Line 007 sat between an open conditional and a bare noun, so it could face either way. This time the sentence before it is closed, and the noun after it has its own verb. The repeat sounds the same, but its grammar settles: it most naturally describes the voice.

### Light and shadow after <ruby>本<rt>ほん</rt>当<rt>とう</rt></ruby>

Both times the song mentions <ruby>本<rt>ほん</rt>当<rt>とう</rt></ruby> “what’s real”, this line follows. After <ruby>本<rt>ほん</rt>当<rt>とう</rt></ruby>を<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>ってしまったら (line 006), light and shadow could be what the truth brings. After <ruby>本<rt>ほん</rt>当<rt>とう</rt></ruby>が<ruby>待<rt>ま</rt></ruby>っているんだろう (line 018), they surround the voice that rings out once the truth is in sight. Either way, the truth does not come as pure light.

### 〜に<ruby>包<rt>つつ</rt></ruby>まれる

The passive of <ruby>包<rt>つつ</rt></ruby>む “wrap” is often used for being surrounded completely: <ruby>炎<rt>ほのお</rt></ruby>に<ruby>包<rt>つつ</rt></ruby>まれる “be engulfed in flames”, <ruby>謎<rt>なぞ</rt></ruby>に<ruby>包<rt>つつ</rt></ruby>まれる “be shrouded in mystery”, <ruby>光<rt>ひかり</rt></ruby>に<ruby>包<rt>つつ</rt></ruby>まれる “be bathed in light”. The line takes the familiar <ruby>光<rt>ひかり</rt></ruby>に<ruby>包<rt>つつ</rt></ruby>まれる and pairs the light with shadow (see line 007).

### 〜ていく

The subsidiary verb いく puts the change on a line running forward: the wrapping grows as time goes on. The song writes this いく in kana. Line 016 used the literary variant ゆく (<ruby>流<rt>なが</rt></ruby>れてゆく), and the two are the same word.

### Link to line 020

Line 020 (まほろのうたごえだけ　<ruby>響<rt>ひび</rt></ruby>く) adds two things to line 008’s bare noun phrase: だけ “only” and the verb <ruby>響<rt>ひび</rt></ruby>く “resound”. Where lines 007–008 ended on an image, lines 019–020 end on a sound filling the space.
