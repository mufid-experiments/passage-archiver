# Line 010 · それは「最後」じゃない　時間を乗り越えて

## Context

- Line 009 (previous): それは「魔法」じゃない　想いを分け合って
- **Line 010 (explained here): それは「最後」じゃない　時間を乗り越えて**
- Line 011 (next): それは「奇跡」じゃない　透き通った世界でふたり

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — same pattern as line 009: a complete negative sentence (それは「最後」じゃない) and an open te-form clause (時間を乗り越えて).

The line is grammatical and has the same two-part shape as line 009. それは「最後」じゃない "it isn't 'the end'" is a complete sentence: topic それは, noun predicate 最後 in brackets, negated with the spoken じゃない. After the full-width space, 時間を乗り越えて "getting past time" is a te-form clause with nothing after it. It gives the answer to the denial: not the end, because it carries on past time. The subject of 乗り越えて is unstated. It can be "we", like the sharers of line 009, or それ itself, the thing that does not end. 時間を乗り越える is not a fixed phrase (the usual "transcend time" is 時を超える), but it is clear, and it treats time as an obstacle to be climbed over. The line returns unchanged at lines 026 and 030. Nothing needs correcting.

## Reading

- Kana: それ は さいご じゃ ない / じかん を のりこえて
- Romaji: sore wa saigo ja nai / jikan o norikoete

## How it fits together

`[それ は [「最後」 じゃ ない]]　／　[時間 を [乗り越え て]] → (line 011)`

As on line 009, the first half denies a label and the second half says what is true instead. The te-form 乗り越えて does not end anything. It stands parallel to line 009's 分け合って, and the chorus runs on.

## Translation

- Literal: "it is not 'the last'; overcoming time, …"
- Natural: "It isn't 'the end': we're getting past time itself…"

## Notes

- **Second of three.** Lines 009–011 have the same frame: それは「X」じゃない + what it is instead. 「魔法」 (009) was something that would come from outside, and 「最後」 here is something that ends. Line 011's 「奇跡」 "a miracle" is like magic again: something that happens to people, not something they do. Each time, the answer puts the cause in people instead: feelings shared, time got through, two people together.
- **「最後」.** 最後 is "the last, the end": 最後の日 "the last day", 最後まで "to the end", これが最後 "this is the last time". Turning it down says that whatever "it" is, it is not over. The chorus then ends on a beginning, 日が昇る場所 "the place where the sun rises" (line 012). By ear, さいご could also be 最期, "one's last moments, the end of a life". The written 最後 shows the general sense, "the end".
- **時間を乗り越える.** 乗り越える usually takes an obstacle: 困難を乗り越える "overcome hardship", 壁を乗り越える "get over a wall", 悲しみを乗り越える "get over grief". The usual phrase for "transcend time" is 時を超える, with plain 超える "go beyond". 乗り越える makes time something to be climbed over with effort, such as a long wait, time apart, or the passing years that might bring an end. That fits the denial of 「最後」 just before it.
- **Who gets past time?** The te-form clause has no stated subject. Read like line 009, where 分け合う needs more than one person, it is "we", the two of line 011. It can also be それ itself, which outlasts time. Line 021 points that way: それは未来につなぐ　過去につなぐ "it links to the future, links to the past".
- **時間 and 時.** Both mean "time". 時間 is the everyday word and also covers measurable time (時間がない "there's no time"). 時 (とき) is softer and more poetic, and it is the usual choice in phrases like 時を超える. The song uses the everyday word, which keeps the line plain.
- **Link to line 011.** Line 011 (それは「奇跡」じゃない　透き通った世界でふたり) completes the three. Its second half is not a te-form verb but ends on a noun, ふたり "the two of us", and line 012 then closes the chorus.
