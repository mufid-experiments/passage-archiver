# Line 017 · ひとつひとつ辿った先には

## Context

- Line 016 (previous): 流れてゆく日々よ　Ah
- **Line 017 (explained here): ひとつひとつ辿った先には**
- Line 018 (next): 本当が待っているんだろう

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — fragment: an adverbial of place (〜た先には, "at the end of having followed…") that sets the scene for line 018.

The line is grammatical but is not a sentence. ひとつひとつ辿った "followed one by one" is a relative clause on 先 "the point ahead, the end of the way", and には marks that point as the place, and the topic, of what follows. The main clause comes on line 018: 本当が待っているんだろう "what's real must be waiting". Together: ひとつひとつ辿った先には本当が待っているんだろう "at the end of what we follow, one by one, what's real must be waiting". The object of 辿る ("follow, trace") is not stated, and neither is its subject. The natural objects are a path or a trail, or the memories and signs along the way. The subject is "we", as elsewhere in the song. 〜た先に "beyond, at the end of ~ing" is a standard pattern. 辿る is usually written in kana (たどる), and the kanji is a stylistic choice. Line 016 closed the previous section with "Ah", and this line starts a new one. Nothing needs correcting.

## Reading

- Kana: ひとつひとつ たどった さき に は
- Romaji: hitotsu hitotsu tadotta saki ni wa

## How it fits together

`[[[ひとつひとつ 辿っ た] 先] に は] → 本当 が 待って いる ん だろう (line 018)`

ひとつひとつ modifies 辿った, and the clause modifies 先. に marks 先 as a place, and は makes it the topic. Line 018 says what is there.

## Translation

- Literal: "at the point beyond (what we) followed one by one"
- Natural: "At the end of the road we follow, step by step…" (with line 018: "At the end of the road we follow step by step, what's real must be waiting.")

## Notes

- **A section that mirrors lines 005–008.** Lines 017–020 have the same plan as lines 005–008. A two-line sentence about 本当 "what's real" comes first, then 光と影に包まれていく, then the singing voice of mahoro. In the first section, 本当 was something one might find out, with dread (もしも…本当を知ってしまったら, lines 005–006). Here it is waiting at the end of the road (本当が待っているんだろう, line 018). The wording is close, but the feeling has changed.
- **ひとつひとつ.** ひとつひとつ is "one by one, each and every one": ひとつひとつ確かめる "check them one by one", 思い出をひとつひとつ数える "count the memories one by one". It stresses care and patience, every step taken in turn, not all at once. ひとつずつ "one at a time" is close in meaning.
- **辿る.** 辿る is "follow (a road, a trail), trace": 道を辿る "follow a road", 足跡を辿る "follow footprints", 記憶を辿る "trace back through one's memories", 家路を辿る "make one's way home". It is often used of a way that has to be followed with care, step by step. The song gives no object. A path, a trail of signs, or memories all fit, and ひとつひとつ suggests separate stepping stones. 辿 is not on the Jōyō list, so newspapers write たどる in kana.
- **〜た先に.** 先 is "the point ahead". After a verb in た, it means the place you reach once the action is done: この道を進んだ先に駅がある "the station is further along this road", 角を曲がった先 "just around the corner". Figuratively: 努力した先に "at the end of hard work". The た marks the following as finished by the time one gets there, not as the past from now, so 辿った先 can lie in the future: "where we'll be once we've followed it all". 先 returns on line 023, まだ見ぬ先へ "toward what lies ahead, not yet seen".
- **には.** に marks the place, and は makes it the topic and adds contrast: "there, at the end", as opposed to here and now. 〜には〜がある / いる is the usual frame for saying what is to be found somewhere, and line 018's 待っている "is waiting" fits that frame.
- **Link to line 016.** The days on line 016 flow past on their own (流れてゆく). This line turns to something done on purpose: a way followed, one step at a time. Time passes, and the travellers keep walking.
