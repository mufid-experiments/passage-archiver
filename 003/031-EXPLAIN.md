# Line 031 · それは「奇跡」じゃない　透き通った世界でふたり

## Context

- Line 030 (previous): それは「最後」じゃない　時間を乗り越えて
- **Line 031 (explained here): それは「奇跡」じゃない　透き通った世界でふたり**
- Line 032 (next): 日が昇る場所

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — poetic: repeats line 011: a complete negative sentence (それは「奇跡」じゃない), then a phrase ending on the bare noun ふたり with no predicate.

The line is grammatical and repeats line 011 word for word, restoring the ふたり that line 027 left out. それは「奇跡」じゃない "it isn't 'a miracle'" is a complete sentence. After the full-width space, 透き通った世界で "in a clear, transparent world" is a place phrase, and で needs a verb to belong to. None comes. The line ends on the noun ふたり "the two of us". Prose would need a particle and a predicate, for example 透き通った世界でふたり（でいる） "(we are) together, the two of us, in a clear world". As in the rest of the chorus, the second half answers the denial: not a miracle, but the two of us. Because ふたり stands between 世界で and line 032, the last line, 日が昇る場所, is a separate noun phrase and not the end of this one. Nothing needs correcting.

## Reading

- Kana: それ は きせき じゃ ない / すきとおった せかい で ふたり
- Romaji: sore wa kiseki ja nai / sukitōtta sekai de futari

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | それ | それ (sore) | demonstrative pronoun (指示代名詞) | "that, it": the same それ as lines 029–030 |
| 2 | は | は (wa) | binding particle (係助詞), topic | "as for it" |
| 3 | 「 | — | opening quotation bracket (かぎかっこ) | sets 奇跡 off as a word, a label to be turned down |
| 4 | 奇跡 | きせき (kiseki) | noun (Sino-Japanese, 漢語) | miracle |
| 5 | 」 | — | closing quotation bracket | closes the quoted word |
| 6 | じゃ | じゃ (ja) | contraction of では: copula だ in its continuative form で + binding particle は | spoken では in a negative noun predicate |
| 7 | ない | ない (nai) | auxiliary adjective (補助形容詞) ない, terminal form (終止形) | "is not": closes the first sentence |
| 8 | 透き通っ | すきとおっ (sukitōt-) | godan compound verb (複合動詞) 透き通る, from 透く "be see-through" + 通る "pass through"; continuative form with sound change to small っ (促音便: 透き通り → 透き通っ) before た | to be transparent, to be clear |
| 9 | た | た (ta) | auxiliary verb (助動詞) た, attributive form (連体形) | marks a resulting state: 透き通った世界 = "a clear world" |
| 10 | 世界 | せかい (sekai) | noun | world |
| 11 | で | で (de) | case particle (格助詞), place | "in": where the two are; the verb it would attach to is left unsaid |

## How it fits together

`[それ は [「奇跡」 じゃ ない]]　／　[[[透き通っ た] 世界] で] ふたり（でいる…） → 日が昇る場所 (line 032)`

The first half denies the label. In the second half, 透き通った世界で sets the scene and ふたり names who is in it. The predicate that で is waiting for is not given, and line 032 adds a second noun phrase, not a verb.

## Translation

- Literal: "it is not 'a miracle'; in a transparent world, two (of us)"
- Natural: "It isn't 'a miracle': it's the two of us, in a world grown clear…"

## Notes

- **ふたり comes back.** The second chorus dropped it (line 027), and its 透き通った世界で ran straight into 日が昇る場所, so the answer there was a place. The last chorus returns to the first version, and the answer is the two people again, with the place beside them. The song's last pair of lines therefore holds both: ふたり, then 日が昇る場所.
- **What is missing after ふたり.** 世界で points to a predicate, such as ふたりでいる "be there, the two of us" or ふたりで生きていく "live on as two", but none is given. Ending on the bare noun states who is there without telling a story about them (see line 011).
- **Three denials, three answers.** In the last chorus, the full set is: not 「魔法」, but feelings shared (line 029); not 「最後」, but time got through (line 030); not 「奇跡」, but the two of us (this line). The first two answers are actions and the last is the people who did them. The song has moved from 人 "people" in general (line 002) to 僕たち "we" (line 024) and ends the chorus on ふたり.
- **奇跡 and 透き通った.** 奇跡 "miracle" is, like 魔法, something that happens to people from outside, and the brackets mark it as a label. 透き通った "clear, transparent" uses た for a state, not a past. A clear world, with nothing hidden, also answers the song's opening worry about what is real (lines 005–006). Both words are discussed at line 011.
- **Link to line 032.** Line 032 (日が昇る場所) is the last line of the song. It ends the chorus, and the song, on the place where the sun rises.
