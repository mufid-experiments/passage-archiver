### Is it valid Japanese?

{% include explain/verdict.html n="025" %}

The line is grammatical and repeats line 009 word for word. It opens the second chorus (lines 025–028). それは「<ruby>魔<rt>ま</rt>法<rt>ほう</rt></ruby>」じゃない “it isn’t ‘magic’” is a complete sentence: the topic それは and the noun predicate <ruby>魔<rt>ま</rt>法<rt>ほう</rt></ruby>, negated with the spoken じゃない. The brackets set <ruby>魔<rt>ま</rt>法<rt>ほう</rt></ruby> off as a label that the song turns down. After the full-width space, <ruby>想<rt>おも</rt></ruby>いを<ruby>分<rt>わ</rt></ruby>け<ruby>合<rt>あ</rt></ruby>って “sharing our feelings with each other” is a te-form clause with nothing after it. Placed after the denial, it gives the real explanation, roughly <ruby>魔<rt>ま</rt>法<rt>ほう</rt></ruby>ではなく、<ruby>想<rt>おも</rt></ruby>いを<ruby>分<rt>わ</rt></ruby>け<ruby>合<rt>あ</rt></ruby>って（<ruby>生<rt>う</rt></ruby>まれたもの）だ “it’s not magic: it came from sharing our feelings”. This time the unstated subject has a name: line 024 has just said <ruby>僕<rt>ぼく</rt></ruby>たち “we”. それ picks up the “it” of line 024 (<ruby>幻<rt>まぼろし</rt></ruby>なんかじゃない), the melody of lines 021–022. Nothing needs correcting.

### How it fits together

{% include explain/tree.html n="025" %}

As on line 009, the first half denies a label and the second half says what is true instead. The subject of <ruby>分<rt>わ</rt></ruby>け<ruby>合<rt>あ</rt></ruby>って is the <ruby>僕<rt>ぼく</rt></ruby>たち of line 024. The te-form leaves the chorus open for the next denial.

### The chorus returns

### After <ruby>幻<rt>まぼろし</rt></ruby>なんかじゃない

The first time, the chorus followed the singing voice of line 008, and its それ pointed back to that voice. Now it follows line 024, <ruby>幻<rt>まぼろし</rt></ruby>なんかじゃない　<ruby>僕<rt>ぼく</rt></ruby>たちは<ruby>知<rt>し</rt></ruby>ってるはず “it’s no illusion, and we know it”. The denial 「<ruby>魔<rt>ま</rt>法<rt>ほう</rt></ruby>」じゃない now continues that one: not an illusion, and not magic either. Both words describe something unreal or given from outside, and the song says what the two have is neither.
