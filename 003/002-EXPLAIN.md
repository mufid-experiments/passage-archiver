# Line 002 · だって人は弱いから

## Context

- Line 001 (previous): 見えないものを信じ合う
- **Line 002 (explained here): だって人は弱いから**
- Line 003 (next): 触れられないものばかり

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — colloquial: a reason clause (だって〜から) standing as its own sentence, after the statement it explains (line 001).

The line is grammatical and typical of speech. だって opens a justification ("because, after all"), and から closes it. There is no main clause after から. The statement being explained came first, on line 001, so the two lines work as 見えないものを信じ合う。だって人は弱いから。 "We believe in what we can't see. Because people are weak." Ending a sentence on から when the reason comes as an afterthought is ordinary in speech. 人は is a generic topic, so the line is a statement about people as such, the singer included, and not about any one person. Line 003 begins a new sentence, which ends on line 004. Restored prose with the reason first: 人は弱いから、見えないものを信じ合う。 Nothing needs correcting.

## Reading

- Kana: だって ひと は よわい から
- Romaji: datte hito wa yowai kara

## Word by word, particle by particle

| # | Japanese | Reading | Type | Meaning / function |
|---|----------|---------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | だって | だって (datte) | conjunction (接続詞), colloquial | "because, after all": opens a justification for what was just said; it looks ahead to から at the end |
| 2 | 人 | ひと (hito) | noun | person; here people in general, human beings |
| 3 | は | は (wa) | binding particle (係助詞), topic | "as for people": marks a generic topic, as in a maxim |
| 4 | 弱い | よわい (yowai) | i-adjective (形容詞) 弱い, terminal form (終止形) | weak: lacking strength, especially strength of heart here |
| 5 | から | から (kara) | conjunctive particle (接続助詞), reason | "because": the reason clause ends the sentence; what it explains is line 001, said first |

## How it fits together

`(line 001: 見えないものを信じ合う) ← だって [人 は 弱い] から`

人は弱い is a complete clause, "people are weak". から makes it a reason, and だって at the front signals in advance that a reason is coming. What it explains is not after it but before it: the sentence on line 001.

## Translation

- Literal: "because people are weak"
- Natural: "Because, after all, people are weak." (with line 001: "We believe, together, in things we cannot see, because people are weak, after all.")

## Notes

- **Link to line 001.** Line 001 (見えないものを信じ合う) is complete on its own, and this line adds the reason for it. Prose would usually put the reason first, but the order here is the order of speech: say what you do, then defend it, as in 行かない。だって寒いから。 "I'm not going. It's cold, that's why." The reason is weakness: people need something to hold on to, and they share their belief in it.
- **Link to line 003.** Line 003 (触れられないものばかり) starts a new sentence, which ends on line 004 with a question: 触れられないものばかり / 求めてしまうのは　なぜ "why do we end up seeking only what we can't touch?". Heard in order, 人は弱いから → 触れられないものばかり求めてしまう sounds like cause and effect, "people are weak, so they reach for what they can't touch", until のは　なぜ turns the clause into a question. The song seems to offer an answer before it asks the question, and then asks it anyway.
- **だって, two different words.** At the start of a sentence, だって is a conjunction: "because, after all; but", used to justify oneself or to object (だって知らなかったんだもん "But I didn't know!"). It is casual and can sound like an excuse or a protest. It is usually closed by から, もの/もん or んだ. The formal equivalent is なぜなら〜からだ. After a noun, だって is a different word, a particle meaning "even, too" (子供だって分かる "even a child understands"). Here it is the conjunction.
- **Ending on から.** A sentence ending in から leaves the main clause either unsaid or, as here, already said. In speech it is the usual way to add a reason afterwards. 〜からだ, with the copula, sounds more like an explanation given to someone ("it is because…"). The bare から is softer and more personal.
- **人は弱い as a maxim.** 人 here means human beings, and は marks a generic topic, as in sayings like 人は一人では生きられない "people can't live alone". The plain non-past fits: it states how things are, not what happened once. The song narrows its "we" as it goes: 人 "people" here, ふたり "the two of us" on line 011, and 僕たち "we" on line 024.
- **What 弱い means here.** 弱い covers physical weakness (体が弱い "in poor health") and weakness of heart or will (心が弱い, 意志が弱い). After 見えないものを信じ合う, it is the second kind: people are not strong enough to go without something to believe in.
