# Line 030 · それは「最後」じゃない　時間を乗り越えて

## Context

- Line 029 (previous): それは「魔法」じゃない　想いを分け合って
- **Line 030 (explained here): それは「最後」じゃない　時間を乗り越えて**
- Line 031 (next): それは「奇跡」じゃない　透き通った世界でふたり

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — repeats lines 010 and 026: a complete negative sentence (それは「最後」じゃない) and an open te-form clause (時間を乗り越えて).

The line is grammatical and repeats lines 010 and 026 word for word, as the second line of the last chorus. それは「最後」じゃない "it isn't 'the end'" is a complete sentence: topic それは, noun predicate 最後 in brackets, negated with the spoken じゃない. After the full-width space, 時間を乗り越えて "getting past time" is a te-form clause with nothing after it. It gives the answer to the denial: not the end, because it carries on past time. Its subject is unstated. It can be 僕たち "we" (line 024) or それ itself. 時間を乗り越える is not a fixed phrase (the usual "transcend time" is 時を超える), but it is clear, and it treats time as an obstacle to be climbed over. Line 031 follows with ふたり restored. Nothing needs correcting.

## Reading

- Kana: それ は さいご じゃ ない / じかん を のりこえて
- Romaji: sore wa saigo ja nai / jikan o norikoete

## How it fits together

`[それ は [「最後」 じゃ ない]]　／　[時間 を [乗り越え て]] → (line 031)`

The first half denies a label, and the second half says what is true instead. The te-form 乗り越えて stands parallel to line 029's 分け合って, and the chorus runs on to its third denial.

## Translation

- Literal: "it is not 'the last'; overcoming time, …"
- Natural: "It isn't 'the end': we're getting past time itself…"

## Notes

- **"Not the end", near the end.** This is the last time the line is sung, two lines before the song finishes. A song that is ending says that "it" is not ending, and the claim reaches past the song. Whatever the two of them share is meant to outlast the last note, as line 021 said the melody does (それは未来につなぐ　過去につなぐ).
- **The words, once more.** The full analysis is at line 010. 「最後」 is "the end, the last time", with the brackets marking it as a label the song turns down. Heard by ear, さいご could also be 最期, "the end of a life". The written 最後 shows the general sense.
- **時間を乗り越える.** 乗り越える usually takes an obstacle (困難を乗り越える "overcome hardship"). With 時間, time becomes something to be got over with effort, not simply transcended (時を超える). Over the course of the song, the singer's view of the truth has moved from dread (本当を知ってしまったら, line 006) through expectation (本当が待っているんだろう, line 018) to conviction (僕たちは知ってるはず, line 024). The chorus, coming back between those stages, can be heard as what carries them through that time.
- **Who gets past time?** As before, the te-form has no stated subject. 僕たち (line 024) is the natural "we". それ itself, the voice or melody that links future and past, fits as well.
- **Link to line 031.** Line 031 (それは「奇跡」じゃない　透き通った世界でふたり) is the third denial. Unlike line 027, it keeps ふたり, so the last chorus ends with the two people back in the picture before the final 日が昇る場所.
