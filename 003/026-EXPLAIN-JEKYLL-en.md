### Is it valid Japanese?

{% include explain/verdict.html n="026" %}

The line is grammatical and repeats line 010 word for word, as the second line of the second chorus. それは「<ruby>最<rt>さい</rt>後<rt>ご</rt></ruby>」じゃない “it isn’t ‘the end’” is a complete sentence: topic それは, noun predicate <ruby>最<rt>さい</rt>後<rt>ご</rt></ruby> in brackets, negated with the spoken じゃない. After the full-width space, <ruby>時<rt>じ</rt>間<rt>かん</rt></ruby>を<ruby>乗<rt>の</rt></ruby>り<ruby>越<rt>こ</rt></ruby>えて “getting past time” is a te-form clause with nothing after it. It gives the answer to the denial: not the end, because it carries on past time. Its subject is unstated. It can be <ruby>僕<rt>ぼく</rt></ruby>たち “we” (line 024), or それ itself, the melody that line 021 said links to the future and the past. <ruby>時<rt>じ</rt>間<rt>かん</rt></ruby>を<ruby>乗<rt>の</rt></ruby>り<ruby>越<rt>こ</rt></ruby>える is not a fixed phrase (the usual “transcend time” is <ruby>時<rt>とき</rt></ruby>を<ruby>超<rt>こ</rt></ruby>える), but it is clear, and it treats time as an obstacle to be climbed over. The next line, 027, is the one place where the second chorus differs from the first. Nothing needs correcting.

### How it fits together

{% include explain/tree.html n="026" %}

The first half denies a label, and the second half says what is true instead. The te-form <ruby>乗<rt>の</rt></ruby>り<ruby>越<rt>こ</rt></ruby>えて stands parallel to line 025’s <ruby>分<rt>わ</rt></ruby>け<ruby>合<rt>あ</rt></ruby>って, and the chorus runs on.

## Notes

### The same words, after the bridge

The full analysis is at line 010. What has changed is what comes before. Line 021 said the melody links to the future and the past (それは<ruby>未<rt>み</rt>来<rt>らい</rt></ruby>につなぐ　<ruby>過<rt>か</rt>去<rt>こ</rt></ruby>につなぐ), so “getting past time” now has a clearer picture behind it. Something that reaches both ways in time is not bound by an end.

### 「<ruby>最<rt>さい</rt>後<rt>ご</rt></ruby>」じゃない, in a song that repeats

The chorus says “it isn’t the end”, and the chorus itself does not end here: it comes back a third time at lines 029–032. The repetition acts out the denial.

### Who gets past time?

As on line 010, the te-form has no stated subject. After line 024 the natural “we” is <ruby>僕<rt>ぼく</rt></ruby>たち. それ itself, the melody that outlasts time, also fits, because the line puts it right after the denial about それ.

### <ruby>時<rt>じ</rt>間<rt>かん</rt></ruby>を<ruby>乗<rt>の</rt></ruby>り<ruby>越<rt>こ</rt></ruby>える

<ruby>乗<rt>の</rt></ruby>り<ruby>越<rt>こ</rt></ruby>える usually takes an obstacle: <ruby>困<rt>こん</rt>難<rt>なん</rt></ruby>を<ruby>乗<rt>の</rt></ruby>り<ruby>越<rt>こ</rt></ruby>える “overcome hardship”, <ruby>壁<rt>かべ</rt></ruby>を<ruby>乗<rt>の</rt></ruby>り<ruby>越<rt>こ</rt></ruby>える “get over a wall”. With <ruby>時<rt>じ</rt>間<rt>かん</rt></ruby> as its object, time becomes something to be climbed over with effort, such as a long wait or time apart, rather than simply transcended (<ruby>時<rt>とき</rt></ruby>を<ruby>超<rt>こ</rt></ruby>える). See line 010.
