# Line 020 · まほろのうたごえだけ　響く

## Context

- Line 019 (previous): 光と影に包まれていく
- **Line 020 (explained here): まほろのうたごえだけ　響く**
- Line 021 (next): それは未来につなぐ　過去につなぐ

## Is it valid Japanese?

**Verdict:** Valid — completes the sentence begun on line 019: subject まほろのうたごえ + だけ (standing in for が) + the terminal verb 響く.

The line is grammatical and ends a sentence. まほろのうたごえ "the singing voice of mahoro" is the subject. だけ "only" follows it in place of が (だけが is also correct), and 響く "resound, ring out" is the predicate, in the plain terminal form. Line 019's 光と影に包まれていく modifies the noun, so the full sentence is 光と影に包まれていくまほろのうたごえだけ（が）響く "only the singing voice of mahoro, enfolded in light and shadow, rings out". The full-width space before 響く is a pause that holds the verb back a beat. The line takes line 008's bare noun phrase (まほろのうたごえ) and gives it a verb: what was an image is now a sound. For まほろ, see line 008. Line 021 starts a new sentence whose それ points back to this voice. Nothing needs correcting.

## Reading

- Kana: まほろ の うたごえ だけ / ひびく
- Romaji: mahoro no utagoe dake / hibiku

## How it fits together

`(line 019: [光と影に包まれていく] →) [[[まほろ の] うたごえ] だけ]　響く`

The relative clause from line 019 and まほろの both modify うたごえ. だけ limits the subject to that voice, and 響く is its verb. The sentence ends here.

## Translation

- Literal: "only the singing voice of mahoro resounds"
- Natural: "only the singing voice of Mahoro rings out." (with line 019: "Only the singing voice of Mahoro, enfolded in light and shadow, rings out.")

## Notes

- **Line 008, completed.** The first time, the phrase stood alone: まほろのうたごえ (line 008), a noun with no verb. This time it is the subject of a sentence and has a verb. The two sections end differently: lines 005–008 with a picture, after an open "if", and lines 017–020 with a sound, after a sentence that expects the truth at the end of the road.
- **だけ, "only".** だけ is the neutral "only, just": 少しだけ "just a little", 君だけ "only you". It can replace が and を, or stand in front of them (だけが, だけを). With a subject, it says that everything else is quiet: only the voice can be heard. It differs from ばかり on line 003 (触れられないものばかり "nothing but things we can't touch"), which hinted at too much of one thing. だけ here clears the space for the voice.
- **響く.** 響く is used for a sound that fills a space and carries: 鐘の音が響く "the bell rings out", 足音が廊下に響く "footsteps echo down the corridor". It is also used of words or music that reach the heart: 心に響く歌 "a song that moves you". Both senses fit: the voice rings out, and it reaches the listener.
- **A pause before the verb.** The full-width space sets 響く apart, the same device as on line 004 (求めてしまうのは　なぜ), where the question word was held back. The verb gets a beat to itself, much as a sound hangs in the air.
- **Link to line 021.** Line 021 (それは未来につなぐ　過去につなぐ "it links to the future, links to the past") begins with それ, and the nearest thing it can point to is this voice. Line 022 then calls it お伽噺みたいな調べ "a melody like a fairy tale". The same order, voice and then それは…, is found at the first chorus (lines 008–009), which supports hearing the chorus's それ as the voice too.
