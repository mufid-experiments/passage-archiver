### Is it valid Japanese?

{% include explain/verdict.html n="031" %}

The line is grammatical and repeats line 011 word for word, restoring the ふたり that line 027 left out. それは「<ruby>奇<rt>き</rt>跡<rt>せき</rt></ruby>」じゃない “it isn’t ‘a miracle’” is a complete sentence. After the full-width space, <ruby>透<rt>す</rt></ruby>き<ruby>通<rt>とお</rt></ruby>った<ruby>世<rt>せ</rt>界<rt>かい</rt></ruby>で “in a clear, transparent world” is a place phrase, and で needs a verb to belong to. None comes. The line ends on the noun ふたり “the two of us”. Prose would need a particle and a predicate, for example <ruby>透<rt>す</rt></ruby>き<ruby>通<rt>とお</rt></ruby>った<ruby>世<rt>せ</rt>界<rt>かい</rt></ruby>でふたり（でいる） “(we are) together, the two of us, in a clear world”. As in the rest of the chorus, the second half answers the denial: not a miracle, but the two of us. Because ふたり stands between <ruby>世<rt>せ</rt>界<rt>かい</rt></ruby>で and line 032, the last line, <ruby>日<rt>ひ</rt></ruby>が<ruby>昇<rt>のぼ</rt></ruby>る<ruby>場<rt>ば</rt>所<rt>しょ</rt></ruby>, is a separate noun phrase and not the end of this one. Nothing needs correcting.

### How it fits together

{% include explain/tree.html n="031" %}

The first half denies the label. In the second half, <ruby>透<rt>す</rt></ruby>き<ruby>通<rt>とお</rt></ruby>った<ruby>世<rt>せ</rt>界<rt>かい</rt></ruby>で sets the scene and ふたり names who is in it. The predicate that で is waiting for is not given, and line 032 adds a second noun phrase, not a verb.

## Notes

### ふたり comes back

The second chorus dropped it (line 027), and its <ruby>透<rt>す</rt></ruby>き<ruby>通<rt>とお</rt></ruby>った<ruby>世<rt>せ</rt>界<rt>かい</rt></ruby>で ran straight into <ruby>日<rt>ひ</rt></ruby>が<ruby>昇<rt>のぼ</rt></ruby>る<ruby>場<rt>ば</rt>所<rt>しょ</rt></ruby>, so the answer there was a place. The last chorus returns to the first version, and the answer is the two people again, with the place beside them. The song’s last pair of lines therefore holds both: ふたり, then <ruby>日<rt>ひ</rt></ruby>が<ruby>昇<rt>のぼ</rt></ruby>る<ruby>場<rt>ば</rt>所<rt>しょ</rt></ruby>.

### What is missing after ふたり

<ruby>世<rt>せ</rt>界<rt>かい</rt></ruby>で points to a predicate, such as ふたりでいる “be there, the two of us” or ふたりで<ruby>生<rt>い</rt></ruby>きていく “live on as two”, but none is given. Ending on the bare noun states who is there without telling a story about them (see line 011).

### Three denials, three answers

In the last chorus, the full set is: not 「<ruby>魔<rt>ま</rt>法<rt>ほう</rt></ruby>」, but feelings shared (line 029); not 「<ruby>最<rt>さい</rt>後<rt>ご</rt></ruby>」, but time got through (line 030); not 「<ruby>奇<rt>き</rt>跡<rt>せき</rt></ruby>」, but the two of us (this line). The first two answers are actions and the last is the people who did them. The song has moved from <ruby>人<rt>ひと</rt></ruby> “people” in general (line 002) to <ruby>僕<rt>ぼく</rt></ruby>たち “we” (line 024) and ends the chorus on ふたり.

### <ruby>奇<rt>き</rt>跡<rt>せき</rt></ruby> and <ruby>透<rt>す</rt></ruby>き<ruby>通<rt>とお</rt></ruby>った

<ruby>奇<rt>き</rt>跡<rt>せき</rt></ruby> “miracle” is, like <ruby>魔<rt>ま</rt>法<rt>ほう</rt></ruby>, something that happens to people from outside, and the brackets mark it as a label. <ruby>透<rt>す</rt></ruby>き<ruby>通<rt>とお</rt></ruby>った “clear, transparent” uses た for a state, not a past. A clear world, with nothing hidden, also answers the song’s opening worry about what is real (lines 005–006). Both words are discussed at line 011.

### Link to line 032

Line 032 (<ruby>日<rt>ひ</rt></ruby>が<ruby>昇<rt>のぼ</rt></ruby>る<ruby>場<rt>ば</rt>所<rt>しょ</rt></ruby>) is the last line of the song. It ends the chorus, and the song, on the place where the sun rises.
